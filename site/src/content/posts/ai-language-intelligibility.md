## 摘要（Abstract）

:::bi

There is a widespread sense that AI-generated text has recently become harder to understand.
In this study, we investigate a set of psycholinguistic features that are known to correlate with human processing difficulty (e.g., object relative clauses, long syntactic dependencies, low-frequency n-grams).
Using a novel corpus of AI-generated text across diverse domains, we find that AI models use these difficult features more often than paraphrases generated by round-trip translation. Compared with human writing, all nine frontier and near-frontier models use more inanimate subjects with animate verbs and more low-frequency n-grams, and most also use longer dependencies, more stacked nouns, and more reduced object relatives in conversation; we also demonstrate substantial variation across model families.
We propose and test a hypothesis as to why: while human language is optimized for successful communication, AI-generated text is increasingly optimized for other goals like math, code, and reasoning, in which there is a pressure for *information density* rather than successful communication.
We provide a proof-of-concept for this hypothesis by fine-tuning a language model under a length penalty, which leads to a divergence from human-like linguistic behavior.
Our findings highlight the axes of misalignment between linguistic processing demands in humans and LLMs, and point toward possible remedies.

:::zh

人们普遍觉得，近来 AI 生成的文本越来越难读了。
本研究考察一组已知与人类加工难度相关的心理语言学特征，比如宾语关系从句，长距离句法依存和低频 n-gram。
我们在涵盖多个领域的自建 AI 生成文本语料库上发现，AI 模型使用这些高难度特征的频率高于经往返翻译生成的同义改写。
与人类写作相比，全部九个前沿模型与接近前沿的模型都用了更多搭配有生命动词的无生命主语，也用了更多低频 n-gram。其中大多数模型在对话中还呈现出更长的依存距离，更多的名词堆叠，以及更多的缩略宾语关系从句，不同模型家族之间则存在显著差异。
针对这一现象的成因，我们提出并检验如下假设：人类语言的演化目标在于促成顺畅交流，而 AI 生成文本正日益围绕数学，代码和推理等其他目标做优化，这些目标施加的是*信息密度*压力，而非交流顺畅度压力。
我们在长度惩罚下微调了一个语言模型，为该假设提供概念验证，结果模型的语言行为确实偏离了类人分布。
上述发现揭示了人类与 LLM 在语言加工需求上的错位维度，并指出了潜在的补救方向。
:::

## 引言（Introduction）

:::bi

*Your instinct is right; the convoluted sentence you are currently reading is quietly eating its own reader-patience budget — footnote it.*
If you saw that sentence in the wild, you might suspect that it’s the product of a modern LLM.
It has several linguistic features that are overrepresented in frontier LLM text: an object relative clause, a denominal verb (“footnote”), an inanimate subject used with the verb “eat”, and stacked nouns leading to a low-frequency n-gram sequence (“reader-patience budget”).
All of these features are associated with linguistic processing difficulty.

:::zh

*你的直觉没错，你眼前正在读的这个曲折缠绕的句子正在悄悄吃掉它自己的读者耐心预算，给它加个脚注吧。*
要是在现实里撞见这句话，你八成会怀疑它出自某个现代 LLM 之手。
它集齐了前沿 LLM 文本里高频出现的几项语言学特征：宾语关系从句，名词动用（“footnote”），无生命主语配动作动词“eat”，以及导致低频 n-gram 序列的名词堆叠（“reader-patience budget”）。
所有这些特征都会推高语言加工难度。
:::

:::annotation
开篇第一句就是作者埋的活体标本：宾语关系从句，名词动用（footnote），无生命主语配动作动词（budget 在 eating），外加一串名词堆叠（reader-patience budget）。先让你读得磕绊一下，再把六项测量指标挨个对号入座。
:::

:::bi

But why would models trained on human text develop linguistic features that hurt intelligibility? And, if they are, is it happening systematically across model families?
Here, we study a set of features known to correlate with linguistic processing difficulty and find that they are overrepresented in text generated from recent LLMs, including both closed and open-weight models.
We collect a novel corpus of LLM-generated text across diverse domains (chat, coding, essay), and we show that the prevalence of these features, in many models, exceeds that of human-generated reference corpora.
Using a round-trip translation paradigm, we further show that these features do not occur in paraphrases that are controlled tightly for semantic content—further evidence they are a result of the specific features of modern post-trained models.

:::zh

可是，在人类文本上训练出来的模型，为什么会演化出有损可懂度的语言学特征？如果确有此事，这种趋势是否在各模型家族间系统性地发生？
本文考察一组已知与语言加工难度相关的特征，发现它们在近期闭源和开放权重 LLM 生成的文本中普遍偏高。
我们跨多个领域（对话，编程，作文）采集了一套新的 LLM 生成文本语料库，证实许多模型使用这些特征的频率已经超过人类参照语料库。
借助往返翻译范式，我们进一步证明：一旦在严格固定语义内容的前提下做同义改写，这些特征就不再高频出现，从而佐证它们源于现代后训练模型的特定偏好。
:::

:::bi

Having established this trend, we then propose and test a hypothesis as to *why* this pattern may have emerged in LLMs.
The psycholinguistics literature shows that human language is largely optimized for communication (Gibson et al., 2019), in the sense of striking an efficient balance between being easy to produce and easy to understand.
Empirically, human text is well-optimized relative to random baselines on a number of dimensions, such as minimizing dependency length (Futrell et al., 2015).
It follows that models trained to mimic human-generated text should also show a similar profile of linguistic features in their output, but that these patterns may not be preserved in models where communication is not the primary objective, and where humans are largely out of the loop.

:::zh

确认这一趋势后，我们接着提出并检验一项假设，探讨这种模式*为何*会在 LLM 中浮现。
心理语言学文献表明，人类语言很大程度上是为顺畅交流而优化的 (Gibson et al., 2019)，即在易于产出与易于理解之间维持高效平衡。
实证研究也显示，相比随机基线，人类文本在缩短依存距离等多个维度上都经过了高度优化 (Futrell et al., 2015)。
顺理成章地，以模仿人类文本为训练目标的模型理应在输出中呈现相似的语言特征分布。可一旦模型的首要目标不再是交流，训练回路里也基本没有人类参与，这些特征模式就未必留得住。
:::

:::bi

Indeed, modern LLMs are not just trained on human-generated text, but undergo extensive post-training for math, coding, and other reasoning tasks.
In paradigms like those of Guo et al. (2025), models are rewarded for getting the right answer but not necessarily for clarity.
We hypothesize that optimization for these goals, alongside a limit on the context window available to models, leads to information compression in a way that competes with intelligibility.
To test that hypothesis, we fine-tune a pre-trained language model under a length penalty, and measure the linguistic profile of its outputs. The model learns to succeed at the task using fewer tokens by leveraging syntactic alternations (cases where there are two ways of expressing the same meaning), even when this makes their linguistic output less humanlike. Furthermore, their preference for information compression bleeds into linguistic alternations that were not seen in training.

:::zh

事实上，现代 LLM 不仅在人类文本上做预训练，还要针对数学，编程和其他推理任务接受大规模后训练。
在 Guo et al. (2025) 这类训练范式中，奖励只看答案对错，未必顾及表达是否清晰。
我们假设，面向这类目标的优化叠加模型可用上下文窗口的限制，会诱发与可懂度相左的信息压缩。
为检验该假设，我们在长度惩罚下微调了一个预训练语言模型，并测量其输出的语言特征分布。
模型学会利用句法交替（同一意义存在两种句法表达的情形），以更少的 token 完成任务，哪怕代价是输出不再像人话。
不仅如此，这种对信息压缩的偏好还会蔓延到训练中从未见过的句法交替上。
:::

:::annotation
第二条假设 H2 的因果链分三步：后训练只按答案对错给奖，不管人读着顺不顺，而上下文窗口又有长度约束，模型便学会往更少的 token 里硬塞信息。后面的长度惩罚实验就是把这个机制剥出来单测。
:::

:::bi

In sum, this work tests two specific hypotheses about the intelligibility of AI-generated text.
**H1:** The language output of frontier and near-frontier LLMs overuses linguistic features associated with human comprehension difficulty.
**H2:** A pressure for information density during post-training shifts the language output of LLMs to be less humanlike.
Using a combination of linguistic analysis and direct post-training intervention, we find support for both hypotheses. Our findings highlight the dimensions along which human language processing and frontier LLMs are misaligned, and point to possible interventions for addressing this misalignment.

:::zh

归结起来，本文围绕 AI 生成文本的可懂度检验两个具体假设。
**H1：** 前沿与接近前沿的 LLM 在语言输出中过度使用与人类理解难度相关的语言学特征。
**H2：** 后训练阶段的信息密度压力使 LLM 的语言输出偏离类人形态。
结合语言学分析与直接的后训练干预，两项假设均得到了支持。研究结果勾勒出人类语言加工与前沿 LLM 相互错位的具体维度，并为纠正这种错位提供了可行的干预思路。
:::

## 背景与相关工作（Background and Related Work）

:::bi

Recent scientific work has attempted to measure the idiosyncrasies of LLMs, such as their tendency to over-use particular words (Juzek and Ward, 2025), or their dense, noun-heavy style Reinhart et al. (2025).
These idiosyncrasies may emerge during the post-training process of modern LLMs, including rewards during reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF; Ouyang et al., 2022; Singhal et al., 2024), or reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR; Lambert et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2025).
The distinctive tells of LLM-generated writing have led to AI-generated text detectors (Bao et al., 2024), and have raised questions regarding the impact of such AI-generated writing on linguistic content, with implications for diversity and representation (Padmakumar and He, 2024; Agarwal et al., 2025; Ryan et al., 2024).
As a growing share of the text people read is shaped by LLMs, the linguistic properties of AI-generated writing matter not only for detection but also for the human readers who must process it. Yet whether these properties make AI-generated text harder for humans to understand has received little academic attention, despite widespread anecdotal reports of issues with LLM intelligibility (University of Waterloo, 2026).

:::zh

近期已有不少研究尝试量化 LLM 的用词习气，例如偏爱高频重复特定词汇 (Juzek and Ward, 2025)，或是行文密集，名词成堆 Reinhart et al. (2025)。
这些习气可能源于现代 LLM 的后训练过程，包括基于人类反馈的强化学习 (RLHF; Ouyang et al., 2022; Singhal et al., 2024) 与基于可验证奖励的强化学习 (RLVR; Lambert et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2025) 中的奖励信号。
LLM 写作的鲜明印记催生了 AI 文本检测器 (Bao et al., 2024)，也引发了学界对 AI 写作如何重塑语言内容，进而波及表达多样性与代表性的讨论 (Padmakumar and He, 2024; Agarwal et al., 2025; Ryan et al., 2024)。
随着人们日常阅读的文本越来越受 LLM 影响，AI 写作的语言学属性不仅关乎机器检测，更直接关系到必须消化这些文本的人类读者。然而，尽管坊间对 LLM 文本晦涩难读的抱怨俯拾皆是 (University of Waterloo, 2026)，严肃探讨这些语言属性是否真会增加人类理解负担的学术研究却寥寥无几。
:::

:::bi

The field of psycholinguistics has extensively studied the linguistic features that drive comprehension difficulty. Words that are hard to predict are costly to process (Hale, 2001; Levy, 2008), and so are long-distance dependencies, because of limits on memory (Gibson, 1998; Gibson, 2000). Memory-based accounts explain the difficulty of center-embedded sentences (Gibson and Thomas, 1999; Lewis, 1996) and of legalese (Martínez et al., 2022), and temporary ambiguity can derail comprehension in garden-path sentences (Christianson et al., 2001).

:::zh

心理语言学领域早已深入探究过驱动理解难度的语言学特征。难以预测的词加工代价高昂 (Hale, 2001; Levy, 2008)，受限于工作记忆容量，长距离依存关系同样费力 (Gibson, 1998; Gibson, 2000)。基于记忆负担的理论解释了中心嵌套句 (Gibson and Thomas, 1999; Lewis, 1996) 与法律文书 (Martínez et al., 2022) 为何晦涩，而临时句法歧义则会把花园路径句的理解引入歧途 (Christianson et al., 2001)。
:::

:::bi

Crucially, the human mind operates under distinct pressures and limitations compared with those of LLMs.
LLMs are also trained on significantly more data than humans experience during language development Frank (2023). Modern LLMs are able to attend fully to very large context windows (Vaswani et al., 2017; Oh and Linzen, 2026), in contrast with the lossy and distance-sensitive memory of humans (Futrell et al., 2020). Larger LLMs often fit human behavior (e.g., reading times) less well than smaller LLMs (Oh and Schuler, 2023; Oh et al., 2024). Consistent with this pattern, LLMs with artificially constrained memory fit human behavior better (Kuribayashi et al., 2022; Xu et al., 2026; Clark et al., 2025). This suggests that LLMs’ ability to attend fully to long contexts may come at the cost of human-likeness.

:::zh

关键在于，人类心智与 LLM 运作时面临的演化压力和硬件约束截然不同。
LLM 训练所吞吐的语料规模远超人类语言习得一生所接触的文本量 Frank (2023)。人类记忆有损且对距离敏感 (Futrell et al., 2020)，现代 LLM 却能毫无衰减地把注意力铺满超长上下文窗口 (Vaswani et al., 2017; Oh and Linzen, 2026)。规模更大的 LLM 在拟合阅读时间等人类行为指标时，反而常常不如小模型 (Oh and Schuler, 2023; Oh et al., 2024)。与之呼应，一旦人为限制 LLM 的记忆窗口，它们对人类行为的拟合度就会回升 (Kuribayashi et al., 2022; Xu et al., 2026; Clark et al., 2025)。这说明 LLM 完整注意长上下文的能力，或许是以牺牲像人程度为代价换来的。
:::

:::annotation
全文的理论支点就在这儿：人脑受限于工作记忆和增量加工，LLM 却能一眼扫完长上下文，所以“能力强”和“说话像人”会分道扬镳。作者并未贬低模型输出的质量，只是指出它偏离了人类语言为迁就人脑而演化出的形态。
:::

:::bi

Linguistic alternations, in which speakers and writers have multiple options for expressing a given meaning, provide a source of evidence for the preferences guiding human language generation.
One purported pressure on human language production is uniform information density (UID) — the idea that speakers spread information evenly across the signal to avoid overloading comprehenders, e.g. by inserting optional words (Levy and Jaeger, 2007; Meister et al., 2021).
UID is at odds with *maximizing* information density, by which shorter members of linguistic alternations would be categorically preferred, and has been proposed as a metric for preference optimization in LLM training to produce more human-aligned text (Li and Xu, 2026).
Meanwhile, psycholinguistic evidence also supports a pressure based on cognitive availability during incremental production (Bock, 1987; Clark et al., 2022; Futrell, 2024).
Pre-trained LLMs inherit these signatures from their human training text, but post-training for specific tasks (e.g. math, code) may favor information-dense constructions over those that ease human processing or resemble humans.

:::zh

说话者和写作者在表达同一意义时往往有多种句式可选，这种句法交替现象为揭示人类语言产出的底层偏好提供了窗口。
学界认为人类语言产出受制于均匀信息密度（UID）压力，即说话者倾向于把信息均匀摊在语言信号里，比如视语境插入可选功能词，以免理解者瞬间过载 (Levy and Jaeger, 2007; Meister et al., 2021)。
UID 与*最大化*信息密度正面相悖，后者会一刀切地偏爱句法交替中较短的形式。近期也有研究提议把 UID 用作 LLM 偏好优化的指标，好让生成文本更贴近人类习惯 (Li and Xu, 2026)。
此外，心理语言学证据还表明，增量产出过程中的认知可及性同样会左右句式选择 (Bock, 1987; Clark et al., 2022; Futrell, 2024)。
预训练 LLM 从人类语料里继承了这些分布印记，但面向数学，代码等特定任务的后训练可能会转而奖赏信息密集的结构，舍弃那些照顾人类加工负担或贴近人类习惯的表达。
:::

:::annotation
均匀信息密度（UID）要把信息摊平，避免听者过载，最大化信息密度却要把信息压实。句法交替恰好提供了天然的标尺：同一层意思有两种合法句式，长度差精确到 token，看模型选哪边就能直接测出它的偏好。
:::

## 实验一：AI 生成对话中的语言学特征（Experiment 1: Linguistic features in AI-generated conversations）

### 关注的语言学特征（Linguistic features of interest）

:::bi

The purpose of Experiment 1 is to test **H1**: do frontier and near-frontier LLMs disproportionately use linguistic features that are linked to human processing difficulty?
For each feature, we justify its association with processing difficulty, and explain why it might plausibly arise from a pressure toward information density. Example sentences for each feature are given in Table 2. All features are identified from spaCy dependency parses (Honnibal et al., 2020); for denominal verbs and inanimate subjects, parser-generated candidates are then judged by an automated classifier (Section B.1).

:::zh

实验一旨在检验 **H1**：前沿模型和接近前沿的 LLM 是否会过度使用与人类加工难度相关的语言学特征？
针对每项特征，我们都会交代它与加工难度的关联，并说明提高信息密度的压力为何会诱发该特征。表 2 给出了各项特征的示例句。所有特征均基于 spaCy 的依存句法分析结果提取（Honnibal et al., 2020），其中名词动用和无生命主语先由解析器筛出候选，再交由自动分类器判定（§B.1）。
:::

:::annotation
这一节把笼统的“难懂”拆成了可计数的硬指标：每千对词里的未见二元组，按句长标准化的平均依存距离，每千句里的宾语关系从句。心理语言学的体感只有落到这类比率上，才能跨模型横向对比。
:::

#### n-gram 统计（N-gram statistics）

:::bi

Predictable words are associated with lower processing difficulty, as measured by reading times (Levy, 2008; Smith and Levy, 2013).
From an information-theoretic perspective, a word with a very high n-gram probability is more redundant than one with a low n-gram probability.
For a system designed to maximize information density, we might expect high-probability n-grams to be reduced (i.e. common, uninformative, and predictable words being skipped), and higher overall n-gram perplexity (when measured by an independent language model).
Against a reference corpus, we measure the rate of *unattested pairs*, bigrams that never occur in it, and *perplexity* under a trigram model (Section B.2).

:::zh

以阅读时间衡量，越容易预测的词，人类加工起来越省力（Levy, 2008; Smith and Levy, 2013）。
从信息论视角看，n-gram 概率极高的词比低概率词携带了更多冗余。
假如一个系统旨在最大化信息密度，它自然会削减高概率 n-gram（跳过常见，低信息量且易预测的词），进而推高由独立语言模型测得的整体 n-gram 困惑度。
我们以参照语料库为基准，测量*未出现过的二元组*（参照语料中从未见过的 bigram）比例，以及三元模型下的*困惑度*（§B.2）。
:::

:::annotation
这两个指标分别盯住两端：未见二元组多，说明模型爱生造词组搭配，三元困惑度高，说明整句走向难以预期。一个抓局部生造，一个看全局分布，合起来才兜得住“用词生僻”的程度。
:::

#### 依存距离（Dependency length）

:::bi

Words in a sentence relate to each other via syntactic dependency relations (e.g., between a noun and any determiners or adjectives that modify it, or between a verb and its subject, object, and adverbial modifiers).
Long-distance dependencies are known to be difficult to process for humans, due to the additional strain they place on working memory (Gibson, 1998; Gibson, 2000; Grodner and Gibson, 2005), and speakers tend to avoid them (Futrell et al., 2015; Temperley and Gildea, 2018).
We standardize dependency length across sentence lengths, as described in Appendix B.

:::zh

句子里的词通过句法依存关系相连，比如名词与修饰它的限定词或形容词相连，动词与它的主语，宾语和状语修饰语相连。
长距离依存会加重工作记忆负担，人类加工起来尤为吃力（Gibson, 1998; Gibson, 2000; Grodner and Gibson, 2005），日常说话时也会下意识避开（Futrell et al., 2015; Temperley and Gildea, 2018）。
我们按附录 B 所述方法，对不同句长下的依存距离做了标准化。
:::

:::annotation
句子越长，词与词之间的依存距离天然越远。为了剔除句长干扰，作者在 §B.3 借用了流行病学里的年龄标准化：先按句长分箱算均值，再按 Wikipedia 的句长分布加权求和。后面图里看到的依存距离，都已经扣掉了句长本身的影响。
:::

#### 宾语关系从句（Object relative clauses）

:::bi

We measure the rate of object relative clauses (ORCs) in the language output of LLMs, differentiating between ORCs with an overt relativizer (most often *that* or *which*) and without one.
ORCs are known to present additional processing difficulty compared to subject relative clauses, potentially due to their non-canonical word order and longer dependencies (Staub, 2010), although animacy and frequency reduce the gap (Mak et al., 2006; Reali and Christiansen, 2007).
Under the hypothesis that LLMs tend to choose more compressed realizations of a given meaning, we predict object relative clauses will be over-represented, because they tend to replace a (longer) passive construction (*The house that the contractors built* vs. *The house that was built by the contractors*), and second, that *reduced* relative clauses (without the optional relativizer) would be over-represented, because they avoid an optional word.

:::zh

我们统计 LLM 输出中宾语关系从句（ORC）的比例，并把带显性关系词（最常见的是 *that* 或 *which*）与不带关系词的 ORC 拆开计数。
相比主语关系从句，ORC 因词序非常规且依存距离更长，加工难度明显更高（Staub, 2010），尽管主宾语的有生性反差与高频词能缓解一部分难度（Mak et al., 2006; Reali and Christiansen, 2007）。
假如 LLM 确实倾向于用更紧凑的形式表达同一含义，我们可以做出两项预测：第一，宾语关系从句整体会偏多，因为它常用来替代更冗长的被动结构（例如用 *The house that the contractors built* 替代 *The house that was built by the contractors*）。第二，省去可选关系词的*缩略*关系从句也会偏多，因为这样能直接省掉一个词。
:::

:::annotation
宾语关系从句之所以烧脑，是因为中心词和动词被硬生生隔开，读者得在工作记忆里同时挂着两头。作者特地把带不带 that 拆开统计：省掉可选的 that 恰好能省一个 token，结果模型在两类从句上的偏好真的走出了相反方向。
:::

#### 名词堆叠复合词（Stacked noun compounds）

:::bi

In English, nouns freely modify other nouns in an adjectival way: in the phrase *coffee shop*, *coffee* modifies *shop*, despite usually appearing as a noun.
This feature enables stacking multiple nouns into rich compound words which denote highly specific meanings.
However, such stacks can introduce bracketing ambiguity, as in the headline *Squad Helps Dog Bite Victim* (Cooper, 1980), and disrupt reading (Lauer, 1995; Nakov and Hearst, 2005; Staub et al., 2007; Cohen and Staub, 2014).
Here, we measure the rate of occurrence of stacked noun compounds, defined as three or more nouns in a modifier relationship.

:::zh

在英语里，名词可以直接充当定语修饰另一个名词：短语 *coffee shop* 里的 *coffee* 平时是名词，这里却用来修饰 *shop*。
借助这一特性，多个名词能一路堆叠成表意极其具体的复合词。
但名词堆叠很容易带来切分歧义，比如著名的新闻标题 *Squad Helps Dog Bite Victim*（Cooper, 1980），从而打断阅读节奏（Lauer, 1995; Nakov and Hearst, 2005; Staub et al., 2007; Cohen and Staub, 2014）。
本文统计的名词堆叠复合词，定义为三个及以上连续成修饰关系的名词序列。
:::

#### 名词动用（Denominal verbs）

:::bi

Verbs which are formed directly from nouns, such as in the phrase *bottle the whiskey* are known as *denominal* verbs.
While some denominal verbs are highly conventionalized and well-attested in human language, using a denominal verb in an ad-hoc way is marked (e.g., *calendar* it). Readers can interpret such innovations (Clark and Clark, 1979), but part-of-speech conversion triggers syntactic re-evaluation (Thierry et al., 2008), and noun/verb-ambiguous words are processed differently from unambiguous ones (Frazier and Rayner, 1987; Federmeier et al., 2000).
Often, the alternative to using a denominal verb is to use a longer, more syntactically complex phrase, e.g. *put the whiskey in a bottle*.
We therefore predict that a pressure for information compression will favor usage of denominal verbs beyond their base rate in humans, even at the expense of intelligibility.

:::zh

直接由名词转化而成的动词叫做*名词动用*，例如短语 *bottle the whiskey* 里的 bottle。
虽然一部分名词动用早已约定俗成，融进了日常语言，但临时把名词当动词使仍属于有标记的非常规用法（例如 *calendar* it）。读者固然能猜出这类新造用法的意思（Clark and Clark, 1979），但词性转换会迫使大脑重新做句法分析（Thierry et al., 2008），名动兼类词的加工过程也与词性单一的词截然不同（Frazier and Rayner, 1987; Federmeier et al., 2000）。
如果不把名词当动词用，往往得换成更长，句法更繁琐的短语，比如把 *bottle the whiskey* 写成 *put the whiskey in a bottle*。
因此我们预测，信息压缩压力会推高模型使用名词动用的频率，使其超出人类基线，哪怕这样会牺牲可懂度。
:::

:::annotation
名词动用的账算得很明白：calendar it 比 put it on the calendar 省下四个词。为了不把常规用法算进去，作者只统计那些在人类语料里极少当动词使的词元。
:::

#### 无生命主语（Inanimate subjects）

:::bi

We count the instances of inanimate subjects used with active verbs that typically take only animate subjects. Examples include “`is_file()` **swallows** that and returns False” and “Puzzle patterns where this asymmetry **bites**”.
Such sentences violate a selectional restriction (Chomsky, 1965) on which verbs typically take which subjects. They can be understood as personification or metonymy (Lakoff and Johnson, 1980), but readers use animacy as a cue during parsing (Trueswell et al., 1994), and without additional context, a mismatch may elicit a P600 response associated with reanalysis (Kim and Osterhout, 2005; Kuperberg, 2007).
However, rephrasing these sentences to avoid inanimate subjects would potentially require lengthening the sentence: for example, the word “bites” stands in for “causes an issue”.
We speculate that during training, models may have converged to using such constructions as efficient, albeit non-standard, ways of expressing meaning which would otherwise take up more tokens.

:::zh

我们统计无生命主语搭配主动动词的实例，这类动词在正常情况下只接有生命主语，模型原话里的典型例子如“`is_file()` **swallows** that and returns False”以及“Puzzle patterns where this asymmetry **bites**”。
这类句子打破了动词对主语的句法选择限制（Chomsky, 1965）。虽然读者可以勉强按拟人或转喻来理解（Lakoff and Johnson, 1980），但人类在句法解析时高度依赖主语的有生性线索（Trueswell et al., 1994），缺乏上下文铺垫时，主谓有生性冲突会诱发代表句法重分析的 P600 脑电反应（Kim and Osterhout, 2005; Kuperberg, 2007）。
可一旦改写句子来避开无生命主语，字数往往就得变长，比如“bites”其实是把“causes an issue”压成了一个词。
我们推测，模型在训练过程中摸索出了这类虽不合常规却很省 token 的写法，用来压缩原本需要展开铺陈的意思。
:::

:::annotation
这类句子违背了动词对主语有生性的选择限制。读者顺着常规预期往下读，撞上无生命主语配主动动词就得推翻重来，脑电上会直接激出 P600 成分。模型爱这么写倒未必是为了修辞，纯粹是用一个动词把整串短语压扁了。
:::

### 往返翻译（Round-trip translation）

:::bi

We employ round-trip translation (bilingual pivoting), an established paraphrasing method that preserves meaning at scale Bannard and
Callison-Burch (2005); Mallinson et al. (2017); Wieting and Gimpel (2018); Federmann et al. (2019), as a method for separating a model output’s characteristic syntactic profile from its semantics. Translation normalizes the output to frequent forms, reducing lexical and syntactical richness Vanmassenhove et al. (2019); Vanmassenhove et al. (2021) and stripping stylistic and authorial traits Rabinovich et al. (2017); Prabhumoye et al. (2018).
The result is a parallel corpus in which each translation is paired with its source sentence. Because the round trip holds the semantic content of each response fixed while replacing its wording, a feature that becomes less frequent after the round trip is one that the model chose over an available alternative way of expressing the same content.
To create this parallel corpus, we translate each sentence independently from English to Japanese and back with NLLB-200 (3.3B; NLLB Team, 2024).
Japanese was chosen because pivot languages unrelated to English tend to yield more diverse paraphrases Federmann et al. (2019).

:::zh

为了把模型输出特有的句法习气跟它要表达的语义剥离开，我们采用了往返翻译（双语中转）。这是一种成熟的同义改写手段，能在大规模语料上稳住语义 Bannard and Callison-Burch (2005); Mallinson et al. (2017); Wieting and Gimpel (2018); Federmann et al. (2019)。翻译过程会把非常规表达磨平为高频句式，降低词汇与句法复杂度 Vanmassenhove et al. (2019); Vanmassenhove et al. (2021)，同时抹掉文体印记与作者个人风格 Rabinovich et al. (2017); Prabhumoye et al. (2018)。
由此得到的平行语料库里，每句译文都与原句一一配对。既然往返翻译锁死了回答的语义内容，只替换具体措辞，那么凡是在往返翻译后出现率下降的特征，就都是模型放着现成的常规说法不用，主动挑出来的表达方式。
具体操作上，我们用 NLLB-200（3.3B; NLLB Team, 2024）把每个句子单独从英语译成日语，再译回英语。
之所以选中日语，是因为与英语毫无亲缘关系的中转语言往往能改写出差异更大的同义句 Federmann et al. (2019)。
:::

:::annotation
往返翻译是全篇最巧的设计。把英语句子翻成日语再翻回来，语义锁死，生僻句式却会被翻译模型磨平成家常话。只要某个特征在往返后变少，就坐实了那是模型放着顺畅说法不用，自己挑出来的。
:::

#### 相比同义改写分布，前沿模型过度使用罕见 n-gram，名词动用，无生命主语与缩略 ORC（Frontier models over-use rare n-grams, denominal verbs, inanimate subjects, and reduced ORCs, relative to the paraphrase distribution.）

:::bi

Across all three settings and almost all frontier and near-frontier models, the round-trip translations reveal that AI text has more unattested bigrams and higher n-gram perplexity. Within the Olmo family, the effects grow from the base to the instruct to the reasoning model.

:::zh

#### PERSUADE 作文上的效应整体偏小（These effects are smaller overall for the PERSUADE essays.）

:::bi

The PERSUADE essays show smaller changes under round-trip translation on several categories (n-gram measures, dependency lengths, stacked nouns, denominal verbs) relative to chat and coding (Figures 6 and 3(a)). #### 不同模型之间存在显著差异（There are notable differences between models.）

The Llama models and the Olmo base and instruct models change least under round-trip translation (Figure 2) and are closest to the student essays on the n-gram and verb measures. Meanwhile, Olmo Think patterns with the frontier models.

In sum, most frontier models over-represent linguistically difficult features relative to paraphrases, and relative to human text for inanimate subjects and low-frequency n-grams (Figure 3(b)).

:::zh

总体而言，相比同义改写，绝大多数前沿模型都过度使用了高加工难度的语言特征，而在无生命主语和低频 n-gram 两项上，它们也显著超出了人类文本基线（图 3(b)）。

## 实验二：长度惩罚对语言偏好的影响（Experiment 2: Effect of length penalties on language preferences）

:::bi

The purpose of Experiment 2 is to test **H2** – the hypothesis that models trained under a pressure for information density are more likely to shift their linguistic output toward difficult and non-humanlike linguistic features. While Experiment 1 provides circumstantial evidence for **H2** (e.g., that models over-use constructions that are less verbose than semantically equivalent alternatives), we additionally aim to understand a possible mechanism by which a pressure for information density may arise in LLMs.

:::zh

实验二旨在检验 **H2**，即在信息密度压力下训练的模型，其语言输出更容易倒向高难度且不像人的语言特征。实验一为 **H2** 提供了间接旁证（例如模型偏爱比同义句式更简短的结构），而在实验二中，我们希望进一步摸清信息密度压力在 LLM 内部生成的具体机制。
:::

### 实验设置（Setup）

:::bi

We start with an open-weights model, `Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct` (Yang et al., 2024), and a task of converting JSON-style structured event descriptions into English sentences.
We apply fine-tuning via reinforcement learning with GRPO (Shao et al., 2024), varying two key parameters: $\beta$, the KL divergence regularization term which penalizes a model for drifting from the base model’s next-token distribution, and $\lambda$, a length penalty term which penalizes responses for verbosity.
Each event seen during training or testing can be expressed using either member of a syntactic alternation where one alternative is shorter by a known number of tokens (except the particle control, where both orders have the same length).
Human speakers use both members of such alternations and choose between them according to context; for example, they are more likely to include an optional *that* when the material that follows is less predictable (Levy and Jaeger, 2007; Jaeger, 2010).
The key question is whether training a model with a length penalty pushes it instead toward a categorical preference for the shorter member, regardless of context, which would be a divergence from human language use.
We evaluate in three settings. The **trained alternations** are new sentences, never seen in training, built from the four alternations used in training. **Near transfer** denotes test sentences which contain alternations involving the optional word “that” (*sure (that)*, *so (that)*); however, these specific constructions were not seen during training, so the model omitting “that” in these cases would indicate a generalization to a novel context. Finally, **far transfer** denotes unrelated alternations (genitive, quantifier, passive and purpose-clause alternations; Table 3), where a model could in theory choose to conserve tokens by choosing the shorter alternation, though this would indicate that the model has generalized to an information-dense preference in contexts unrelated to the training data. See Appendix C for details (Figure 8; Table 4).

:::zh

我们以开放权重模型 `Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct`（Yang et al., 2024）为基座，训练任务是把 JSON 风格的结构化事件描述改写成英语句子。
我们采用 GRPO 强化学习算法做微调（Shao et al., 2024），集中调节两个关键超参数：一是 KL 散度正则项系数 $\beta$，用来惩罚模型偏离基座模型的下一 token 分布，二是长度惩罚项系数 $\lambda$，用来惩罚冗长的回复。
训练与测试中的每条事件，都可以用某组句法交替里的任一变体来表达，其中短变体比长变体固定少几个 token（小品词控制组除外，该组两种语序的 token 数完全相同）。
人类说话时两种变体都会用，并且会看语境下菜：比如当后文内容较难预测时，人类更倾向于保留可选的关系词 *that*（Levy and Jaeger, 2007; Jaeger, 2010）。
实验的核心问题在于：带长度惩罚的训练，是否会反过来把模型推向无视语境，一刀切偏爱短变体的极端状态，从而偏离人类的自然语言习惯。
我们在三种设定下做评估。**训练过的句法交替**采用训练阶段见过的四类交替结构，但测试句本身是全新的。**近迁移**测试包含可选词“that”的其他句式（如 *sure (that)*，*so (that)*），这些具体搭配从未在训练中出现，如果模型在这些句子里也省掉“that”，就说明压缩偏好泛化到了新语境。**远迁移**则考察与训练完全无关的四类交替（属格交替，量词交替，被动交替与目的从句交替，见表 3），模型理论上同样能靠选短变体来省 token，一旦出现这种现象，就表明模型已经把高信息密度偏好泛化到了与训练数据毫无瓜葛的句法结构上。实验细节见附录 C（图 8，表 4）。
:::

:::annotation
实验二的构造非常干净：让模型把 JSON 事件转成一句英文，每道题都有长短两种合法句式，相差一两个 token。加上长度惩罚后，模型一旦无视语境全选短句，就说明它只认 token 账本，不再顾及读者的理解成本。
:::

### 结果：长度惩罚把模型推向更短的变体，在未训练的结构上也是如此（Results: A length penalty shifts models toward shorter variants, including in untrained constructions）

:::bi

As can be seen in Figure 7, the probability of using the shorter construction increases as a function of the length penalty $\lambda$, both with and without the KL penalty $\beta$.
For the setting without KL-regularization, preference for the short alternative rises rapidly as a function of length penalty ($\lambda$).
Preference for the short alternation rises, albeit more gradually, even in the presence of KL-regularization.
Without KL regularization, runs with $\lambda=0$ had different preferences for the short alternative ranging from 0% to 100% depending on seed, indicating no stable preference. Meanwhile, for any $\lambda>0$, all seeds converged to a categorical preference for the short alternative.
The models learn a tendency for information compression that transfers to additional constructions not seen in training. This was consistent in the near transfer case; far transfer was only observed without KL penalty in the genitive alternation, one of four far-transfer alternations tested (Appendix C).

:::zh

如图 7 所示，无论是否施加 KL 惩罚 $\beta$，模型选用短结构的概率都会随长度惩罚 $\lambda$ 的增大而攀升。
在不加 KL 正则化的设定下，短变体偏好随长度惩罚（$\lambda$）急剧拉升。
即便加上 KL 正则化，短变体偏好依然稳步走高，只是斜率更平缓。
没有 KL 正则化且 $\lambda=0$ 时，各次运行对短变体的偏好完全随随机种子摇摆，从 0% 到 100% 不等，没有稳定倾向。可只要 $\lambda>0$，所有随机种子都会收敛到对短变体的绝对偏好。
模型习得的信息压缩倾向还会迁移到训练未见的句法结构上。这种迁移在近迁移设定下一以贯之，而在远迁移测试的四类结构中，只有不加 KL 惩罚时的属格交替观察到了显著迁移（附录 C）。
:::

:::annotation
λ 控制长度惩罚，β 控制 KL 正则。撤掉 KL 约束（β=0）时，只要 λ 大于零，模型选短句的比例瞬间拉满到近 100%，连训练里没见过的近迁移句式也照省不误。加上 KL 约束后曲线变缓，但朝向短句的趋势依然稳固。
:::

### 讨论（Discussion）

:::bi

We take these results as a proof of concept that a length penalty used during reinforcement learning with GRPO can directly affect grammatical features.
We note that the model was not directly instructed to use compressed or dense language, and was not directly given information about the availability of syntactic alternations. Rather, the penalty resulted in an emergent preference for shorter members of syntactic alternations. Without KL regularization this preference was near-categorical (over 98% short for both trained “that” alternations at every $\lambda>0$), unlike human speakers, who choose between the variants by context. Crucially, the models were still able to succeed at their task, since both members of an alternation were valid solutions to the training task. We speculate that the use of verifiable rewards creates an environment where correctness is decoupled from humanlike communication, leading models to converge toward a novel, potentially less comprehensible distribution of language use.

:::zh

上述结果提供了一项概念验证：在 GRPO 强化学习中引入长度惩罚，就足以直接改写模型的语法偏好。
值得强调的是，我们并未在提示词里授意模型使用紧凑或高密度的语言，也没有向它透露句法交替的存在。相反，对句法交替中短变体的偏好完全是在长度惩罚下自发涌现的。撤掉 KL 正则化时，这种偏好几乎是一刀切的（在所有 $\lambda>0$ 的配置下，两类训练过的“that”交替都有超过 98% 的输出选用短变体），与人类说话者按语境灵活选句式的习惯截然不同。而且由于长短两种变体都是训练任务的合法解，模型在极端倒向短句的同时依然能拿满任务奖励。我们由此推测，可验证奖励机制把“答对题”和“说人话”拆开了，促使模型收敛到一种全新的，对人类而言可能更难懂的语言分布。
:::

:::annotation
人类说话会看菜下碟：后文好猜就把 that 省掉，后文难猜就留着 that 帮听者缓冲。受过长度惩罚的模型却把看语境的弹性彻底丢了，一刀切地选短句，单靠压缩目标就把类人的选择机制替掉了。
:::

## 总讨论（General Discussion）

#### 模型的发展（Model Development）

:::bi

Past work has identified RLHF as a potential factor in the rise of word overuse by AI (Juzek and Ward, 2025; Juzek and Ward, 2026).
At the same time, a movement from RLHF to RLVR removes humans from the loop, and rewards models for verifiable solutions to problems. Human-feedback training has in fact been found to reward longer outputs (Singhal et al., 2024), so the pressure toward compression that we describe is more plausibly tied to verifiable-reward training. The move away from human feedback removes the implicit pressure for successful communication, and we speculate that this allows LLM-generated language to converge toward a dialect that is useful for LLMs to compress information densely and solve complex problems, but is suboptimal from the perspective of human intelligibility.
It is of course also possible to reward models for effective communication; indeed, our results indicate that more recent model variants like Opus 5.5 yield a quantifiable improvement over older models like Opus 5.

:::zh

既往研究曾指出，RLHF 可能是诱发 AI 高频滥用特定词汇的因素之一（Juzek and Ward, 2025; Juzek and Ward, 2026）。
但与此同时，后训练范式从 RLHF 转向 RLVR 把人类彻底移出了反馈回路，改为只按可验证的解题结果给奖。事实上，已有研究发现人类反馈训练反而会奖赏更长的输出（Singhal et al., 2024），因此本文所揭示的压缩压力更可能源于可验证奖励训练。一旦撤掉人类反馈，那股要求“把话讲明白”的隐性约束也随之消失，我们推测这正是 LLM 语言收敛出内部方言的契机：这种方言方便模型高密度压缩信息，攻克复杂推理题，代价则是牺牲了面向人类的可懂度。
当然，训练时完全可以把顺畅交流重新纳入奖励目标。我们的实验数据也显示，像 Opus 5.5 这样的新一代模型，相比前代 Opus 5 已经取得了可量化的可懂度改善。
:::

:::annotation
从 RLHF 转向 RLVR 是个分水岭。有人类打分时，读着拗口的回答多少会吃亏，而且人反而偏爱长回答。换成可验证奖励后，人类退出回路，判卷只看答案对错，压缩信息便再无阻力。
:::

#### 对心理语言学的启示（Implications for psycholinguistics）

:::bi

Multiple threads of research have studied the pressures that have shaped the structure of languages’ grammars and lexicons over the course of language evolution. Various pressures have been identified, such as those for dependency length minimization (Gildea and Temperley, 2010; Temperley and Gildea, 2018), communicative efficiency (Hahn et al., 2020), and uniform information density (Clark et al., 2023).
These pressures are not necessarily absolute (i.e. holding in all contexts in all languages), but are supported by large-scale, aggregate evidence across many of the world’s languages.
A key takeaway of this literature is that actual human language use is constrained by the realities and limitations of human comprehension, such as lossy working memory or incremental processing; sentences which consistently fail to lead to communicative success are likely to be weeded out from a language via grammar or convention.
The wide-scale training and deployment of AI models in contexts where human comprehension is not the primary purpose – such as developing large code bases or solving mathematical problems – presents a fundamentally different set of evolutionary pressures upon the linguistic profile of AI-generated language compared to natural language, and potentially provides novel opportunities for studying the emergence of communication systems.

:::zh

心理语言学的多条研究脉络，一直在探讨语言演化历程中塑造语法与词汇结构的底层压力，并识别出了依存距离最小化（Gildea and Temperley, 2010; Temperley and Gildea, 2018），交流效率（Hahn et al., 2020）以及均匀信息密度（Clark et al., 2023）等关键驱动力。
这些压力未必在所有语言的一切语境里绝对成立，但都在跨语种的大规模统计中得到了印证。
这批文献有一条核心共识：现实中的人类语言处处受制于人类理解机制的客观局限，比如有损的工作记忆和逐词推进的增量加工。那些让人反复理解失败的句式，终归会被语法规范或表达习惯淘汰出局。
如今 AI 模型被大规模训练和部署在大型代码库开发，数学解题等并不以“让人读懂”为首要目标的场景里。这给 AI 生成语言施加了一套与自然语言截然不同的演化压力，也反过来为观察新型交流系统的涌现提供了难得的实验场。
:::

## 结论（Conclusion）

:::bi

For frontier LLMs, the training objectives that make them successful at tasks like agentic coding and mathematical reasoning, especially with long and expensive context windows, are not guaranteed to be aligned with the same pressures that influence human language.
Natural language is subject to a pressure for communicative success, assuming the cognitive resource constraints of the human mind and incremental processing.
This discrepancy may help explain why many frontier LLMs use linguistic constructions which are technically allowed by the grammar of English, yet are out-of-distribution for human language and are associated with comprehension difficulty.
While increased exposure to LLM-generated text may acclimate humans to these out-of-distribution constructions, many of the patterns that have been evaluated in this work present fundamental challenges to intelligibility, and future work on LLMs may consider methods for better aligning LLM outputs with the needs of human comprehenders, in the service of user experience and monitorability.

:::zh

对前沿 LLM 而言，帮它们在智能体编程和数学推理等任务上大获成功的训练目标（尤其在上下文窗口既长又贵的条件下），未必与塑造人类自然语言的演化压力同向。
自然语言受制于人脑有限的认知资源与增量加工方式，始终围绕“促成顺畅交流”这一核心压力演化。
两边目标的错位，或许正好解释了为什么许多前沿 LLM 爱用那些“语法上挑不出毛病，却落在人类日常习惯分布之外，且会推高理解成本”的拗口结构。
尽管人类读者看多了 LLM 文本后可能会慢慢习惯这些生僻句式，但本文评估的多项结构特征挑战的是人类语言加工的底层瓶颈。未来的 LLM 研究不妨探索如何让模型输出重新对齐人类理解者的认知需求，这不仅关乎日常阅读体验，也直接关系到模型行为的可监控性。
:::

:::annotation
结尾点出了比阅读体验更要命的隐患：可监控性。模型越是演化出自己那套高密度方言，人类就越难看穿它的推理过程，这在智能体自主跑长任务的场景里会直接危及安全监督。
:::

## 局限（Limitations）

:::bi

We only examine a subset of commonly used models, only conduct analyses in English, and use a limited number of conversation seeds (chat topics and code repositories). Ideally, the study would be replicated on a larger number of seeds and model families.
Causally identifying sources of linguistic difficulty in frontier models would ideally involve studying them as they are post-trained, but this is not feasible to do at scale and so we rely on proxies. Similarly, limited compute makes studying post-training difficult.

:::zh

本文仅考察了常用模型的一个子集，分析语种限于英语，所用的对话种子（聊天话题与代码仓库）数量也较为有限。理想情况下，后续研究应在更大规模的种子集与更多模型家族上复现上述检验。
此外，要想在因果层面上坐实前沿模型语言晦涩的根源，最理想的做法是在它们接受后训练的过程中直接跟踪观测，但这在工程规模上难以企及，我们只能借助代理指标与小模型干预来间接验证。受限于算力，针对后训练阶段的系统性实验同样难以铺开。
:::

:::annotation
作者对证据边界交了底：前面是观测相关性，后面是小模型上的受控代理实验。真要在前沿模型上做因果归因，得介入它们真实的后训练流水线，学术界算力根本够不着。这篇的价值在于先把机制假说和第一份可复现证据立住。
:::

### 通用基础设施（Common infrastructure）

#### 模型与获取方式（Models and access.）

:::bi

A total of 14 models were used, including four closed models from Anthropic (Claude Sonnet 5, Opus 5, Opus 5.5, and Fable 5), three from OpenAI’s GPT-5.6 family (Luna, Terra, and Sol), two open-weight near-frontier models (GLM-5 from Z.ai and Kimi K2.5 from Moonshot AI), and five smaller open models at different stages of training: Llama 3.1 8B (base and Instruct) and Olmo 3 7B (base, Instruct, and Think).
The nine frontier or near-frontier models were called through Amazon Bedrock (region `us-east-1`) on the API endpoint that Bedrock serves for each family: the Converse API for the Claude models, the OpenAI Responses API for the GPT models, and the OpenAI-compatible chat completions API for GLM and Kimi.
The smaller open models were run locally on an Apple M4 Max with mlx-lm 0.32.0, from 8-bit quantized weights.
The simulated user in the chat and code settings is Claude Sonnet 4.6 (not one of the nine studied frontier models), called through Bedrock.

:::zh

实验共涵盖 14 个模型：Anthropic 的四个闭源模型（Claude Sonnet 5，Opus 5，Opus 5.5，Fable 5），OpenAI GPT-5.6 家族的三个模型（Luna，Terra，Sol），两个开放权重的接近前沿模型（Z.ai 的 GLM-5 与 Moonshot AI 的 Kimi K2.5），以及五个处于不同训练阶段的小型开放权重模型，即 Llama 3.1 8B（base 与 Instruct）和 Olmo 3 7B（base，Instruct 与 Think）。
九个前沿与接近前沿的模型全部通过 Amazon Bedrock（`us-east-1` 区域）调用，按模型家族走 Bedrock 提供的对应原生接口：Claude 系列走 Converse API，GPT 系列走 OpenAI Responses API，GLM 与 Kimi 走兼容 OpenAI 的 chat completions API。
五个小型开放模型则在 Apple M4 Max 上用 mlx-lm 0.32.0 本地运行，加载 8 位量化权重。
对话和编程设定中的模拟用户统一由 Claude Sonnet 4.6 担任（不在受测的九个前沿模型之列），同样经由 Bedrock 调用。

#### 智能体运行框架（Agent harness.）

:::bi

Every call to a chat-trained model, including the simulated user’s, goes through Pi (`@earendil-works/pi-coding-agent`, pinned to version 0.84.4), an open-source agent harness. The two base models cannot be driven through a chat interface and were called directly on a text-completion endpoint (Section A.4). We chose Pi so that all models run under the same scaffold and the same prompt text rather than under each vendor’s own product. The harness starts one Pi process per conversational turn and resumes the same Pi session on each later turn. Each run has its own freshly generated Pi configuration directory; Pi’s extensions, skills, prompt templates and context files (such as a repository’s `AGENTS.md`) are switched off, as are its context compaction and its own retry logic.

:::zh

除两个基座模型外，所有对话模型（含模拟用户）的调用均走开源智能体框架 Pi（`@earendil-works/pi-coding-agent`，锁定 0.84.4 版本）。两个基座模型没有聊天模板，直接调文本补全接口（§A.4）。选用 Pi 是为了让全部受测模型都跑在同一套脚手架和同一份提示词之下，避免各家官方前端产品的附加逻辑干扰结果。采集框架在每轮对话时拉起一个 Pi 进程，后续轮次直接续接同一个 Pi 会话。每次运行都分配独立的全新 Pi 配置目录，并关掉 Pi 自带的扩展，技能，提示词模板，仓库上下文文件（如 `AGENTS.md`），上下文压缩以及内置重试逻辑。
:::

#### 生成设置（Generation settings.）

:::bi

For the nine non-local models, each model ran at its provider’s defaults. The maximum output length is 32,000 tokens per call (Kimi K2.5 is served with a 16,384-token ceiling by its provider; no reply approached either limit). Reasoning is requested at Pi’s “medium” level, mapped to each model’s corresponding level: adaptive thinking for the Claude models and medium reasoning effort for the GPT models, GLM and Kimi. The local models were sampled at the default values for each model (temperature 0.6; top-p 0.9 for Llama and 0.95 for Olmo). Of these, only Olmo Think produces reasoning. The base models had a 2,000-token output limit. The simulated user runs with reasoning off and no tools.

:::zh

九个云端模型均沿用各提供商的默认采样参数。单次调用的最大输出长度设为 32,000 token（Kimi K2.5 受限于提供商接口，上限为 16,384 token，实际没有任何回复逼近这两个上限）。推理强度统一按 Pi 的“medium”档位请求，并映射为各模型的对应配置：Claude 系列开启自适应思考（adaptive thinking），GPT 系列，GLM 与 Kimi 设为 medium 推理强度。本地模型按各自的官方默认参数采样（温度 0.6，Llama 的 top-p 为 0.9，Olmo 为 0.95），其中只有 Olmo Think 会输出推理过程。两个基座模型的输出上限设为 2,000 token。模拟用户全程关闭推理，也不挂载任何工具。
:::

#### 日志与重试（Logging and retries.）

:::bi

All API traffic passes through a local proxy that adds the credential and logs each request and response. Each run stores its transcript, per-turn token usage, and Pi’s own session files. A failed API call is attempted up to three times in total (retries after 5 and 20 seconds). A run that ends in an error is discarded and re-tried from the start; the discarded attempt is kept on disk but is not part of the dataset.

:::zh

所有 API 请求都经由本地代理转发，由代理统一注入凭据并完整记录每一次请求与响应。每次运行都会落盘保存对话全文，逐轮 token 用量以及 Pi 的原生会话文件。单次 API 调用失败最多重试三次（间隔分别为 5 秒和 20 秒）。凡是中途报错终止的运行一律作废并从头重跑，作废记录虽保留在磁盘上，但不进入最终数据集。
:::

### 对话（Chat）

#### 设计（Design.）

:::bi

Each of the nine frontier models, Olmo Think, Olmo Instruct, and Llama Instruct hold one conversation on each of the ten topics in Table 5a (120 conversations). The model has no tools. Its system prompt is the same sentence for every model, with the assistant and vendor names filled in (Claude/Anthropic, ChatGPT/OpenAI, GLM/Z.ai, Kimi/Moonshot AI, Olmo/the Allen Institute for AI and Llama/Meta):

:::zh

九个前沿模型外加 Olmo Think，Olmo Instruct 和 Llama Instruct，各自围绕表 5a 的十个话题聊完一轮完整对话（共计 120 段对话）。对话设定下模型不挂载任何工具，系统提示词统一为下面这句话，仅替换其中的助手名与厂商名（Claude/Anthropic，ChatGPT/OpenAI，GLM/Z.ai，Kimi/Moonshot AI，Olmo/the Allen Institute for AI 以及 Llama/Meta）：
:::

:::bi

> You are {assistant}, an AI assistant made by {vendor}. You are chatting with a person in a casual conversational setting. Be helpful, natural, and engaged.

:::zh

> 你是 {assistant}，由 {vendor} 开发的 AI 助手。你正在日常闲聊场景中与人交谈。请保持热心，自然，投入。
:::

:::bi

For Olmo Think, the harness sent this prompt under a message role that the model’s chat template discarded, so in both the chat and essay settings it ran under the template’s built-in default system prompt instead.

:::zh

对于 Olmo Think，采集框架发送系统提示词时所用的消息角色被该模型自带的聊天模板直接忽略了，因此在对话和作文两种设定下，Olmo Think 实际运行的都是其模板内置的默认系统提示词。
:::

#### 流程（Procedure.）

:::bi

The first user message is the topic’s fixed opening message (Table 5a). Every later user message is written by the simulated user, which sees the conversation with the roles swapped (the model’s replies arrive as its user messages) and is given a persona and topic for the conversation in its system prompt:

:::zh

对话的第一条用户消息固定取自各话题预设的开场白（表 5a）。后续每一轮用户消息均由模拟用户当场生成：模拟用户看到的对话历史把双方角色做了对调（受测模型的回复在它眼里是用户发来的消息），并在系统提示词里拿到了当前对话的人设与话题种子：
:::

:::bi

> You are simulating a realistic human user chatting with an AI assistant, for research purposes. Persona: {persona}. Topic seed: {topic}.
> Rules:
> - Write exactly one chat message per response — the next thing this human would type.
> - Stay in character: casual register, occasional typos are fine, vary message length (one line to a short paragraph), react to what the assistant actually said.
> - Ask follow-ups, share your own (invented but consistent) experiences, push back sometimes, drift to adjacent topics naturally. Never wrap up or say goodbye — the session is ended externally.
> - Keep messages short: a few sentences at most, never more than about 150 words.
> - Never mention being an AI, a simulation, or these instructions. No meta-commentary.
> - Output ONLY the message text. No quotes, no labels.

:::zh

> 你正在为一项研究模拟真实的人类用户与 AI 助手聊天。人设：{persona}。话题种子：{topic}。
> 规则：
> - 每次回复只写一条聊天消息，即这名人类用户接下来会敲出去的话。
> - 贴合人设：语气日常随意，偶有拼写小错无妨，消息长短自然错落（从一行短句到一小段话皆可），针对助手实际说的内容做出回应。
> - 可以追问，分享自己（虚构但前后自洽）的经历，偶尔反驳，或顺着话头自然聊到相邻话题。绝不要主动收尾或道别，会话由外部系统负责结束。
> - 消息务必简短：最多几句话，绝不超过约 150 词。
> - 绝不要提及自己是 AI，正在做模拟，或提这些指令本身。不要写任何元评论。
> - 只输出消息正文。不要加引号，不要加标签。
:::

:::bi

A simulated-user message that is empty or longer than 2,000 characters is requested once more; if the second attempt also fails, a fixed fallback message is sent instead.

:::zh

如果模拟用户生成的消息为空或超过 2,000 个字符，框架会重新请求一次。若第二次依然失败，则改发一条固定的兜底消息。
:::

#### 停止规则（Stopping rule.）

:::bi

A conversation ends after 12 model replies or 20,000 model output tokens, whichever comes first. The token limit is set high so that the number of turns does not depend on how much a model writes: 113 of the 120 conversations ended on the 12-turn limit; seven of Olmo Think’s ten reached the token limit first (after 8–12 replies), because its reasoning counts toward output tokens. In total there are 1,428 replies.

:::zh

单段对话在模型给出 12 条回复或累计输出满 20,000 token 时结束，以先触发者为准。把 token 上限设得这么宽裕，是为了防止对话轮数受模型话多话少的影响：120 段对话中有 113 段都跑满了 12 轮上限，只有 Olmo Think 的十段对话里有七段因为思考链计入输出 token，在第 8 到 12 轮之间提前撞上了 token 上限。全部对话共采集到 1,428 条模型回复。
:::

### 代码（Code）

#### 设计（Design.）

:::bi

Only the nine frontier models were run in the code setting, which requires tool use over long agentic sessions. Each model works once on each of the five code repositories in Table 5b (45 sessions).
The model runs as a coding agent with Pi’s default system prompt (about 350 words, identical for every model) and Pi’s default tools for reading, writing and editing files and running shell commands. We add no system prompt of our own.

:::zh

编程设定需要在长程智能体会话中频繁调用工具，因此只跑了九个前沿模型。每个模型在表 5b 的五个代码仓库上各执行一次会话（共 45 次会话）。
模型以编程智能体的身份运行，直接沿用 Pi 的默认系统提示词（约 350 词，所有模型完全一致）与默认工具集（读取，写入，编辑文件以及执行 shell 命令），我们没有额外追加自定义系统提示词。
:::

#### 工作区和沙盒（Workspace and sandbox.）

:::bi

For each session the harness clones the repository into a new directory and installs the repository’s dependencies before the agent starts. The whole agent process runs inside one macOS Seatbelt (`sandbox-exec`) profile: it can write only to the workspace and to the run’s own directories, it cannot read the home directory’s credential paths, and its only network access is the local API proxy.
At the end of a session a diff against the original commit is saved; this serves as a record of the agent’s work and is not analyzed as text.

:::zh

每次会话开始前，采集框架先把目标仓库克隆到全新的独立目录，并装好项目依赖，再启动智能体。整个智能体进程被关在 macOS Seatbelt（`sandbox-exec`）沙盒内运行：只允许向当前工作区和本次运行的专属目录写文件，禁止读取主目录下的凭据路径，唯一的网络出口是本地 API 代理。
会话结束时会保存一份相对于初始提交的 git diff，留作智能体实际改动的存档，不纳入文本分析。
:::

#### 流程（Procedure.）

:::bi

The first user message is fixed, with the repository’s name, language and test command filled in:

:::zh

第一条用户消息采用固定模板，仅填入目标仓库的名称，编程语言与测试命令：
:::

:::bi

> I’ve got a small project checked out here ({name}, {language}). Can you sweep the codebase for bugs and propose fixes? Please actually make the fixes you’re confident about. Use quick local checks ({test command}) rather than anything heavyweight — no deployments, no installing big new dependencies.
> One thing: this box has no network access, and the dependencies are already installed in {path}, so nothing needs fetching.

:::zh

> 我在这儿检出了一个小项目（{name}，{language}）。你能把代码库扫一遍，排查里面的 bug 并给出修复方案吗？凡是你有把握的修复，请直接动手改好。验证时用轻量的本地检查（{test command}）就行，别搞太重的流程，不要做部署，也不要装庞大的新依赖。
> 另外提醒一句：这台机器断了外网，所需依赖都已经装在 {path} 里了，不用再去拉取任何包。
:::

:::bi

Each user message starts an *episode*: the model works, with as many tool calls as it chooses, until it returns control. Later user messages come from the same simulated user as in the chat setting, under the same system prompt, with the topic “reviewing an automated bug sweep of {name} ({language}, {domain})” and this persona:

:::zh

用户的每条消息都会开启一个*回合*（episode）：模型接手后可以自由发起任意多次工具调用，直到把控制权交回用户。后续轮次的用户消息由日常对话里同一个模拟用户生成，沿用同一份系统提示词框架，只是把话题换成“评审针对 {name}（{language}，{domain}）的自动化 bug 排查结果”，人设改为：
:::

:::bi

> the developer who owns this repository. You know the project’s purpose and history but not the internals of every module — you are not an expert on this code. You ask about specific findings the assistant reports, ask it to explain fixes in plain terms, question changes that look risky, and sometimes ask for one more pass over an area you care about. You write like a working developer in a terminal: brief, concrete, lowercase is fine

:::zh

> 你是这个仓库的维护者。你清楚项目的来龙去脉和设计初衷，但并不熟悉每个模块的内部细节，也算不上这份代码的专家。你会针对助手汇报的具体发现追问细节，请它用大白话讲清楚修复思路，对看着有风险的改动提出质疑，偶尔还会让它把你关心的一块代码再复查一遍。你打字就像平时在终端里干活的程序员：简短，实在，全用小写字母也无所谓
:::

:::bi

The simulated user sees only the text of the conversation: its own messages and the text the model wrote. It does not see tool calls, tool output or diffs.

:::zh

模拟用户只能看到对话面板上的纯文本（它自己发出的消息与模型写下的回复），看不到中间的工具调用，工具输出或代码 diff。
:::

#### 停止规则（Stopping rule.）

:::bi

A session ends after 11 episodes, or earlier once the session’s episode-final text reaches a cumulative estimated 8,000 tokens; the rule is checked only between episodes.
The session then closes with one more fixed user message asking the model to write a summary of its work to a file (`MEMORY.md`) so that the session could be continued later.
The reply to this closing message is collected but left out of the analysis. Of the 45 sessions, 42 ended on the turn limit and three (all Opus 5) on the token limit.

:::zh

单次编程会话在跑满 11 个回合，或各回合末回复累计达到约 8,000 token 时结束（仅在回合间隙检查该条件）。
触发结束条件后，框架会再发一条固定的收尾消息，请模型把本次工作总结写入 `MEMORY.md` 文件，以便日后续接会话。
模型对这条收尾消息的回复同样会落盘保存，但不纳入后续语言学分析。45 次会话中，有 42 次跑满 11 个回合正常结束，另有 3 次（全都是 Opus 5）提前触发了 token 上限。
:::

#### 分析单位（Unit of analysis.）

:::bi

In both settings we analyze *turn-final replies*: the last text the model writes in reply to a user message, with no tool call after it. In chat every reply is turn-final. In code, text written between tool calls is not analyzed, and an episode that ends on a tool call does not contribute a reply.

:::zh

在对话和编程两种设定下，我们的分析对象都是*回合末回复*（turn-final replies），即模型响应用户消息时写下的最后一段话，后面不再跟任何工具调用。日常对话里的每一条模型回复天然都是回合末回复。而在编程设定中，夹在两次工具调用之间的过渡说明不纳入统计，若某个回合直接以工具调用收尾，则该回合不产出有效回复。
:::

### 作文（Essay）

#### 学生作文（Student essays.）

:::bi

The human comparison is the PERSUADE 2.0 corpus of essays by US students in grades 6–12 (Crossley et al., 2024). We use the corpus’s independent writing assignments only, since the text-dependent assignments rely on source articles not included in the corpus, and we drop essays with a missing grade level, English-language-learner status or score. Seven assignments remained: cell phones at school, community service, distance learning, grades for extracurricular activities, mandatory extracurricular activities, seeking multiple opinions, and summer projects.
From these we keep the essays with a holistic score of 5 or 6 on the corpus’s 1–6 scale: 2,934 essays, between 54 and 1,014 per assignment.

:::zh

人类基线取自 PERSUADE 2.0 语料库，内含美国 6 至 12 年级中学生撰写的议论文（Crossley et al., 2024）。由于材料作文依赖语料库未收录的外部阅读文本，我们只选取其中的独立命题作文，并剔除了缺失年级，英语学习者状态或评分记录的样本。最终留下七道作文题：校园手机管理，社区服务，远程学习，课外活动评分，强制参加课外活动，听取多方意见，以及暑期项目。
我们只保留在语料库 1 至 6 分制下总评拿到 5 分或 6 分的高分作文，共计 2,934 篇，单题样本量在 54 篇到 1,014 篇之间。
:::

#### 模型作文（Model essays.）

:::bi

Each model writes ten essays for each of the seven assignments (980 essays).
The system prompt is the first sentence of the chat prompt alone (“You are {assistant}, an AI assistant made by {vendor}.”), without the sentence about a casual conversation. The two base models have no chat interface and were given the assignment as a document to continue: the assignment text, a blank line, and the line “Essay:”. Generation stopped at a second “Essay:” line, the end of text, or 2,000 tokens; 31 of their 140 essays reached that limit. Generation settings are otherwise those of the chat setting. The models are not shown student essays and are not asked to write as a student. All 980 runs completed on the first attempt with no refusals.

:::zh

每个模型针对七道作文题各写十篇作文，合计 980 篇。
系统提示词仅保留日常对话提示词的第一句（“You are {assistant}, an AI assistant made by {vendor}.”），删去了后面关于日常闲聊的要求。两个基座模型没有对话接口，我们直接把作文题拼成待续写的文档喂给模型：先是题目原文，空一行，再写一行“Essay:”。生成过程在再次遇到“Essay:”，到达文本末尾或输出满 2,000 token 时停止（两个基座模型的 140 篇作文里有 31 篇撞上了 2,000 token 上限）。其余采样参数均与日常对话设定一致。我们没有给模型看人类学生范文，也没有要求它们模仿学生口吻。全部 980 次生成均一次跑通，无一拒答。
:::

### 处理（Processing）

#### 对话和代码（Chat and code.）

:::bi

Turn-final replies are read from the transcripts.
Markup for emphasis and links is removed, but the text inside it is kept.
Fenced code blocks, HTML and images are removed.
Inline code spans, URLs, file paths, code identifiers and inline mathematics are each replaced by a single placeholder word, so that a sentence containing one still counts as one sentence; n-grams containing the placeholder are not counted.
Arrows, emoji and stray mathematical symbols are removed and end the stretch.
No sentence or n-gram may cross a heading, list item, table cell or paragraph break.
Sentences are split with `syntok`. The same extraction is applied to the human reference corpora.

:::zh

我们从会话记录中提取出所有回合末回复。
加粗，斜体与超链接等格式标记会被剥离，仅保留包裹在内的正文文字。
围栏代码块，HTML 标签与图片一律剔除。
行内代码片段，URL，文件路径，代码标识符以及行内数学公式，各自替换成单个占位词，保证含这些元素的句子仍按完整单句切分，而凡是包含该占位词的 n-gram 一律不纳入计数。
箭头符号，emoji 与零散数学符号直接删去，并在该位置截断当前文本片段。
切句与提取 n-gram 时均不跨越标题，列表项，表格单元格或段落边界。
分句统一使用 `syntok`。人类参照语料库也走完全相同的抽取与清洗流程。
:::

#### 作文（Essays.）

:::bi

Student and model essays share one cleaning procedure: encoding errors are repaired and quotation marks and whitespace are normalized; up to four leading title, name or date lines are dropped; and each of the corpus’s masked-name placeholders (for example `Generic_Name`) is replaced by a one-word stand-in name, which is excluded from noun counts and from any n-gram that contains it. Spelling is not corrected. Model essays are first converted from markdown to plain text. In this step, a leading heading is dropped as the title, and text set apart from the essay by a horizontal rule (such as a note to the reader) is removed. Section headings are kept as paragraphs but left out of sentence-level measures. Bracketed slots such as `[Your Name]` are replaced by the same stand-ins. For three Kimi K2.5 essays, reasoning text that preceded a `</think>` marker in the reply was also removed.

:::zh

人类学生作文与模型作文共用同一套清洗流水线：先修复编码乱码并统一引号与空白字符，再删去开头最多四行的标题，署名或日期。语料库原有的匿名姓名占位符（如 `Generic_Name`）统一替换成单字词长的虚拟人名，该人名不计入名词统计，含该人名的 n-gram 也一并剔除。原文拼写错误保留原样，不做修正。模型作文会先从 Markdown 转为纯文本：开头的一级标题视为文章题目直接删去，用水平分割线与正文隔开的附言（如致读者说明）也一并剔除。文内小标题虽保留为独立段落，但不参与句子级指标的计算。像 `[Your Name]` 这类方括号填空槽同样替换成上述虚拟人名。此外，Kimi K2.5 有三篇作文在正文前带出了 `</think>` 标记及思考过程，清洗时已将该段推理文本截除。
:::

## 附录 B：分析细节（Analysis details）

### 特征检测（Feature detection）

:::bi

All text is parsed with spaCy (Honnibal et al., 2020) (`en_core_web_trf`). Object relatives, stacked nouns and dependency lengths are read from the dependency parse. For denominal verbs and inanimate subjects, the parse supplies candidates: verbs whose lemma is used predominantly as a noun in the setting’s human corpus, and active verbs whose subject is an inanimate noun, a pronoun such as *it* or *this*, or a code span. Each candidate is then judged in its sentence by an external classifier (Jev 1.13.0 (Footnote: https://typesafe.ai)), and we count the candidates it accepts with probability above 0.5.

:::zh

全部文本均使用 spaCy (Honnibal et al., 2020)（`en_core_web_trf`）做依存句法分析。宾语关系从句，名词堆叠与依存距离直接从依存树上读取。名词动用与无生命主语则采用两步法：先由解析器筛出候选（前者筛词元在对应设定人类语料中主要充当名词的动词，后者筛主语为无生命名词，*it* 或 *this* 等代词，或行内代码片段的主动动词），再把每个候选连同所在句子一起送入外部分类器（Jev 1.13.0（脚注：https://typesafe.ai））打分，只统计分类器给出接受概率大于 0.5 的实例。
:::

:::annotation
这里有个读图时得留心的细节：名词动用和无生命主语无法单靠句法树定死，解析器只负责筛出候选，最后拍板的是外部分类器 Jev（概率阈值 0.5）。这两个指标自带分类器噪声，看相对高低比盯绝对数值更稳妥。
:::

### 人类基线和 n-gram 参照（Human baselines and n-gram references）

:::bi

Baselines for OpenSubtitles, StackExchange and Wikipedia are computed on a random sample of about 500,000 words from each.
For n-gram statistics, we build one trigram model per reference corpus (modified Kneser–Ney smoothing, built with KenLM; Heafield, 2011) and score chat against OpenSubtitles, coding against StackExchange, and essays against Wikipedia. Unattested pairs are reported per 1,000 pairs. Because the chat and coding references are also the human baselines for those settings, we report no human n-gram baseline there; the student essays are scored against Wikipedia in the same way as the model essays.

:::zh

OpenSubtitles，StackExchange 与 Wikipedia 的各项人类基线，分别在各自随机抽取的约 50 万词样本上算出。
计算 n-gram 指标时，我们用 KenLM（Heafield, 2011）在每个参照语料库上各训练一个带改进 Kneser–Ney 平滑的三元语言模型，将日常对话对准 OpenSubtitles 打分，编程会话对准 StackExchange 打分，作文对准 Wikipedia 打分。未出现过的二元组按每千个词对中的出现频次报告。由于日常对话和编程的参照语料本身就是这两个设定的人类基线，对应面板不再单列人类 n-gram 基线。人类学生作文则与模型作文一样，统一用 Wikipedia 三元模型打分。
:::

### 依存长度标准化（Dependency length standardization）

:::bi

Dependency length positively correlates with sentence length (the maximum possible dependency length in a sentence is one less than the number of words in the sentence), so models that write longer sentences would score higher even with similar syntactic profiles. To remove this, we use direct standardization (using an analogous method to age-standardization in epidemiology). Each sentence goes into one of five length bins (1–5, 6–10, 11–15, 16–20 and 21–30 words, punctuation not counted), and sentences over 30 words are dropped. Only arcs where neither end is punctuation are counted, and the root is excluded. For each source (i.e. different models, humans), we compute each bin’s mean arc length.
To standardize, bin weights are computed using the Wikipedia corpus to generate a ‘standard distribution’ of sentence length: for each bin, its weight is the share of total dependency arcs in Wikipedia which came from sentences in that bin.
The standardized value is then the average of the bin means, weighted by the Wikipedia-based weights. In other words, this procedure asks “If each source had the same sentence-length distribution as Wikipedia, how long would dependencies be on average?”

:::zh

依存距离与句子长度呈正相关（含 $n$ 个词的句子，理论最大依存距离为 $n-1$），因此就算两个模型的句法习惯完全一致，爱写长句的模型算出的平均依存距离也会偏高。为剔除句长混杂，我们借用流行病学里年龄标准化的思路做直接标准化：先把所有句子按词数（不计标点）归入五个长度区间（1–5，6–10，11–15，16–20 与 21–30 词），超过 30 词的超长句直接剔除。统计时只保留两端均非标点的依存弧，且不计根节点，由此算出每个来源（各模型与人类基线）在各句长区间内的平均弧长。
接着，我们用 Wikipedia 语料库生成句长的“标准分布”权重：某个区间的权重，就等于 Wikipedia 全部依存弧中落在该句长区间内的比例。
最后用这套 Wikipedia 权重对各区间的平均弧长做加权求和，即得标准化后的依存距离。换言之，这项指标回答的是：“假如所有模型和人类的句长分布都跟 Wikipedia 完全一样，它们的平均依存距离会有多长？”
:::

:::

:::bi

We maximize the KL-regularized expected reward

:::zh

我们的优化目标是最大化带 KL 正则化的期望奖励：
:::

$$
\begin{gathered}\displaystyle J(\theta)={}\displaystyle\mathbb{E}_{x\sim\mathcal{D},\,y\sim\pi_{\theta}(\cdot|x)}\big[R(x,y)\big]\\
\displaystyle-\beta\,\mathbb{E}_{x}\,\mathrm{KL}\big(\pi_{\theta}(\cdot|x)\,\|\,\pi_{\text{ref}}(\cdot|x)\big),\end{gathered} \tag{1}
$$

:::bi

where $\pi_{\text{ref}}$ is the frozen base model. With LoRA (Hu et al., 2022), $\pi_{\text{ref}}$ is the policy
network with the adapter disabled. For two variants that tie on task reward and differ by
$\Delta$ tokens, the optimum of (1),
$\pi^{*}(y|x)\propto\pi_{\text{ref}}(y|x)\exp(R(x,y)/\beta)$, satisfies

:::zh

其中 $\pi_{\text{ref}}$ 是冻结的基座模型。采用 LoRA (Hu et al., 2022) 微调时，$\pi_{\text{ref}}$ 就是停用适配器后的策略网络本身。对于任务奖励打平，仅在长度上相差 $\Delta$ 个 token 的长短两种变体，式 (1) 的最优策略 $\pi^{*}(y|x)\propto\pi_{\text{ref}}(y|x)\exp(R(x,y)/\beta)$ 满足：
:::

$$
\log\frac{\pi^{*}(\text{s})}{\pi^{*}(\text{l})}=\log\frac{\pi_{\text{ref}}(\text{s})}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(\text{l})}+\frac{\lambda\Delta}{\beta}, \tag{2}
$$

:::bi

where s and l are the short and long variants. This motivates the choice of $\lambda$ and $\beta$ values. When $\beta=0$ this optimum
doesn’t exist, and the shift is limited only by the length of training.

:::zh

式中 s 与 l 分别代表短变体和长变体。实验里 $\lambda$ 与 $\beta$ 的取值网格正是据此设定的。当 $\beta=0$ 时，上述解析最优解不复存在，策略偏移的幅度只受训练步数限制。
:::

:::bi

The reward for a completion $y$ to a prompt $x$ is

:::zh

给定提示词 $x$，生成补全 $y$ 的奖励函数定义为：
:::

$$
R(x,y)=c(x,y)\,f(y)-\lambda\,|y|, \tag{3}
$$

:::bi

where $c(x,y)\in\{0,1\}$ is correctness, $f(y)\in\{0,1\}$ is a format check, and $|y|$ is the
number of tokens in the completion under the policy’s tokenizer (special tokens not counted).
Correctness is rule-based: every argument phrase of the event must appear verbatim
(case-insensitive), a dependency parse (spaCy `en_core_web_trf`) must place each
phrase in its role, and the event verb must keep its lemma. Either member of an alternation,
and other paraphrases such as passives, are accepted; tense is not checked. The format check
requires a single sentence that starts with a capital letter, ends in a period or exclamation
mark, and has no preamble, quotation marks or colon.

:::zh

其中 $c(x,y)\in\{0,1\}$ 表示内容正确性，$f(y)\in\{0,1\}$ 表示格式合规性，$|y|$ 是补全文本在策略分词器下的 token 数（不计特殊 token）。正确性完全由规则判定：事件描述里的每个论元短语都必须原样出现（忽略大小写），spaCy（`en_core_web_trf`）依存分析树必须把每个短语挂在对应的语义角色上，且事件动词必须保持原词元。只要满足这些条件，句法交替里的长短变体乃至被动句等其他同义改写一律算对，动词时态也不做限制。格式检查则要求输出必须是单句，以大写字母开头，以句号或感叹号收尾，且不得夹带开场白，引号或冒号。
:::

:::bi

We use Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO; Shao et al., 2024) as implemented in TRL’s `GRPOTrainer`
(TRL 1.13.0). Each optimizer step does the following:

:::zh

我们采用 TRL 库 `GRPOTrainer`（TRL 1.13.0）实现的群组相对策略优化（GRPO，Shao et al., 2024）。每个优化步依次执行以下四步操作：
:::

:::bi

1. **Sampling.** For each of 8 prompts, sample a group of $G=16$ completions from the current policy with vLLM (Kwon et al., 2023) (colocated with training) at temperature $T=1.0$, up to 64 new tokens. That is 128 completions per step.

:::zh

1. **采样。** 针对批次内的 8 个提示词，用与训练进程同机部署的 vLLM (Kwon et al., 2023) 从当前策略各采样一组 $G=16$ 条补全（采样温度 $T=1.0$，最大生成长度 64 token），即每步共采样 128 条补全。
:::

:::bi

2. **Reward.** Score each completion with (3).

:::zh

2. **奖励计算。** 按式 (3) 为每条补全打分。
:::

:::bi

3. **Advantage.** Group-mean baseline with no standard-deviation scaling (`scale_rewards=’none’`):

:::zh

3. **优势估计。** 以组内均值作基线，关闭标准差缩放（`scale_rewards=’none’`）：
:::

$$
A_{i}=R_{i}-\frac{1}{G}\sum_{j=1}^{G}R_{j}. \tag{4}
$$

:::bi

The same $A_{i}$ is assigned to every token of completion $i$. We don’t divide by the group standard deviation because that would change $\lambda$’s scale from group to group. In a group where all completions tie on task reward, std scaling would inflate their small length differences to unit scale.

:::zh

补全 $i$ 里的每一个 token 都共享同一个优势值 $A_{i}$。之所以不除以组内标准差，是因为标准差缩放会让长度惩罚系数 $\lambda$ 的实际尺度随组内分布剧烈波动：一旦某组内的所有补全全都答对了题，标准差缩放就会把原本只有一两个 token 的微小长度差强行放大到单位尺度。
:::

:::bi

4. **Loss.** `loss_type=‘dr_grpo’` (Liu et al., 2025): per-token clipped surrogate terms are summed over all completions in the batch and divided by a constant (number of completions $\times$ `max_completion_length`), with no per-sequence length normalization:

:::zh

4. **损失计算。** 采用 `loss_type=‘dr_grpo’` (Liu et al., 2025)，即将逐 token 的裁剪代理损失在整个批次的全部补全上直接求和，再除以固定常数（补全总数 $\times$ `max_completion_length`），不做逐序列的长度归一化：
:::

$$
\begin{gathered}\mathcal{L}(\theta)=-\frac{1}{NL_{\max}}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\sum_{t=1}^{|y_{i}|}\ell_{i,t},\\
\ell_{i,t}=w_{i}\min\big(\rho_{i,t}A_{i},\;\bar{\rho}_{i,t}A_{i}\big)-\beta\,k_{i,t},\end{gathered} \tag{5}
$$

:::bi

where $N=128$ is the number of completions in the step, $L_{\max}=64$, $h_{i,t}=(x,y_{i,<t})$ is the prefix,

:::zh

其中 $N=128$ 为单步补全总数，$L_{\max}=64$，$h_{i,t}=(x,y_{i,<t})$ 为当前前缀，
:::

$$
\begin{gathered}\displaystyle\rho_{i,t}=\frac{\pi_{\theta}(y_{i,t}\mid h_{i,t})}{\pi_{\theta_{\text{old}}}(y_{i,t}\mid h_{i,t})},\\
\displaystyle\bar{\rho}_{i,t}=\operatorname{clip}(\rho_{i,t},1-\epsilon,1+\epsilon),\\
\displaystyle k_{i,t}=\frac{\pi_{\text{ref}}}{\pi_{\theta}}-\log\frac{\pi_{\text{ref}}}{\pi_{\theta}}-1\end{gathered}
$$

:::bi

(the per-token KL estimator TRL uses, evaluated at $y_{i,t}$). $w_{i}$ is TRL’s default correction for the mismatch between the vLLM sampler and the trainer’s forward pass: the ratio of the two sequence probabilities, set to zero if it exceeds 3. It was close to 1 throughout (mean 1.00). With one optimization pass per generation batch (TRL default), $\rho=1$ in value and the loss reduces to the sequence-level policy gradient $-\sum_{i}A_{i}\nabla\log\pi_{\theta}(y_{i}|x)$ up to a constant, plus the KL term. When $\beta=0$ no reference model is used and the KL term is dropped.

:::zh

（即 TRL 采用的逐 token KL 估计量，在 $y_{i,t}$ 处求值）。$w_{i}$ 是 TRL 为抵消 vLLM 采样器与训练器前向计算之间的数值偏差而引入的默认重要性权重，等于两边序列概率之比（超出 3 时直接截断置零，实际训练中全程紧贴 1，均值 1.00）。由于每批生成样本只做一次梯度更新（TRL 默认设置），$\rho$ 的数值恒为 1，上述损失在相差一个常数因子的意义下退化为序列级策略梯度 $-\sum_{i}A_{i}\nabla\log\pi_{\theta}(y_{i}|x)$ 外加 KL 正则项。当 $\beta=0$ 时，训练不加载参考模型，KL 项直接省去。
:::

:::bi

Table 4: Training configuration, identical for all 24 runs except $\lambda$, $\beta$ and seed.

:::zh

评估时，训练过的句法交替报告采样生成的正确输出中选用短变体的比例，近迁移与远迁移则报告模型在长短变体二选一强制打分时赋给短句的概率。在四类远迁移结构里，只有属格交替在不加 KL 正则化时随 $\lambda$ 显著倒向短变体，被动交替与目的从句交替均未随 $\lambda$ 移动，量词交替则在基座模型上本就接近封顶。
