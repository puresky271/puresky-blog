All scholarly fields strive to contribute new knowledge. In the field of human-computer interaction (HCI), this new knowledge increasingly comes in rich forms like videos and demos, but the archival research paper remains the most widely used and accepted capture and delivery mechanism for research knowledge. The knowledge contribution made by a research paper—or more precisely, made by the work a research paper describes—is any research paper’s central feature. For example, a theoretical physics paper may contribute a new mathematical model for the behavior of light near black holes. A civil engineering paper may contribute a new method for stress-testing bridges. A social anthropology paper may contribute an account of people’s reactions to teen pregnancies in rural religious communities. Whatever the field of inquiry, whatever the phenomenon of interest, every research paper strives to make a research contribution by offering new knowledge. In an effort to distinguish this kind of knowledge from everyday know-how, some scholars even capitalize the term: Knowledge. In the whole of human inquiry, there are, of course, countless specific research contributions to be made. But the types of these contributions—the general forms this new knowledge takes—are relatively few. The three examples in the opening paragraph each make a different type of contribution: The first is theoretical, the second is methodological, and the third is empirical. These are three different research contribution types—the knowledge they contribute comes to us from three different “ways of knowing” [1]. As is often observed, HCI is highly inter- and multidisciplinary. It is also young. It has taken a few decades for the types of knowledge in HCI to emerge, converge, and stabilize, and new ways of knowing still swirl about [2]. But over time, an identifiable pattern of research contribution types has evolved. These types were recently refined and put to use in the submission process for ACM CHI 2016, the flagship conference in HCI. In this article, we offer an encapsulation of the research contribution types in HCI, endeavoring to provide definition to our field and the types of knowledge it produces. We also describe how different contribution types have been codified in the CHI submission process. For the CHI 2016 conference, we show how the submitted and accepted papers were distributed across contribution types, providing insight into the kinds of knowledge the HCI field develops and disseminates.

:::zh

所有学术领域都力求贡献新知识。在人机交互（HCI）领域，新知识越来越多地以视频和演示等丰富形态呈现，但归档发表的研究论文依然是记录与传播研究知识最通用，也最受认可的载体。一篇研究论文所做出的知识贡献，或者更准确地说，论文所记述的那项工作所做出的知识贡献，是一切研究论文的核心所在。举几个例子，理论物理论文可能贡献一个刻画黑洞附近光线行为的新数学模型，土木工程论文可能贡献一种测试桥梁应力的新方法，社会人类学论文可能贡献一份记录偏远宗教社区如何看待少女怀孕的田野考察。无论探究哪个领域，关注什么现象，每一篇研究论文都在力图提供新知识，以此做出研究贡献。为了把这种知识与日常的经验诀窍区分开，有些学者甚至把首字母大写，专称为“Knowledge”。放眼整个人类的求知活动，具体的研究贡献自然数不胜数，但这些贡献所属的类型，也就是新知识呈现出来的基本形态，其实屈指可数。开篇的三个例子就分属三种不同的贡献类型：第一个是理论贡献，第二个是方法论贡献，第三个是实证贡献。三者之所以不同，是因为它们带来的知识源于三种不同的“求知之道”（ways of knowing）[1]。人们常说 HCI 高度跨学科，而且还很年轻。HCI 里的知识类型花了几十年才逐渐浮现，收束并稳定下来，至今仍有新的求知方式在不断涌动[2]。不过随着时间推移，一套清晰可辨的研究贡献类型已经演化成形。这套分类近期经过重新提炼，正式用在了 HCI 旗舰会议 ACM CHI 2016 的投稿流程里。在本文中，我们将梳理并概括 HCI 的研究贡献类型，试着为我们这个领域以及它所生产的知识勾勒出清晰的轮廓。我们还会回顾不同贡献类型是如何在 CHI 投稿流程中一步步固定下来的，并展示 CHI 2016 的投稿与录用论文在各个贡献类型上的分布，借此看清 HCI 领域究竟在生产和传播怎样的知识。
:::

:::annotation

把“工程工作量”直接当成“学术贡献”，是写系统或应用型论文时最常见的失焦。很多人在写完一个庞大项目后，开口只能说出“我搭了一套系统”，却答不出它究竟沉淀出了哪一类可迁移的知识。Wobbrock 和 Kientz 在开篇点破了一件要害事：论文只是载体，背后的工作究竟通过哪条“求知之道”刷新了共同体的认知，才决定了它的立身之本。
:::

## 核心提要（Insights）

:::bi

- Knowledge generated by HCI research can be categorized into certain contribution types.
- Each contribution type has key characteristics that imply how it is judged.
- The contribution types used for submissions to the CHI conference have evolved over time to distill types of knowledge from other concerns.

:::zh

- HCI 研究产出的知识可以归入几种明确的贡献类型。
- 每种贡献类型都有各自的核心特征，这些特征直接决定了审稿人用什么标准评判它。
- CHI 会议投稿所用的贡献分类历经多年演变，才把纯粹的“知识类型”从具体的工作形式与研究议题中剥离出来。
:::

## HCI 的七种研究贡献类型（Seven Research Contribution Types in HCI）

:::bi

In 2012, the first author posted an unpublished whitepaper to his academic website describing seven research contribution types in HCI [3]. This whitepaper was used as a source in creating the contribution types for the new CHI keyword structure. The whitepaper presented seven research contribution types, how they are judged, and specific examples of each. Here we summarize those contribution types, with examples.

:::zh

2012 年，本文第一作者在个人学术主页上放过一份未正式发表的白皮书，梳理了 HCI 的七种研究贡献类型[3]。后来 CHI 重构投稿关键词体系时，就以这份白皮书为蓝本设计了贡献分类。白皮书列出了七种贡献类型，各自的评判标准以及代表性案例。下面我们就结合具体论文，逐一概述这七类贡献。
:::

### 实证研究贡献（Empirical Research Contributions）

:::bi

Empirical research contributions are the backbone of science. They provide new knowledge through findings based on observation and data-gathering. Data may be qualitative or quantitative, aspiringly objective or unapologetically subjective, from the laboratory or from the field. In HCI, empirical contributions arise from a variety of sources, including experiments, user tests, field observations, interviews, surveys, focus groups, diaries, ethnographies, sensors, log files, and many others. Empirical research contributions are evaluated mainly on the importance of their findings and on the soundness of their methods. If empirical findings are uninteresting or unimportant, or if the methods by which those findings arise are sloppy, imprecise, or confounded, then empirical contributions are judged unfavorably.

:::zh

实证研究贡献是科学的脊梁。它以观察和数据收集为基础，靠研究发现来提供新知识。数据既可以是定性的，也可以是定量的，可以力求客观，也可以坦率地呈现主观视角，可以采自受控的实验室，也可以来自真实的田野现场。在 HCI 中，实证贡献的来源五花八门，包括受控实验，用户测试，实地观察，访谈，问卷，焦点小组，日记研究，民族志，传感器记录，系统日志等等。评审实证研究贡献时，主要看两条：发现本身够不够重要，研究方法站不站得住脚。要是得出的发现平淡无奇，无关痛痒，或者获取发现的过程粗糙失准，混入了干扰变量（confounded），这类实证工作就很难过关。
:::

:::annotation

实证论文的死穴通常有两个：要么方法严丝合缝，得出的结论却是人尽皆知的常识（发现不重要），要么问题极有噱头，实验设计却漏掉了关键的混淆变量（方法不扎实）。落到写作上，引言得负责讲清楚“为什么这个发现能改变大家的认知”，方法节则必须把控制变量，样本局限和复现边界摊在桌面上。
:::

### 制品贡献（Artifact Contributions）

:::bi

HCI is driven by the creation and realization of interactive artifacts. Whereas empirical contributions arise from descriptive discovery-driven activities (science), artifact contributions arise from generative design-driven activities (invention). Artifacts, often prototypes, include new systems, architectures, tools, toolkits, techniques, sketches, mockups, and envisionments that reveal new possibilities, enable new explorations, facilitate new insights, or compel us to consider new possible futures. New knowledge is embedded in and manifested by artifacts and the supporting materials that describe them. Artifact research contributions are evaluated according to the type of artifact that gave rise to them. They are often accompanied by empirical studies but do not have to be, and sometimes should not be [4]. New systems, architectures, tools, and toolkits are evaluated in a holistic fashion according to what they make possible and how they do so [5]. New input and interaction techniques, by contrast, are evaluated precisely and quantitatively so as to isolate their human performance benefits. New design expressions, including sketches, mockups, and envisionments, are evaluated by how insightful, compelling, and innovative is their portrayal. Of particular importance is how well designs negotiate trade-offs and hold competing priorities in balance.

:::zh

HCI 由交互制品的创造与实现驱动。实证贡献来自描述性的，以发现为驱动的活动，也就是科学，制品贡献则来自生成性的，以设计为驱动的活动，也就是发明。制品通常是原型，包括新系统，架构，工具，工具包，技术，草图，样机和愿景，它们揭示新的可能，开启新的探索，促成新的洞见，或者迫使我们去考虑新的未来。新知识嵌入并体现在制品以及描述它的支撑材料之中。制品研究贡献按产生它们的制品类型来评判。它们常常伴随实证研究，但并不必须如此，有时也不应该如此[4]。新系统，架构，工具和工具包按它们使什么成为可能，又如何做到这一点来整体评判[5]。相比之下，新的输入和交互技术会被精确，定量地评判，以便分离出它们在人的绩效上的收益。新的设计表达，包括草图，样机和愿景，则按表达的洞察力，说服力和创新性来评判。尤其重要的是，设计如何处理权衡，并在相互竞争的优先事项之间保持平衡。
:::

:::annotation

制品贡献最容易被工程背景的人误读成“做了个酷东西”。这一段其实在讲评价尺度：系统看它使什么成为可能，技术看量化绩效，设计表达看洞察力与说服力。写之前先选对尺子。
:::

### 方法论贡献（Methodological Contributions）

:::bi

Methodological research contributions create new knowledge that informs how we carry out our work. Such contributions may improve research or practice. They may influence how we do science or how we do design. They may improve how we discover things, measure things, analyze things, create things, or build things. Methodological research contributions are evaluated on the utility, reproducibility, reliability, and validity of the new method or method enhancement. Useful methods that can be reproduced and provide reliable, valid results are judged favorably. Knowing that a method has these properties requires repeated validation.

:::zh

方法论研究贡献创造的新知识，告诉我们该如何开展工作。这类贡献可以改进研究，也可以改进实践。它们可能影响我们怎么做科学，也可能影响我们怎么做设计。它们可能改进我们发现事物，测量事物，分析事物，创造事物或建造事物的方式。方法论研究贡献按新方法或方法改进的实用性，可复现性，可靠性和有效性来评判。有用，可复现，能给出可靠而有效结果的方法会得到正面评价。要确认一个方法具备这些性质，需要反复验证。
:::

### 理论贡献（Theoretical Contributions）

:::bi

Theoretical research contributions consist of new or improved concepts, definitions, models, principles, or frameworks. They are vehicles for thought. Whereas methodological contributions inform how we do things, theoretical contributions inform what we do, why we do it, and what we expect from it. Theories may be qualitative or quantitative. They may have both descriptive and predictive power—by distilling the essential features of a phenomenon, they are also able to suggest how that phenomenon will behave. Fully developed theories offer explanatory accounts, not simply observing that but explaining why. Theories should be testable and falsifiable; if they are not, they do not qualify as scientific theories [6].

:::zh

理论研究贡献由新的或改进过的概念，定义，模型，原则或框架构成。它们是思考的载体。方法论贡献告诉我们如何做事，理论贡献则告诉我们做什么，为什么做，以及对它该有什么期待。理论可以是定性的，也可以是定量的。理论可以同时具备描述力和预测力：通过提炼一个现象的本质特征，理论也能提示这个现象将如何表现。成熟的理论提供解释性叙述，不只是观察到“如此”，还要解释“为何如此”。理论应当可检验，可证伪。如果做不到这两点，就算不上科学理论[6]。
:::

:::bi

Theoretical research contributions are evaluated based on their novelty, soundness, and power to describe, predict, and explain. A theory that accounts well for observed data from a specific situation but has no ability to generalize to new situations is of limited use. Conversely, a theory that is so broad it can account for just about anything probably does not contain any true descriptive power. Theory validation is almost always achieved through empirical work.

:::zh

理论研究贡献按新颖性，可靠性，以及描述，预测和解释的能力来评判。一个理论如果只能很好地解释特定情境下观察到的数据，却无法推广到新情境，用处就有限。反过来，如果一个理论宽泛到几乎能解释一切，那它大概不具备什么真正的描述力。理论的验证几乎总是通过实证工作完成。
:::

:::annotation

理论贡献的评价标准是新颖性，可靠性和解释力。这里还有一个很实用的忠告：太窄的理论只能解释一个情境，太宽的理论什么都能解释，两者都不算好理论。写理论类论文时，讨论节要主动交代“这个理论不能解释什么”。
:::

### 数据集贡献（Dataset Contributions）

:::bi

A dataset contribution provides a new and useful corpus, often accompanied by an analysis of its characteristics, for the benefit of the research community.

:::zh

数据集贡献提供一个全新且有用的语料库，通常还附带一份对其特征的剖析，以惠及研究社区。
:::

:::bi

Benchmark tests may accompany datasets to standardize comparisons. Datasets enable evaluations of shared repositories by new algorithms, systems, or methods. Dataset research contributions are judged favorably by the extent to which they supply the research community with a useful and representative corpus against which to test and measure. Often, datasets are published with new tools that enable researchers to work with the new corpus. Dataset contributions often accompany methodological contributions where a new method is meant to exercise the dataset.

:::zh

基准测试可能随数据集一起发布，以便统一比较标准。数据集让新算法，新系统或新方法能够对共享的语料库进行评估。数据集研究贡献按它向研究社区提供了多大程度上有用且具代表性的语料库，可供测试和测量来评判。数据集常常和新工具一起发布，让研究者能用上新语料库。数据集贡献也常伴随方法论贡献出现，因为新方法往往是为了在这个数据集上练手。
:::

### 综述贡献（Survey Contributions）

:::bi

Survey research contributions and other meta-analyses review and synthesize work done on a research topic with the goal of exposing trends and gaps. Survey contributions are appropriate after a topic has reached a certain level of maturity. It is not uncommon for surveys to have references numbering in the hundreds. The journal ACM Computing Surveys is solely devoted to publishing survey contributions. In HCI, the journal Foundations and Trends in HCI regularly publishes survey contributions. Survey research contributions, and meta-analyses in general, are evaluated based on how well they organize what is currently known about a topic and reveal opportunities for further research. To be effective, survey contributions must not be mere laundry lists of prior work. Good surveys exhibit completeness, depth, maturity, and organization.

:::zh

综述研究贡献以及其他元分析，会回顾和综合某个研究课题上已完成的工作，目标是暴露趋势和空缺。综述贡献适合在一个课题已经达到一定成熟度之后出现。综述的参考文献动辄数百条，这并不罕见。期刊 ACM Computing Surveys 就专门发表综述贡献。在 HCI 领域，期刊 Foundations and Trends in HCI 定期发表综述贡献。综述研究贡献以及更一般的元分析，按它们对某个课题当前已知内容的整理程度，以及能否揭示后续研究机会来评判。要真正有效，综述贡献不能只是把先前工作列成流水账。好的综述表现出完整性，深度，成熟度和组织性。
:::

### 观点贡献（Opinion Contributions）

:::bi

Opinion research contributions, also called essays or arguments, seek to change the minds of readers through persuasion. Although the term opinion might suggest a less-than-scientific effort, in fact, opinion contributions draw upon many of the above contribution types to make their case. Opinion contributions are considered a separate research contribution type not because they lack a research basis, but because their goal is to persuade, not just inform. Along with persuasion, the goal of opinion contributions is to compel reflection, discussion, and debate. Opinion research contributions are evaluated on the strength of their argument. Strong arguments credibly use supporting evidence and fairly consider opposing perspectives. They focus on topics of wide interest and should be broadly accessible.

:::zh

观点研究贡献，也叫随笔或论证，力图通过说服改变读者的想法。虽然“观点”这个词听起来不那么科学，但事实上，观点贡献会调用上面许多贡献类型来支撑自己的主张。观点贡献之所以被视为一种独立的研究贡献类型，不是因为它缺少研究基础，而是因为它的目标是说服，不只是告知。除了说服，观点贡献还要推动反思，讨论和辩论。观点研究贡献按论证的力量来评判。有力的论证会可信地使用支持性证据，并公正地考虑对立观点。它们聚焦广泛关注的话题，也应当写得让广泛读者读得懂。
:::

## ACM CHI 会议里的贡献类型（Contribution Types at the ACM CHI Conference）

:::bi

The ACM CHI conference has increasingly come to consider research contribution types as part of its submission process. From 1996 to 2001, authors were asked to indicate a paper type, although it was never referred to as a “contribution.” Examples from 1996 included “experience papers” and “systems papers.” The year 1998 was the first that the call-for-papers (CfP) included the word contribution. By 2000, lengthy contribution descriptors appeared, such as “a thought-provoking, well-substantiated analysis of an HCI-related issue,” or “experience gained in adapting designs and applying other HCI contributions to real-world conditions, presented in the form of a design briefing or case history.” From 2002 to 2008, authors were asked to submit a 30-word freeform “contributions and benefits statement,” only for use in the program. From 2009 to 2013, authors were asked to choose from a list of contribution types, but from 2014 to 2015, contribution types were listed in the CfP but not selectable when submitting. In 2016, contribution types appeared as optional submission keywords devised by Frank Bentley and Jofish Kaye and influenced by Wobbrock’s whitepaper [3]. An important difference from Wobbrock’s original list was that the empirical contribution type was split in two, one for studies of systems and one for studies of people. Tables 1, 2, and 3 show how research contribution types were codified over the past decade of CHI.

:::zh

ACM CHI 会议越来越把研究贡献类型视为投稿流程的一部分。1996 年到 2001 年，作者被要求注明论文类型，尽管当时从未称其为“贡献”。1996 年的例子包括“经验论文”和“系统论文”。1998 年是征稿启事（CfP）第一次包含 contribution 这个词。到 2000 年，出现了很长的贡献描述，例如“对某个 HCI 相关议题做出有启发性，论证充分的分析”，或者“在把设计以及 HCI 的其他贡献应用到真实世界条件时获得的经验，以设计简报或案例史的形式呈现”。2002 年到 2008 年，作者被要求提交一段 30 词的自由格式“贡献与收益陈述”，仅用于程序手册。2009 年到 2013 年，作者需要从一份贡献类型清单中选择，但从 2014 年到 2015 年，贡献类型只列在 CfP 中，投稿时不再可选。2016 年，贡献类型作为可选的投稿关键词出现，由 Frank Bentley 和 Jofish Kaye 设计，并受到 Wobbrock 白皮书的影响[3]。与 Wobbrock 原始清单的一个重要区别是，实证贡献类型被一分为二，一个用于系统研究，一个用于人的研究。表 1，表 2 和表 3 展示了研究贡献类型在过去十年 CHI 中是如何被规范化下来的。

:::bi

The earlier CHI contribution types embodied a mix of concerns—not only types of knowledge, but also types of work, substantive concerns, and methodological approaches. Eventually, lengthy descriptors gave way to pithy terms focused on the types of knowledge created.

:::zh

早年 CHI 的贡献类型混杂了多种关切，不只是知识类型，还有工作类型，实质关切和方法论路径。最终，冗长的描述让位给简洁的术语，聚焦于所创造的知识类型。
:::

:::bi

The CHI 2016 submission process made it optional for authors to indicate their papers’ research contribution type. Of 2,316 submissions, only 130 opted not to indicate any contribution (5.6 percent); 1,481 indicated exactly one contribution (63.9 percent); 705 indicated more than one (30.4 percent). On average, a submission had 1.29 contribution types indicated (SD=0.66). Figure 1 shows how contribution types were presented to authors during the CHI 2016 submission process.

:::zh

CHI 2016 的投稿流程让作者可以自行选择是否标注论文的研究贡献类型。在 2,316 份投稿中，只有 130 份没有标注任何贡献（5.6%），1,481 份恰好标注了一种贡献（63.9%），705 份标注了不止一种（30.4%）。平均而言，一份投稿标注了 1.29 种贡献类型（SD=0.66）。图 1 展示了 CHI 2016 投稿流程中贡献类型如何呈现给作者。

:::bi

Of the 2,316 submissions to CHI 2016, nearly half (44.0 percent) were empirical studies of system use, and almost the same percentage of such papers made up the final program, with an acceptance rate (23.5 percent) almost identical to that of the overall conference (23.6 percent). Empirical studies of people also dominated submissions (28.4 percent) and had the best acceptance rate of any contribution type (26.3 percent). Artifact or system papers were also prevalent in submissions (24.2 percent) and had an acceptance rate (23.9 percent) similar to that of the overall conference.

:::zh

在 CHI 2016 的 2,316 份投稿中，将近一半（44.0%）是系统使用方面的实证研究，这类论文在最终程序中的占比几乎相同，接收率（23.5%）也与整体会议（23.6%）几乎一致。关于人的实证研究同样在投稿中占主导（28.4%），并拥有所有贡献类型中最高的接收率（26.3%）。制品或系统类论文在投稿中也很常见（24.2%），接收率（23.9%）接近整体会议。
:::

:::bi

In contrast, acceptance rates for the less prevalent contribution types—Method, Theory, Essay/Argument, Meta-Analysis/Literature Survey, and Dataset—were lower than the overall conference acceptance rate of 23.6 percent. Most were around 17 percent, with Dataset research contributions having the lowest acceptance rate (16.7 percent) as well as being the least submitted (1.3 percent). Essay/Argument contributions enjoyed the highest acceptance rate of this bunch (21.6 percent), yielding 19 accepted papers in the final program (3.5 percent).

:::zh

相比之下，较不常见贡献类型的接收率，包括方法，理论，随笔/论证，元分析/文献综述和数据集，都低于整体会议 23.6% 的接收率。多数在 17% 左右，其中数据集研究贡献的接收率最低（16.7%），投稿量也最少（1.3%）。随笔/论证贡献在这一组里接收率最高（21.6%），最终程序收录了 19 篇（3.5%）。
:::

:::bi

It is clear from Figure 2 that empirical studies and technology artifacts dominated the research activities of the CHI 2016 community, and that other research contribution types were not only less common, but also less likely to be accepted.

:::zh

从图 2 可以清楚看到，实证研究和技术制品主导了 CHI 2016 社区的研究活动，其他研究贡献类型不仅更少见，也更不容易被接收。
:::

:::bi

We stress that the level of research activity should not be confused with the value placed on particular contribution types once they are published. For example, Method and Theory contributions may be hard-won publications, but they may be highly cited and relied upon for years to come.

:::zh

我们强调，研究活动的多少不应与某类贡献类型发表之后所获得的价值混为一谈。例如，方法与理论贡献可能是千辛万苦才写成的论文，但它们也可能在多年里被大量引用和依赖。
:::

:::annotation

这一节值得当作史料读。CHI 的贡献类型从“经验论文”“系统论文”这类工作类型，逐渐演化成“实证”“制品”“方法论”这类知识类型，背后是整个领域对“什么算研究”的共识迁移。今天投稿系统里勾选的那几个框，就是这条演化线的终点。
:::

## 结论（Conclusion）

:::bi

As the HCI field matures, it is important to reflect on the knowledge it produces and the forms that knowledge takes. Such reflection can be challenging in a broad and diverse field, and while we should be wary of constraining our imaginations, we should embrace giving definition to the knowledge we produce. Doing so provides a valuable map for navigating the field of HCI and helps newcomers take their first steps. It is, after all, those newcomers who will discover new possibilities and push the frontiers of our knowledge even further.

:::zh

随着 HCI 领域逐渐成熟，反思它生产的知识以及知识呈现的形式很重要。在一个广阔而多样的领域里，这样的反思并不容易，虽然我们应当警惕限制自己的想象力，但也应当拥抱为所生产的知识给出定义。这样做能为在 HCI 领域里航行提供一张有价值的地图，也能帮助新来者迈出第一步。毕竟，正是这些新来者会发现新的可能，并把我们的知识边界推得更远。
:::
