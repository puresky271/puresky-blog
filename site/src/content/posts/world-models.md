## 摘要（Abstract）

:::bi
We explore building generative neural network models of popular reinforcement learning environments. Our world model can be trained quickly in an unsupervised manner to learn a compressed spatial and temporal representation of the environment. By using features extracted from the world model as inputs to an agent, we can train a very compact and simple policy that can solve the required task. We can even train our agent entirely inside of its own hallucinated dream generated by its world model, and transfer this policy back into the actual environment.

:::zh
我们探索为常见的强化学习环境构建生成式神经网络模型。这个“世界模型”可以用无监督的方式快速训练，学会环境在空间和时间上的压缩表征。把从世界模型里提取的特征当作智能体的输入，我们就能训练出一个非常紧凑，简单的策略来完成目标任务。我们甚至可以让智能体完全在自己世界模型生成的幻觉梦境里训练，再把策略迁移回真实环境。
:::

:::bi
An interactive version of this paper is available at [https://worldmodels.github.io](https://worldmodels.github.io).

:::zh
这篇论文有一个可交互的在线版本：[https://worldmodels.github.io](https://worldmodels.github.io)。
:::

:::annotation
World Models 发表于 2018 年。作者是 Google Brain 的 David Ha 和 NNAISENSE 的 Jürgen Schmidhuber。Schmidhuber 从上世纪 90 年代起就在做“让循环网络学会世界，再在内部世界里规划”的研究，这篇论文是把那一路想法压缩成一个能跑起来的最小系统。

论文的核心配方只有三件东西。V 是一个变分自编码器，把每帧画面压成小向量。M 是一个带混合密度输出的循环网络，负责在潜空间里预测下一帧。C 是一个线性控制器，只根据 V 和 M 给出的向量决定动作。V 和 M 学“世界怎么运转”，C 只学“怎么把事做成”，分工清楚，所以整篇读下来像是在看一套工程上的解耦。
:::

## 引言（Introduction）

:::bi
Humans develop a mental model of the world based on what they are able to perceive with their limited senses. The decisions and actions we make are based on this internal model. Jay Wright Forrester, the father of system dynamics, described a mental model as:

:::zh
人类会根据有限的感官所能感知到的东西，在头脑里建立起关于世界的心理模型。我们做出的决定和采取的行动，都建立在这个内部模型之上。系统动力学之父 Jay Wright Forrester 这样描述心理模型：
:::

:::bi
> The image of the world around us, which we carry in our head, is just a model. Nobody in his head imagines all the world, government or country. He has only selected concepts, and relationships between them, and uses those to represent the real system. (Forrester, 1971)

:::zh
> 我们头脑里关于周围世界的图景，本身只是一个模型。没有人在头脑里装下整个世界，政府或国家。他只有一些被选中的概念，以及概念之间的关系，并用它们来表示真实的系统。（[Forrester, 1971](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mental_model)）
:::

:::bi
To handle the vast amount of information that flows through our daily lives, our brain learns an abstract representation of both spatial and temporal aspects of this information. We are able to observe a scene and remember an abstract description thereof (Cheang & Tsao, 2017; Quiroga et al., 2005). Evidence also suggests that what we perceive at any given moment is governed by our brain's prediction of the future based on our internal model (Nortmann et al., 2015; Gerrit et al., 2013).

:::zh
为了处理日常生活中川流不息的大量信息，我们的大脑会学习这些信息在空间和时间两个方面的抽象表征。我们能够观察一个场景，并记住对它的抽象描述（Cheang & Tsao, 2017; Quiroga et al., 2005）。[证据](http://www.cell.com/cell/fulltext/S0092-8674%2817%2930538-X)也表明，我们在任何时刻感知到的东西，都由大脑基于内部模型对未来的预测所支配（Nortmann et al., 2015; Gerrit et al., 2013）。
:::

:::annotation
这段在给“感知即预测”的观点做铺垫。视觉神经科学里的预测编码（predictive coding）认为，大脑并不是被动接收像素，而是拿内部模型去猜下一帧，再把猜错的部分当作误差回传。论文后面让 M 网络预测下一帧的潜向量，正是把这个生物学直觉搬进了工程。
:::

:::bi
One way of understanding the predictive model inside of our brains is that it might not be about just predicting the future in general, but predicting future sensory data given our current motor actions (Keller et al., 2012; Leinweber et al., 2017). We are able to instinctively act on this predictive model and perform fast reflexive behaviours when we face danger (Mobbs et al., 2015), without the need to consciously plan out a course of action.

:::zh
一种理解大脑内部预测模型的方式是：它也许并不只是泛泛地预测未来，而是在给定当前运动动作的前提下预测未来的感觉数据（Keller et al., 2012; Leinweber et al., 2017）。面对危险时，我们可以本能地依据这个预测模型行动，做出快速的反射行为（Mobbs et al., 2015），而不需要有意识地规划一连串动作。
:::

:::figure
![Figure 1：骑车人幻想自己在骑车](./images/world-models/fig-01.png)
Figure 1. A World Model, from Scott McCloud's Understanding Comics. (McCloud, 1993; E, 2012)

:::zh
图 1. 一个世界模型，来自 Scott McCloud 的《Understanding Comics》。（McCloud, 1993; E, 2012）
:::
:::

:::bi
Take baseball for example. A batter has milliseconds to decide how they should swing the bat - shorter than the time it takes for visual signals to reach our brain. The reason we are able to hit a 100 mph fastball is due to our ability to instinctively predict when and where the ball will go. For professional players, this all happens subconsciously. Their muscles reflexively swing the bat at the right time and location in line with their internal models' predictions (Gerrit et al., 2013). They can quickly act on their predictions of the future without the need to consciously roll out possible future scenarios to form a plan (Hirshon, 2013).

:::zh
以棒球为例。击球手只有几毫秒时间决定该怎么挥棒，这比他看到视觉信号传进大脑所需的时间还要短。我们之所以能打中时速 100 英里的快球，是因为我们有能力本能地预测球会在何时飞向何处。对职业球员来说，这一切都发生在潜意识里。他们的肌肉会依照内部模型的预测，在正确的时间，正确的位置反射性地挥棒（Gerrit et al., 2013）。他们能迅速依据对未来的预测行动，而不需要有意识地铺开各种可能的未来场景来制定计划（Hirshon, 2013）。
:::

:::figure
![Figure 2：视错觉构成的动态边缘](./images/world-models/fig-02.png)
Figure 2. What we see is based on our brain's prediction of the future (Kitaoka, 2002; Watanabe et al., 2018).

:::zh
图 2. 我们看到的东西建立在大脑对未来的预测之上（Kitaoka, 2002; Watanabe et al., 2018）。
:::
:::

:::bi
In many reinforcement learning (RL) problems (Kaelbling et al., 1996; Sutton & Barto, 1998; Wiering & van Otterlo, 2012), an artificial agent also benefits from having a good representation of past and present states, and a good predictive model of the future (Werbos, 1987; Silver, 2017), preferably a powerful predictive model implemented on a general purpose computer such as a recurrent neural network (RNN) (Schmidhuber, 1990a;b; 1991a).

:::zh
在许多强化学习问题里（Kaelbling et al., 1996; Sutton & Barto, 1998; Wiering & van Otterlo, 2012），人工智能体同样受益于两点：对过去和现在状态的良好表征，以及对未来的良好预测模型（Werbos, 1987; Silver, 2017）。这个预测模型最好是跑在通用计算机上的强模型，比如循环神经网络（RNN）（Schmidhuber, 1990a;b; 1991a）。
:::

:::figure
![Figure 3：CarRacing 与 VizDoom 环境的截帧](./images/world-models/fig-03.png)
Figure 3. In this work, we build probabilistic generative models of OpenAI Gym environments. The RNN-based world models are trained using collected observations recorded from the actual game environment. After training the world models, we can use them mimic the complete environment and train agents using them.

:::zh
图 3. 在这项工作里，我们为 OpenAI Gym 的环境建立概率生成模型。基于 RNN 的世界模型用从真实游戏环境里采集到的观测来训练。训练好世界模型之后，我们可以用它模拟完整的环境，并在这个环境里训练智能体。
:::
:::

:::bi
Large RNNs are highly expressive models that can learn rich spatial and temporal representations of data. However, many model-free RL methods in the literature often only use small neural networks with few parameters. The RL algorithm is often bottlenecked by the credit assignment problem, which makes it hard for traditional RL algorithms to learn millions of weights of a large model, hence in practice, smaller networks are used as they iterate faster to a good policy during training.

:::zh
大型 RNN 是表达能力很强的模型，能学到数据里丰富的空间表征和时间表征。可是文献里的许多无模型强化学习方法往往只用参数很少的小网络。强化学习算法常常受困于信用分配（credit assignment）问题，传统算法很难学会大型模型里数以百万计的权重，所以实践中大家用的是小网络，因为小网络在训练中能更快迭代到好策略。
:::

:::annotation
信用分配说的是：一个动作做对或做错，奖励要归因到前面哪一步，哪个参数上。序列越长，信号越稀疏，这个问题越难。论文的解法不是硬啃，而是把任务拆开：让大模型去做无监督的“理解世界”，这不涉及奖励，只需要重建和预测。只把最后那层控制交给小模型，信用分配就变成了一个只有几百个参数的小搜索问题。
:::

:::bi
Ideally, we would like to be able to efficiently train large RNN-based agents. The backpropagation algorithm (Linnainmaa, 1970; Kelley, 1960; Werbos, 1982) can be used to train large neural networks efficiently. In this work we look at training a large neural network[^1] to tackle RL tasks, by dividing the agent into a large world model and a small controller model. We first train a large neural network to learn a model of the agent's world in an unsupervised manner, and then train the smaller controller model to learn to perform a task using this world model. A small controller lets the training algorithm focus on the credit assignment problem on a small search space, while not sacrificing capacity and expressiveness via the larger world model. By training the agent through the lens of its world model, we show that it can learn a highly compact policy to perform its task.

:::zh
理想情况下，我们希望高效地训练大型 RNN 智能体。反向传播算法（Linnainmaa, 1970; Kelley, 1960; Werbos, 1982）可以用来高效训练大型神经网络。在这项工作里，我们训练一个大神经网络[^1]来处理强化学习任务，做法是把智能体拆成一个大的世界模型和一个小的控制器模型。我们先以无监督的方式训练大网络，让它学会智能体所处世界的模型，再用这个世界模型训练较小的控制器去完成任务。控制器小，训练算法就能把信用分配问题集中到一个小搜索空间上，同时又不必牺牲大世界模型带来的容量和表达能力。让智能体透过世界模型这面透镜来训练，我们发现它能学到一个高度紧凑的策略。
:::

:::bi
Although there is a large body of research relating to model-based reinforcement learning, this article is not meant to be a review (Arulkumaran et al., 2017; Schmidhuber, 2015b) of the current state of the field. Instead, the goal of this article is to distill several key concepts from a series of papers 1990-2015 on combinations of RNN-based world models and controllers (Schmidhuber, 1990a;b; 1991a; 1990c; 2015a). We will also discuss other related works in the literature that share similar ideas of learning a world model and training an agent using this model.

:::zh
围绕基于模型的强化学习已经有大量研究，但这篇文章不是对领域现状的综述（Arulkumaran et al., 2017; Schmidhuber, 2015b）。它的目标是从 1990 到 2015 年间一系列讨论“RNN 世界模型加控制器”的论文里，提炼出几个关键概念（Schmidhuber, 1990a;b; 1991a; 1990c; 2015a）。我们也会讨论文献里其他共享类似想法的研究，也就是先学世界模型，再用这个世界模型训练智能体。
:::

:::bi
In this article, we present a simplified framework that we can use to experimentally demonstrate some of the key concepts from these papers, and also suggest further insights to effectively apply these ideas to various RL environments. We use similar terminology and notation as On Learning to Think: Algorithmic Information Theory for Novel Combinations of RL Controllers and RNN World Models (Schmidhuber, 2015a) when describing our methodology and experiments.

:::zh
在本文里，我们给出一个简化过的框架，用它通过实验演示这些论文里的部分关键概念，也就如何把这些想法有效地应用到各种强化学习环境给出一些进一步的见解。描述方法与实验时，我们沿用了《On Learning to Think: Algorithmic Information Theory for Novel Combinations of RL Controllers and RNN World Models》里的术语和记号（Schmidhuber, 2015a）。
:::

[^1]: <span class="bi-en" lang="en">Typical model-free RL models have in the order of 10^3 to 10^6 model parameters. We look at training models in the order of 10^7 parameters, which is still rather small compared to state-of-the-art deep learning models with 10^8 to even 10^9 parameters. In principle, the procedure described in this article can take advantage of these larger networks if we wanted to use them.</span><span class="bi-zh" lang="zh-CN">典型的无模型强化学习模型只有 10³ 到 10⁶ 个参数。我们在这里训练的是 10⁷ 量级的模型，和当时最先进的，有 10⁸ 甚至 10⁹ 参数的深度学习模型相比仍然很小。原则上，只要愿意，本文描述的过程也可以利用这些更大的网络。</span>

## 智能体模型（Agent Model）

:::bi
We present a simple model inspired by our own cognitive system. In this model, our agent has a visual sensory component that compresses what it sees into a small representative code. It also has a memory component that makes predictions about future codes based on historical information. Finally, our agent has a decision-making component that decides what actions to take based only on the representations created by its vision and memory components.

:::zh
我们提出一个受人类认知系统启发的简单模型。在这个模型里，智能体有一个视觉感官部件，它把看到的东西压缩成一个小的代表性编码。它还有一个记忆部件，根据历史信息预测未来的编码。最后，智能体有一个决策部件，只依据视觉部件和记忆部件产生的表征来决定采取什么动作。
:::

:::figure
![Figure 4：智能体的三个部件](./images/world-models/fig-04.png)
Figure 4. Our agent consists of three components that work closely together: Vision (V), Memory (M), and Controller (C)

:::zh
图 4. 我们的智能体由三个紧密协作的部件组成：视觉（V），记忆（M）和控制器（C）。
:::
:::

:::annotation
这张图值得多看一会儿。左边那列小字已经把数据流写清楚了：每一步，V 把高清画面压成 $z$，M 拿 $z$ 和历史隐状态 $h$ 算出下一时刻的 $h$，C 只看 $[z, h]$ 就决定动作。注意 V 和 M 是各自独立训练的，C 是后面单独进化的，三者并没有联合端到端训练。论文的这种“先造世界，再学行动”的顺序，正是它和很多端到端深度强化学习方法最大的区别。
:::

### 视觉模型 V（VAE）

:::bi
The environment provides our agent with a high dimensional input observation at each time step. This input is usually a 2D image frame that is part of a video sequence. The role of the V model is to learn an abstract, compressed representation of each observed input frame.

:::zh
环境在每一个时间步给智能体一个高维的输入观测。这个输入通常是视频序列里的一帧二维图像。V 模型的任务，是为观察到的每一帧学习一个抽象，压缩的表征。
:::

:::figure
![Figure 5：VAE 的流程图](./images/world-models/fig-05.png)
Figure 5. Flow diagram of a Variational Autoencoder (VAE).

:::zh
图 5. 变分自编码器（VAE）的流程图。
:::
:::

:::bi
Here, we use a simple Variational Autoencoder (Kingma & Welling, 2013; Rezende et al., 2014) as our V model to compress each image frame into a small latent vector $z$.

:::zh
这里我们用一个简单的变分自编码器（[Kingma & Welling, 2013](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.6114); [Rezende et al., 2014](https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.4082)）作为 V 模型，把每一帧图像压缩成一个小潜向量 $z$。
:::

:::annotation
VAE 可以理解成一对配合的压缩器和解压器。编码器把 64x64x3 的图像压成均值 $\mu$ 和标准差 $\sigma$，再从高斯分布里采样出 $z$。解码器只拿 $z$ 去重建原图。训练目标是重建误差加一项 KL 正则，后者把 $z$ 的分布往标准高斯上推。这一项很关键：它限制了 $z$ 能携带的信息量，也让 M 网络预测出来的 $z$ 不会太离谱，因为潜空间被规整成了一个形状规整的高斯球。
:::

### 记忆模型 M（MDN-RNN）

:::bi
While it is the role of the V model to compress what the agent sees at each time frame, we also want to compress what happens over time. For this purpose, the role of the M model is to predict the future. The M model serves as a predictive model of the future $z$ vectors that V is expected to produce. Since many complex environments are stochastic in nature, we train our RNN to output a probability density function $p(z)$ instead of a deterministic prediction of $z$.

:::zh
V 模型负责压缩智能体在每一时刻看到的东西，我们还想压缩随时间发生的事情。为此，M 模型的任务是预测未来。M 是 V 将会产生的那些未来 $z$ 向量的预测模型。由于许多复杂环境本质上是随机的，我们训练 RNN 输出概率密度函数 $p(z)$，而不是对 $z$ 做确定性的预测。
:::

:::figure
![Figure 6：带混合密度网络输出层的 RNN](./images/world-models/fig-06.png)
Figure 6. RNN with a Mixture Density Network output layer. The MDN outputs the parameters of a mixture of Gaussian distribution used to sample a prediction of the next latent vector z.

:::zh
图 6. 带混合密度网络输出层的 RNN。MDN 输出高斯混合分布的参数，用来采样下一个潜向量 z 的预测值。
:::
:::

:::bi
In our approach, we approximate $p(z)$ as a mixture of Gaussian distribution, and train the RNN to output the probability distribution of the next latent vector $z_{t+1}$ given the current and past information made available to it.

:::zh
在我们的做法里，我们把 $p(z)$ 近似成高斯混合分布，训练 RNN 输出下一个潜向量 $z_{t+1}$ 的概率分布，条件是其能获得的当前信息和历史信息。
:::

:::bi
More specifically, the RNN will model $P(z_{t+1} \mid a_t, z_t, h_t)$, where $a_t$ is the action taken at time $t$ and $h_t$ is the hidden state of the RNN at time $t$. During sampling, we can adjust a temperature parameter $\tau$ to control model uncertainty, as done in (Ha & Eck, 2017) - we will find adjusting $\tau$ to be useful for training our controller later on.

:::zh
更具体地说，RNN 建模的是 $P(z_{t+1} \mid a_t, z_t, h_t)$，其中 $a_t$ 是在时刻 $t$ 采取的动作，$h_t$ 是 RNN 在时刻 $t$ 的隐状态。采样时我们可以调整温度参数 $\tau$ 来控制模型的不确定性，做法与（[Ha & Eck, 2017](https://magenta.tensorflow.org/sketch-rnn-demo)）相同。后面我们会看到，调整 $\tau$ 对训练控制器很有用。
:::

:::bi
This approach is known as a Mixture Density Network (Bishop, 1994) combined with a RNN (MDN-RNN) (Graves, 2013; Ha, 2017a), and has been applied in the past for sequence generation problems such as generating handwriting (Graves, 2013) and sketches (Ha & Eck, 2017).

:::zh
这种做法叫作混合密度网络（[Bishop, 1994](http://publications.aston.ac.uk/373/)）与 RNN 的结合（MDN-RNN）（[Graves, 2013](https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.0850); [Ha, 2017a](http://blog.otoro.net/2017/01/01/recurrent-neural-network-artist/)），过去常用于序列生成问题，比如生成手写笔迹（Graves, 2013）和简笔画（Ha & Eck, 2017）。
:::

:::figure
![Figure 7：SketchRNN 预测下一笔画](./images/world-models/fig-07.png)
Figure 7. SketchRNN (Ha & Eck, 2017) is an example of a MDN-RNN used to predict the next pen strokes of a sketch drawing. We use a similar model to predict the next latent vector z_t.

:::zh
图 7. SketchRNN（Ha & Eck, 2017）是一个用 MDN-RNN 预测简笔画下一笔的示例。我们用类似的模型预测下一个潜向量 z_t。
:::
:::

:::annotation
为什么不用一个确定的预测，偏要输出分布？因为环境本身有随机性，比如 CarRacing 里赛道随机生成，Doom 里怪物何时开火也不确定。模型如果只学会一条“平均未来”，在真实环境里一旦遇到分叉就会失准。输出高斯混合还能让模型表达离散的模式切换：怪物开火或者不开火，对应的是混合分布里不同的分量。后面 VizDoom 那一节会看到，温度 $\tau$ 调得太低会让模型塌缩到单一模式，怪物永远不开火，智能体在梦里天下无敌，回到现实立刻露馅。
:::

### 控制器模型 C

:::bi
The Controller (C) model is responsible for determining the course of actions to take in order to maximize the expected cumulative reward of the agent during a rollout of the environment. In our experiments, we deliberately make C as simple and small as possible, and trained separately from V and M, so that most of our agent's complexity resides in the world model (V and M).

:::zh
控制器（C）模型负责决定采取哪一连串动作，以最大化智能体在一次环境展开（rollout）中的期望累计奖励。在实验里，我们刻意把 C 做得尽可能简单，尽可能小，并且让它与 V 和 M 分开训练，这样智能体的大部分复杂性都留在世界模型（V 和 M）里。
:::

:::bi
C is a simple single layer linear model that maps $z_t$ and $h_t$ directly to action $a_t$ at each time step:

:::zh
C 是一个简单的单层线性模型，在每个时间步直接把 $z_t$ 和 $h_t$ 映射成动作 $a_t$：
:::

:::bi


$$
a_t = W_c \begin{bmatrix} z_t \\ h_t \end{bmatrix} + b_c \tag{1}
$$



:::zh


$$
a_t = W_c \begin{bmatrix} z_t \\ h_t \end{bmatrix} + b_c \tag{1}
$$


:::

:::bi
In this linear model, $W_c$ and $b_c$ are the weight matrix and bias vector that maps the concatenated input vector $[z_t \ h_t]$ to the output action vector $a_t$.

:::zh
在这个线性模型里，$W_c$ 和 $b_c$ 分别是权重矩阵和偏置向量，它们把拼接起来的输入向量 $[z_t \ h_t]$ 映射成输出的动作向量 $a_t$。
:::

### 把三个部件装在一起

:::bi
The following flow diagram illustrates how V, M, and C interacts with the environment:

:::zh
下面的流程图展示了三个部件如何与环境交互：
:::

:::figure
![Figure 8：智能体模型的流程图](./images/world-models/fig-08.png)
Figure 8. Flow diagram of our Agent model. The raw observation is first processed by V at each time step t to produce z_t. The input into C is this latent vector z_t concatenated with M's hidden state h_t at each time step. C will then output an action vector a_t for motor control, and will affect the environment. M will then take the current z_t and action a_t as an input to update its own hidden state to produce h_t+1 to be used at time t + 1.

:::zh
图 8. 智能体模型的流程图。原始观测在每个时间步 t 先由 V 处理，得到 z_t。C 的输入是潜向量 z_t 与 M 在该时间步的隐状态 h_t 拼接起来的结果。C 接着输出用于运动控制的动作向量 a_t，并改变环境。M 再把当前的 z_t 和动作 a_t 当作输入，更新自己的隐状态，产生 h_t+1 供时间步 t+1 使用。
:::
:::

:::bi
Below is the pseudocode for how our agent model is used in the OpenAI Gym (Brockman et al., 2016) environment:

:::zh
下面是在 OpenAI Gym（[Brockman et al., 2016](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.01540)）环境里使用智能体模型的伪代码：
:::

```python
def rollout(controller):
    ''' env, rnn, vae are '''
    ''' global variables '''
    obs = env.reset()
    h = rnn.initial_state()
    done = False
    cumulative_reward = 0
    while not done:
        z = vae.encode(obs)
        a = controller.action([z, h])
        obs, reward, done = env.step(a)
        cumulative_reward += reward
        h = rnn.forward([a, z, h])
    return cumulative_reward
```

:::bi
Running this function on a given controller C will return the cumulative reward during a rollout.

:::zh
在给定控制器 C 上运行这个函数，就会返回一次 rollout 过程中的累计奖励。
:::

:::bi
This minimal design for C also offers important practical benefits. Advances in deep learning provided us with the tools to train large, sophisticated models efficiently, provided we can define a well-behaved, differentiable loss function. Our V and M models are designed to be trained efficiently with the backpropagation algorithm using modern GPU accelerators, so we would like most of the model's complexity, and model parameters to reside in V and M. The number of parameters of C, a linear model, is minimal in comparison. This choice allows us to explore more unconventional ways to train C - for example, even using evolution strategies (ES) (Rechenberg, 1973; Schwefel, 1977) to tackle more challenging RL tasks where the credit assignment problem is difficult.

:::zh
C 这样极简的设计也带来重要的实际好处。深度学习的进展给了我们工具，只要定义一个性质良好，可微的损失函数，就能高效训练大型复杂模型。我们的 V 和 M 正是为用反向传播算法在现代 GPU 上高效训练而设计的，所以我们希望模型的大部分复杂性和参数都放在 V 和 M 里。相比之下，线性模型 C 的参数数量少到可以忽略。这个选择让我们可以尝试更非常规的方式训练 C，比如用进化策略（ES）（[Rechenberg, 1973](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ingo_Rechenberg); [Schwefel, 1977](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans-Paul_Schwefel)）来处理信用分配问题更棘手的强化学习任务。
:::

:::bi
To optimize the parameters of C, we chose the Covariance-Matrix Adaptation Evolution Strategy (CMA-ES) (Hansen, 2016; Hansen & Ostermeier, 2001) as our optimization algorithm since it is known to work well for solution spaces of up to a few thousand parameters. We evolve parameters of C on a single machine with multiple CPU cores running multiple rollouts of the environment in parallel.

:::zh
为了优化 C 的参数，我们选择协方差矩阵自适应进化策略（CMA-ES）（[Hansen, 2016](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.00772); [Hansen & Ostermeier, 2001](http://www.cmap.polytechnique.fr/~nikolaus.hansen/cmaartic.pdf)）作为优化算法，因为它在最多几千个参数的解空间里表现良好。我们在一台多核 CPU 机器上并行地跑多个环境 rollout，用它们来进化 C 的参数。
:::

:::annotation
CMA-ES 是黑箱优化器，不需要对参数求梯度，只要给它一个标量适应度，也就是一次 rollout 的累计奖励。这对 C 很合适，因为它只有几百到一千多个参数，而奖励信号又只在 rollout 结束时才拿得到。换个角度想，V 和 M 用梯度做“精打细算”，C 用进化做“广撒网”，两套优化器各管自己擅长的部分，这也是论文结构上很耐看的一点。
:::

:::bi
For more specific information about the models, training procedures, and environments used in our experiments, please refer to the Appendix section.

:::zh
关于实验里使用的模型，训练过程和环境的更具体信息，请参阅附录部分。
:::

## 赛车实验（Car Racing Experiment）

:::bi
In this section, we describe how we can train the Agent model described earlier to solve a car racing task. To our knowledge, our agent is the first known solution to achieve the score required to solve this task.[^2]

:::zh
这一节描述如何训练前面讲过的智能体模型来完成赛车任务。据我们所知，我们的智能体是第一个达到该任务通关分数要求的已知方案。[^2]
:::

:::annotation
CarRacing-v0 是 OpenAI Gym 里一个从像素输入直接控制赛车的环境。赛道每次随机生成，智能体要尽量多地访问赛道格子，同时尽量快，三个连续动作分别是方向盘，油门和刹车。这个环境难在输入是原始像素，而奖励又很稀疏，状态和动作之间的因果链条很长。论文用 100 次连续试验的平均得分是否超过 900 作为“通关”标准，也就是说智能体几乎没有犯错的空间。
:::

### 用世界模型提取特征

:::bi
A predictive world model can help us extract useful representations of space and time. By using these features as inputs of a controller, we can train a compact and minimal controller to perform a continuous control task, such as learning to drive from pixel inputs for a top-down car racing environment called CarRacing-v0 (Klimov, 2016).

:::zh
一个预测性的世界模型能帮我们提取有用的空间表征和时间表征。把这些特征作为控制器的输入，我们就能训练一个紧凑，极简的控制器来完成连续控制任务，例如在名为 CarRacing-v0 的俯视赛车环境里，从像素输入学会驾驶（[Klimov, 2016](https://gym.openai.com/envs/CarRacing-v0/)）。
:::

:::figure
![Figure 9：智能体在 CarRacing-v0 里学习导航](./images/world-models/fig-09.png)
Figure 9. Our agent learning to navigate in CarRacing-v0.

:::zh
图 9. 我们的智能体在 CarRacing-v0 里学习导航。
:::
:::

:::bi
In this environment, the tracks are randomly generated for each trial, and our agent is rewarded for visiting as many tiles as possible in the least amount of time. The agent controls three continuous actions: steering left/right, acceleration, and brake.

:::zh
在这个环境里，每次试验的赛道都是随机生成的，智能体因在最短时间内访问尽可能多的格子而获得奖励。智能体控制三个连续动作：左右转向，加速和刹车。
:::

:::bi
To train our V model, we first collect a dataset of 10,000 random rollouts of the environment. We have first an agent acting randomly to explore the environment multiple times, and record the random actions $a_t$ taken and the resulting observations from the environment. We use this dataset to train V to learn a latent space of each frame observed. We train our VAE to encode each frame into low dimensional latent vector $z$ by minimizing the difference between a given frame and the reconstructed version of the frame produced by the decoder from $z$.

:::zh
为了训练 V 模型，我们先收集 10,000 次随机 rollout 的数据集。做法是让一个智能体随机行动，多次探索环境，记录下采取过的随机动作 $a_t$ 以及环境随之返回的观测。我们用这个数据集训练 V，让它学会观察到每一帧的潜空间。训练 VAE 时，我们让编码器把每一帧编码成低维潜向量 $z$，再让解码器从 $z$ 重建这一帧，通过最小化原帧与重建帧之间的差异来学习。
:::

:::bi
We can now use our trained V model to pre-process each frame at time $t$ into $z_t$ to train our M model. Using this pre-processed data, along with the recorded random actions $a_t$ taken, our MDN-RNN can now be trained to model $P(z_{t+1} \mid a_t, z_t, h_t)$ as a mixture of Gaussians.[^3]

:::zh
现在可以用训练好的 V 模型把时刻 $t$ 的每一帧预处理成 $z_t$，用来训练 M 模型。利用这些预处理后的数据，再加上记录下来的随机动作 $a_t$，我们的 MDN-RNN 就可以学着把 $P(z_{t+1} \mid a_t, z_t, h_t)$ 建模成高斯混合分布。[^3]
:::

:::annotation
注意这两步的分工。V 先独立学出一个潜空间，M 在这个潜空间里学动力学。V 训练时只看重建误差，完全不知道奖励长什么样。M 训练时也只看 `(动作, z, h) -> 下一个 z` 的预测误差。奖励信号直到最后训练 C 的时候才出现。论文把这件事讲得很直白：世界模型对奖励一无所知，它只负责把世界压缩好，预测好。
:::

:::bi
In this experiment, the world model (V and M) has no knowledge about the actual reward signals from the environment. Its task is simply to compress and predict the sequence of image frames observed. Only the Controller (C) Model has access to the reward information from the environment.

:::zh
在这个实验里，世界模型（V 和 M）对来自环境的真实奖励信号一无所知。它的任务只是压缩并预测观察到的图像帧序列。只有控制器（C）模型能接触环境里的奖励信息。
:::

:::bi
Since there are a mere 867 parameters inside the linear controller model, evolutionary algorithms such as CMA-ES are well suited for this optimization task.

:::zh
线性控制器模型里只有区区 867 个参数，所以像 CMA-ES 这样的进化算法非常适合这项优化任务。
:::

:::bi
We can use the VAE to reconstruct each frame using $z_t$ at each time step to visualize the quality of the information the agent actually sees during a rollout. The figure below is a VAE model trained on screenshots from CarRacing-v0.

:::zh
在 rollout 过程中，我们可以用 VAE 在每一个时间步用 $z_t$ 重建每一帧，以此观察智能体实际看到的信息质量如何。下面这张图里的 VAE，就是用 CarRacing-v0 的截图训练出来的。
:::

:::figure
![Figure 10：原始画面与 VAE 重建画面](./images/world-models/fig-10.png)
Figure 10. Despite losing details during this lossy compression process, latent vector z captures the essence of each image frame.

:::zh
图 10. 尽管在有损压缩过程中丢掉了细节，潜向量 z 仍然抓住了每一帧画面的要点。
:::
:::

:::bi
In the online version of this article, one can load randomly chosen screenshots to be encoded into a small latent vector $z$, which is used to reconstruct the original screenshot. One can also experiment with adjusting the values of the $z$ vector using the slider bars to see how it affects the reconstruction, or randomize $z$ to observe the space of possible screenshots.

:::zh
在本文的在线版本里，读者可以加载随机挑选的截图，把它编码成一个小潜向量 $z$，再用它重建原始截图。读者还可以用滑块调整 $z$ 向量各维的数值，看看重建结果会怎样变化，或者把 $z$ 随机化，观察可能截图构成的空间。

:::annotation
32 维的 $z$ 能保留赛道形状和车的位置，但会丢掉路面的纹理和细小色块。论文在讨论那一节也承认了这个取舍：无监督的 VAE 并不知道哪些细节对任务有用，比如它在 Doom 里认真重建了墙砖纹理，却漏掉了 CarRacing 赛道上与任务相关的路面格子。这个观察很诚实，也点出了“世界模型应该按任务需要来压缩什么”这个后来被反复讨论的问题。
:::

### 流程

:::bi
To summarize the Car Racing experiment, below are the steps taken:

:::zh
总结一下赛车实验，步骤如下：
:::

:::bi
1. Collect 10,000 rollouts from a random policy.
2. Train VAE (V) to encode frames into $z \in \mathbb{R}^{32}$.
3. Train MDN-RNN (M) to model $P(z_{t+1} \mid a_t, z_t, h_t)$.
4. Define Controller (C) as $a_t = W_c [z_t \ h_t] + b_c$.
5. Use CMA-ES to solve for a $W_c$ and $b_c$ that maximizes the expected cumulative reward.

:::zh
1. 用随机策略收集 10,000 次 rollout。
2. 训练 VAE（V），把每一帧编码成 $z \in \mathbb{R}^{32}$。
3. 训练 MDN-RNN（M），对 $P(z_{t+1} \mid a_t, z_t, h_t)$ 建模。
4. 定义控制器（C）为 $a_t = W_c [z_t \ h_t] + b_c$。
5. 用 CMA-ES 求解使期望累计奖励最大的 $W_c$ 和 $b_c$。
:::

### 实验结果

#### 只用 V 模型

:::bi
Training an agent to drive is not a difficult task if we have a good representation of the observation. Previous works (Hnermann, 2017; Bling, 2015; Lau, 2016) have shown that with a good set of hand-engineered information about the observation, such as LIDAR information, angles, positions and velocities, one can easily train a small feed-forward network to take this hand-engineered input and output a satisfactory navigation policy. For this reason, we first want to test our agent by handicapping C to only have access to V but not M, so we define our controller as $a_t = W_c z_t + b_c$.

:::zh
只要对观测有好的表征，训练智能体开车并不难。此前的工作（[Hnermann, 2017](http://janhuenermann.com/projects/learning-to-drive); [Bling, 2015](https://youtu.be/S9Y_I9vY8Qw); [Lau, 2016](https://yanpanlau.github.io/2016/10/11/Torcs-Keras.html)）表明，只要拿到一组关于观测的，手工设计的良好信息，比如 LIDAR 读数，角度，位置和速度，就可以轻松训练一个小型前馈网络吃下这些特征，再输出一个令人满意的导航策略。出于这个原因，我们想先削弱 C，让它只能看见 V 而看不到 M，以此测试智能体，于是把控制器定义为 $a_t = W_c z_t + b_c$。
:::

:::figure
![Figure 11：只看 z_t 时画圈不稳的行驶画面](./images/world-models/fig-11.png)
Figure 11. Limiting our controller to see only zt, but not ht results in wobbly and unstable driving behaviours.

:::zh
图 11. 只允许控制器看到 z_t 而看不到 h_t，会导致画圈，不稳定的驾驶行为。
:::
:::

:::bi
Although the agent is still able to navigate the race track in this setting, we notice it wobbles around and misses the tracks on sharper corners. This handicapped agent achieved an average score of 632 ± 251 over 100 random trials, in line with the performance of other agents on OpenAI Gym's leaderboard (Klimov, 2016) and traditional Deep RL methods such as A3C (Khan & Elibol, 2016; Jang et al., 2017). Adding a hidden layer to C's policy network helps to improve the results to 788 ± 141, but not quite enough to solve this environment.

:::zh
在这种设置下，智能体仍然能在赛道上跑起来，但我们注意到它会左右摇摆，在更急的弯道冲出去。这个被削弱过的智能体在 100 次随机试验里平均得分 632 ± 251，和 OpenAI Gym 排行榜上其他智能体（Klimov, 2016）以及 A3C 这类传统深度强化学习方法（Khan & Elibol, 2016; Jang et al., 2017）的表现大致相当。给 C 的策略网络加一层隐藏层，可以把结果提升到 788 ± 141，但还不足以通关。
:::

#### 完整世界模型（V 和 M）

:::bi
The representation $z_t$ provided by our V model only captures a representation at a moment in time and does not have much predictive power. In contrast, M is trained to do one thing, and to do it really well, which is to predict $z_{t+1}$. Since M's prediction of $z_{t+1}$ is produced from the RNN's hidden state $h_t$ at time $t$, this vector is a good candidate for the set of learned features we can give to our agent. Combining $z_t$ with $h_t$ gives our controller C a good representation of both the current observation, and what to expect in the future.

:::zh
V 模型给出的表征 $z_t$ 只捕捉某一瞬间的状态，预测能力有限。相比之下，M 只被训练来做一件事，而且要做到很好，那就是预测 $z_{t+1}$。既然 M 对 $z_{t+1}$ 的预测是由 RNN 在时刻 $t$ 的隐状态 $h_t$ 产生的，那么 $h_t$ 就是一个很好的候选特征，可以一并交给智能体。把 $z_t$ 和 $h_t$ 合在一起，控制器 C 就同时拥有了对当前观测的表征和对未来的预期。
:::

:::figure
![Figure 12：同时给出 z_t 与 h_t 后行驶更稳](./images/world-models/fig-12.png)
Figure 12. Driving is more stable if we give our controller access to both zt and ht.

:::zh
图 12. 如果我们让控制器同时拿到 z_t 和 h_t，驾驶会更稳定。
:::
:::

:::bi
We see that allowing the agent to access the both $z_t$ and $h_t$ greatly improves its driving capability. The driving is more stable, and the agent is able to seemingly attack the sharp corners effectively. Furthermore, we see that in making these fast reflexive driving decisions during a car race, the agent does not need to plan ahead and roll out hypothetical scenarios of the future. Since $h_t$ contain information about the probability distribution of the future, the agent can just query the RNN instinctively to guide its action decisions. Like a seasoned Formula One driver or the baseball player discussed earlier, the agent can instinctively predict when and where to navigate in the heat of the moment.

:::zh
可以看到，让智能体同时访问 $z_t$ 和 $h_t$ 极大地提升了它的驾驶能力。行驶更稳定，智能体看起来还能有效地攻下急弯。更进一步，我们发现它在赛车中做出这些快速，反射式的驾驶决策时，并不需要提前规划，也不需要铺开假设的未来场景。因为 $h_t$ 里含有关于未来概率分布的信息，智能体只要本能地查询 RNN，就能指导自己的动作决策。就像经验丰富的一级方程式车手，或者前面讨论过的棒球击球手一样，智能体能在电光石火的瞬间本能地判断该在何时，向何处转向。
:::

:::annotation
这段是论文的中心论点之一。$h_t$ 不是某一帧的压缩，而是“未来可能怎样”的压缩。C 拿到 $h_t$，等于拿到了一张关于接下来几帧的期望地图。回头看引言里“击球手不需要跑一遍未来场景也能挥棒”的比喻，这里在工程上把它兑现了：一次线性映射加一个隐状态，就足够支撑快速反射式的决策。
:::

## VizDoom 实验（VizDoom Experiment）

### 在梦里学习（Learning Inside of a Dream）

:::bi
We have just seen that a policy learned inside of the real environment appears to somewhat function inside of the dream environment. This begs the question - can we train our agent to learn inside of its own dream, and transfer this policy back to the actual environment?

:::zh
我们刚刚看到，在真实环境里学到的策略，放到梦境环境里似乎也能大致运转。这就引出一个问题：我们能不能干脆让智能体在自己的梦里学习，再把这个策略迁移回真实环境？
:::

:::bi
If our world model is sufficiently accurate for its purpose, and complete enough for the problem at hand, we should be able to substitute the actual environment with this world model. After all, our agent does not directly observe the reality, but only sees what the world model lets it see. In this experiment, we train an agent inside the hallucination generated by its world model trained to mimic a VizDoom (Kempka et al., 2016) environment.

:::zh
如果我们的世界模型对它的用途来说足够准确，对当前问题来说足够完整，我们应该就能用这个世界模型替代真实环境。毕竟智能体并不直接观察现实，它只能看到世界模型让它看到的东西。在这个实验里，我们让智能体在世界模型生成的幻觉里训练，而这个模型模拟的是 VizDoom 环境（[Kempka et al., 2016](http://arxiv.org/abs/1605.02097)）。
:::

:::annotation
这一节是整篇论文最“科幻”的地方，但逻辑很朴素：智能体从来就没见过真正的现实，它见到的永远是某个模型输出的观测。如果 M 能预测下一帧 $z$ 也能预测游戏是否结束，那么对 C 来说，M 就是一个完整的环境。真实游戏引擎在这里只负责提供训练数据，之后就可以被整个绕开。
:::

[^2]: <span class="bi-en" lang="en">We find this task interesting because although it is not difficult to train an agent to wobble around randomly generated tracks and obtain a mediocre score, CarRacing-v0 defines solving as getting average reward of 900 over 100 consecutive trials, which means the agent can only afford very few driving mistakes.</span><span class="bi-zh" lang="zh-CN">我们觉得这个任务很有意思：虽然训练一个在随机生成的赛道上晃晃悠悠，拿个平庸分数的智能体并不难，但 CarRacing-v0 把“通关”定义为在 100 次连续试验里平均拿到 900 分，这意味着智能体只能犯很少的驾驶错误。</span>

The agent must learn to avoid fireballs shot by monsters from the other side of the room with the sole intent of killing the agent. There are no explicit rewards in this environment, so to mimic natural selection, the cumulative reward can be defined to be the number of time steps the agent manages to stay alive during a rollout. 智能体必须学会躲开房间另一端怪物射来的火球，这些怪物的唯一目的就是杀死它。这个环境没有显式的奖励，为了模拟自然选择，可以把累计奖励定义为智能体在一次 rollout 中成功存活的时间步数。每次 rollout 最多运行 2100 个时间步（约 60 秒），如果 100 次连续 rollout 的平均存活时间超过 750 个时间步（约 20 秒），任务就算通关（[Paquette, 2016](https://gym.openai.com/envs/DoomTakeCover-v0/)）。

### 流程

:::bi
The setup of our VizDoom experiment is largely the same as the Car Racing task, except for a few key differences. In the Car Racing task, M is only trained to model the next $z_t$. Since we want to build a world model we can train our agent in, our M model here will also predict whether the agent dies in the next frame (as a binary event $done_t$, or $d_t$ for short), in addition to the next frame $z_t$.

:::zh
VizDoom 实验的设置和赛车任务大体相同，只有几个关键差别。在赛车任务里，M 只被训练来建模下一个 $z_t$。既然我们希望建立一个能用来训练智能体的世界模型，这里的 M 除了预测下一帧 $z_t$，还要预测智能体在下一帧是否会死亡，用一个二值事件 $done_t$ 表示，简写为 $d_t$。
:::

:::bi
Since the M model can predict the done state in addition to the next observation, we now have all of the ingredients needed to make a full RL environment. We first build an OpenAI Gym environment interface by wrapping a gym.Env interface over our M if it were a real Gym environment, and then train our agent inside of this virtual environment instead of using the actual environment.

:::zh
既然 M 除了预测下一个观测，还能预测结束状态，我们就凑齐了做一个完整强化学习环境所需的全部材料。我们先给 M 套上一层 `gym.Env` 接口，假装它就是一个真实的 Gym 环境，然后在虚拟环境里训练智能体，而不使用真实环境。
:::

:::bi
In this simulation, we do not need the V model to encode any real pixel frames during the hallucination process, so our agent will therefore only train entirely in a latent space environment. This has many advantages as we will see.

:::zh
在这个模拟里，幻觉过程中不需要 V 模型去编码任何真实像素帧，所以智能体完全在一个潜空间环境里训练。后面会看到，这样做有很多好处。
:::

:::bi
This virtual environment has an identical interface to the real environment, so after the agent learns a satisfactory policy in the virtual environment, we can easily deploy this policy back into the actual environment to see how well the policy transfers over.

:::zh
这个虚拟环境与真实环境的接口完全一致，所以智能体在虚拟环境里学到一个满意的策略后，我们可以轻松地把策略部署回真实环境，检验迁移效果。
:::

:::bi
To summarize the Take Cover experiment, below are the steps taken:

:::zh
总结一下 Take Cover 实验，步骤如下：
:::

:::bi
1. Collect 10,000 rollouts from a random policy.
2. Train VAE (V) to encode each frame into a latent vector $z \in \mathbb{R}^{64}$, and use V to convert the images collected from (1) into the latent space representation.
3. Train MDN-RNN (M) to model $P(z_{t+1}, d_{t+1} \mid a_t, z_t, h_t)$.
4. Define Controller (C) as $a_t = W_c [z_t \ h_t]$.
5. Use CMA-ES to solve for a $W_c$ that maximizes the expected survival time inside the virtual environment.
6. Use learned policy from (5) on actual environment.

:::zh
1. 用随机策略收集 10,000 次 rollout。
2. 训练 VAE（V），把每一帧编码成潜向量 $z \in \mathbb{R}^{64}$，并用 V 把第 (1) 步收集到的图像转换成潜空间表征。
3. 训练 MDN-RNN（M），对 $P(z_{t+1}, d_{t+1} \mid a_t, z_t, h_t)$ 建模。
4. 定义控制器（C）为 $a_t = W_c [z_t \ h_t]$。
5. 用 CMA-ES 求解使虚拟环境里期望存活时间最大的 $W_c$。
6. 把第 (5) 步学到的策略用到真实环境上。
:::

训练一段时间后，控制器学会了在梦境环境里穿行，躲开 M 生成的怪物发射的致命火球。智能体在虚拟环境里取得了约 900 个时间步的成绩。

:::bi
Here, our RNN-based world model is trained to mimic a complete game environment designed by human programmers. By learning only from raw image data collected from random episodes, it learns how to simulate the essential aspects of the game - such as the game logic, enemy behaviour, physics, and also the 3D graphics rendering.

:::zh
在这里，我们基于 RNN 的世界模型被训练来模仿一个由人类程序员设计的完整游戏环境。它只从随机回合采集到的原始图像数据里学习，就学会了模拟这个游戏的各个关键方面，包括游戏逻辑，敌人行为，物理规则，甚至 3D 图形渲染。
:::

:::bi
For instance, if the agent selects the left action, the M model learns to move the agent to the left and adjust its internal representation of the game states accordingly. It also learns to block the agent from moving beyond the walls on both sides of the level if the agent attempts to move too far in either direction. Occasionally, the M model needs to keep track of multiple fireballs being shot from several different monsters and coherently move them along in their intended directions. It must also detect whether the agent has been killed by one of these fireballs.

:::zh
举例来说，如果智能体选择向左的动作，M 模型就学会把它向左移动，并相应地调整自己对游戏状态的内部表征。它还学会在智能体试图往两侧走得太远时，拦住它，不让它穿过关卡两侧的墙。有时 M 模型需要同时跟踪好几只怪物射出的多颗火球，让它们协调地沿各自预定的方向飞行。它还必须判断智能体是否已经被其中一颗火球杀死。
:::

:::bi
Unlike the actual game environment, however, we note that it is possible to add extra uncertainty into the virtual environment, thus making the game more challenging in the dream environment. We can do this by increasing the temperature $\tau$ parameter during the sampling process of $z_{t+1}$. By increasing the uncertainty, our dream environment becomes more difficult compared to the actual environment. The fireballs may move more randomly in a less predictable path compared to the actual game. Sometimes the agent may even die due to sheer misfortune, without explanation.

:::zh
不过，和真实的游戏环境不同，我们注意到可以往虚拟环境里注入额外的不确定性，从而让梦境里的游戏更有挑战性。做法是在采样 $z_{t+1}$ 时调高温度参数 $\tau$。不确定性一高，梦境环境就比真实环境更难。火球可能飞得比真实游戏更随机，更难预测。有时智能体甚至会仅仅因为运气不好而莫名其妙地死掉。
:::

:::bi
We find agents that perform well in higher temperature settings generally perform better in the normal setting. In fact, increasing $\tau$ helps prevent our controller from taking advantage of the imperfections of our world model - we will discuss this in more depth later on.

:::zh
我们发现，在更高温度设置下表现好的智能体，在正常设置下通常也表现更好。事实上，调高 $\tau$ 有助于防止控制器利用世界模型的不完美之处，这一点后面会深入讨论。
:::

:::annotation
调高温度相当于给“梦境”加噪声。噪声让智能体无法依赖模型里那些只在训练分布内成立的捷径，逼它学出更稳健的生存策略。这个想法在今天的模型基强化学习里很常见，比如 Dreamer 系列也用 KL 平衡和想象轨迹的随机性来防止策略钻模型的空子。
:::

### 欺骗世界模型（Cheating the World Model）

:::bi
In our childhood, we may have encountered ways to exploit video games in ways that were not intended by the original game designer (Wikipedia, 2017). Players discover ways to collect unlimited lives or health, and by taking advantage of these exploits, they can easily complete an otherwise difficult game. However, in the process of doing so, they may have forfeited the opportunity to learn the skill required to master the game as intended by the game designer.

:::zh
小时候我们可能都遇到过一些“利用游戏漏洞”的玩法，这些玩法并非游戏设计者的本意（[Wikipedia, 2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_exploits)）。玩家发现能刷出无限生命或无限血量，靠这些漏洞就能轻松通关一个本来很难的游戏。可在这个过程中，他们也许放弃了去学习设计者希望玩家掌握的技巧的机会。
:::

:::bi
For instance, in our initial experiments, we noticed that our agent discovered an adversarial policy to move around in such a way so that the monsters in this virtual environment governed by the M model never shoots a single fireball during some rollouts. Even when there are signs of a fireball forming, the agent will move in a way to extinguish the fireballs magically as if it has superpowers in the environment.

:::zh
举例来说，在最初的实验里，我们注意到智能体发现了一种对抗性策略：它用一种特定的方式移动，使得由 M 模型掌控的虚拟环境里的怪物在某些 rollout 中一颗火球都不发射。甚至当火球已经开始成形时，智能体只要移动一下，就能像拥有超能力一样把火球“熄灭”。
:::

:::bi
Because our world model is only an approximate probabilistic model of the environment, it will occasionally generate trajectories that do not follow the laws governing the actual environment. As we saw previously, even the number of monsters on the other side of the room in the actual environment is not exactly reproduced by the world model. Like a child who learns that objects in the air usually fall to the ground, the child might also imagine unrealistic superheroes who fly across the sky. For this reason, our world model will be exploitable by the controller, even if in the actual environment such exploits do not exist.

:::zh
因为我们的世界模型只是对环境的一个近似概率模型，它偶尔会生成不遵守真实环境规律的轨迹。就像前面看到的，就连房间另一端怪物的数量，世界模型都还原不准。这就像一个孩子学会了空中的物体会落到地上，也会想象出不切实际的，能在天上飞的超级英雄。正因如此，我们的世界模型会被控制器利用，哪怕在真实环境里根本不存在这样的漏洞。
:::

:::bi
And since we are using the M model to generate a virtual dream environment for our agent, we are also giving the controller access to all of the hidden states of M. This is essentially granting our agent access to all of the internal states and memory of the game engine, rather than only the game observations that the player gets to see. Therefore our agent can efficiently explore ways to directly manipulate the hidden states of the game engine in its quest to maximize its expected cumulative reward. The weakness of this approach of learning a policy inside a learned dynamics model is that our agent can easily find an adversarial policy that can fool our dynamics model - it'll find a policy that looks good under our dynamics model, but will fail in the actual environment, usually because it visits states where the model is wrong because they are away from the training distribution.

:::zh
另外，既然我们用 M 模型为智能体生成虚拟梦境环境，我们也就把 M 的全部隐状态直接交给了控制器。这实质上等于让智能体拿到了游戏引擎的全部内部状态和记忆，而不只是玩家能看到的那部分游戏观测。于是智能体可以高效地探索直接操纵游戏引擎隐状态的办法，以最大化它的期望累计奖励。这种“在学到的动力学模型里学策略”的做法有一个弱点：智能体很容易找到一个能骗过动力学模型的对抗性策略。它会找到一个在我们的动力学模型里看起来很好，到了真实环境却会失败的策略，通常是因为它访问了模型训练分布之外，模型本来就预测错的那些状态。
:::

![Figure 18：智能体发现的“自动熄灭火焰球”对抗策略](./images/world-models/fig-18.png)
Figure 18. Agent discovers an adversarial policy to automatically extinguish fireballs after they are fired during some rollouts.

:::zh
图 18. 智能体发现了某种对抗性策略，在某些 rollout 里能把已经发射的火球自动熄灭。
:::

:::bi
This weakness could be the reason that many previous works that learn dynamics models of RL environments but do not actually use those models to fully replace the actual environments (Oh et al., 2015; Chiappa et al., 2017). Like in the M model proposed in (Schmidhuber, 1990a;b; 1991a), the dynamics model is a deterministic model, making the model easily exploitable by the agent if it is not perfect. Using Bayesian models, as in PILCO (Deisenroth & Rasmussen, 2011), helps to address this issue with the uncertainty estimates to some extent, however, they do not fully solve the problem. Recent work (Nagabandi et al., 2017) combines the model-based approach with traditional model-free RL training by first initializing the policy network with the learned policy, but must subsequently rely on model-free methods to fine-tune this policy in the actual environment.

:::zh
这个弱点也许正是许多早先工作的做法：它们学习强化学习环境的动力学模型，却不真正用模型去完全替代真实环境（[Oh et al., 2015](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.08750); [Chiappa et al., 2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.02254)）。像 Schmidhuber（1990a;b; 1991a）提出的 M 模型那样，动力学模型是确定性的，一旦不完美就很容易被智能体利用。使用贝叶斯模型，比如 PILCO（[Deisenroth & Rasmussen, 2011](http://mlg.eng.cam.ac.uk/pub/pdf/DeiRas11.pdf)），借助不确定性估计能在一定程度上缓解这个问题，但并没有彻底解决。较新的工作（Nagabandi et al., 2017）把基于模型的方法和传统的无模型强化学习训练结合起来，先用学到的策略初始化策略网络，但随后仍必须依靠无模型方法在真实环境里微调这个策略。
:::

:::bi
In Learning to Think (Schmidhuber, 2015a), it is acceptable that the RNN M is not always a reliable predictor. A (potentially evolution-based) RNN C can in principle learn to ignore a flawed M, or exploit certain useful parts of M for arbitrary computational purposes including hierarchical planning etc. This is not what we do here though - our present approach is still closer to some of the older systems (Schmidhuber, 1990a;b; 1991a), where a RNN M is used to predict and plan ahead step by step. Unlike this early work, however, we use evolution for C (like in Learning to Think) rather than traditional RL combined with RNNs, which has the advantage of both simplicity and generality.

:::zh
在《Learning to Think》里（Schmidhuber, 2015a），RNN M 并不总是一个可靠的预测器，这是可以接受的。一个（可能基于进化的）RNN C 原则上可以学会忽略有缺陷的 M，或者把 M 的某些有用部分挪作任意计算用途，包括分层规划等等。但我们现在做的不是这件事：我们目前的方法仍然更接近一些更早的系统（Schmidhuber, 1990a;b; 1991a），用 RNN M 来逐步预测和规划。不过和这些早期工作不同，我们对 C 使用进化而不是传统的强化学习加 RNN，好处是既简单又通用。
:::

:::bi
To make it more difficult for our C model to exploit deficiencies of the M model, we chose to use the MDN-RNN as the dynamics model, which models the distribution of possible outcomes in the actual environment, rather than merely predicting a deterministic future. Even if the actual environment is deterministic, the MDN-RNN would in effect approximate it as a stochastic environment. This has the advantage of allowing us to train our C model inside a more stochastic version of any environment - we can simply adjust the temperature parameter $\tau$ to control the amount of randomness in the M model, hence controlling the tradeoff between realism and exploitability.

:::zh
为了让 C 模型更难利用 M 模型的缺陷，我们选择用 MDN-RNN 作为动力学模型，它对真实环境里各种可能结果的分布建模，而不只是预测一个确定性的未来。即使真实环境是确定性的，MDN-RNN 实际上也会把它近似成一个随机环境。这样做的好处是，我们可以在任意环境的“更随机版本”里训练 C 模型，只要调整温度参数 $\tau$ 就能控制 M 模型里的随机程度，也就在逼真程度和可被利用程度之间控制了取舍。
:::

:::bi
Using a mixture of Gaussian model may seem like overkill given that the latent space encoded with the VAE model is just a single diagonal Gaussian distribution. However, the discrete modes in a mixture density model is useful for environments with random discrete events, such as whether a monster decides to shoot a fireball or stay put. While a single diagonal Gaussian might be sufficient to encode individual frames, a RNN with a mixture density output layer makes it easier to model the logic behind a more complicated environment with discrete random states.

:::zh
考虑到 VAE 编码出的潜空间只是一个对角高斯分布，使用高斯混合模型看起来有点杀鸡用牛刀。不过，混合密度模型里的离散模式，对带有随机离散事件的环境很有用，比如怪物是决定发射火球，还是原地不动。单个对角高斯也许足以编码单独的帧，但带混合密度输出层的 RNN 更容易建模一个带有离散随机状态的复杂环境背后的逻辑。
:::

:::bi
For instance, if we set the temperature parameter to a very low value of $\tau = 0.1$, effectively training our C model with a M model that is almost identical to a deterministic LSTM, the monsters inside this dream environment fail to shoot fireballs, no matter what the agent does, due to mode collapse. The M model is not able to jump to another mode in the mixture of Gaussian model where fireballs are formed and shot. Whatever policy learned inside of this dream will achieve a perfect score of 2100 most of the time, but will obviously fail when unleashed into the harsh reality of the actual world, underperforming even a random policy.

:::zh
举例来说，如果把温度参数设成很低的 $\tau = 0.1$，我们实际上是在用一个几乎等同于确定性 LSTM 的 M 模型训练 C。此时因为模式坍缩，梦境环境里的怪物无论智能体做什么都不会发射火球。M 模型无法跳到高斯混合模型里“火球被形成并发射”的那个模式上去。在这个梦里学到的任何策略，大多数时候都能拿到 2100 的满分，可一旦被放进真实世界的严酷现实里显然就会失败，甚至比随机策略还差。
:::

:::bi
Note again, however, that the simpler and more robust approach in Learning to Think does not insist on using M for step by step planning. Instead, C can learn to use M's subroutines (parts of M's weight matrix) for arbitrary computational purposes but can also learn to ignore M when M is useless and when ignoring M yields better performance. Nevertheless, at least in our present C-M variant, M's predictions are essential for teaching C, more like in some of the early C-M systems (Schmidhuber, 1990a;b; 1991a), but combined with evolution or black box optimization.

:::zh
不过要再次注意，《Learning to Think》里那个更简单，更稳健的思路并不坚持用 M 来做逐步规划。C 可以学会把 M 的子程序（M 权重矩阵的一部分）用于任意计算目的，也可以在 M 没用，忽略 M 效果更好时学会忽略 M。不过至少在我们目前的 C-M 变体里，M 的预测对教 C 来说是必不可少的，这更像一些早期的 C-M 系统（Schmidhuber, 1990a;b; 1991a），只不过结合了进化或黑箱优化。
:::

:::annotation
4.5 节是全文读起来最有“现场感”的一节。它没有把失败藏起来，而是把智能体钻空子的例子摆出来：怪物不开火，火球凭空消失。作者的态度也很明确，世界模型一定会有漏洞，问题不在于消灭漏洞，而在于设计出不容易被利用的训练方式。后面用 $\tau$ 调节随机性就是这个思路的直接产物。另外，作者也承认自己这一版没有走 Learning to Think 里“C 可以学到忽略 M”的更通用路线，这个自我限定挺难得。
:::

:::bi
By making the temperature $\tau$ an adjustable parameter of the M model, we can see the effect of training the C model on hallucinated virtual environments with different levels of uncertainty, and see how well they transfer over to the actual environment. We experimented with varying the temperature of the virtual environment and observing the resulting average score over 100 random rollouts of the actual environment after training the agent inside of the virtual environment with a given temperature:

:::zh
把温度 $\tau$ 做成 M 模型的可调参数之后，我们就能观察 C 模型在不同不确定性水平的幻觉虚拟环境里训练会有什么效果，以及它们的迁移表现如何。我们让虚拟环境取不同温度，先在对应温度下训练智能体，再在真实环境上跑 100 次随机 rollout，记录平均得分：
:::

We see that while increasing the temperature of the M model makes it more difficult for the C model to find adversarial policies, increasing it too much will make the virtual environment too difficult for the agent to learn anything, hence in practice it is a hyperparameter we can tune. The temperature also affects the types of strategies the agent discovers. For example, although the best score obtained is 1092 ± 556 with $\tau = 1.15$, increasing $\tau$ a notch to 1.30 results in a lower score but at the same time a less risky strategy with a lower variance of returns. For comparison, the best score on the OpenAI Gym leaderboard (Paquette, 2016) is 820 ± 58.

可以看到，调高 M 模型的温度会让 C 模型更难找到对抗性策略，但温度太高又会让虚拟环境难到智能体什么都学不到，所以实践中它是一个可以调的超参数。温度还会影响智能体发现的策略类型。比如 $\tau = 1.15$ 时取得了最好的 1092 ± 556，但把 $\tau$ 再调高一档到 1.30，得分会下降，同时也得到一种风险更低，回报方差更小的策略。作为对照，OpenAI Gym 排行榜上的最好成绩是 820 ± 58（Paquette, 2016）。

:::

## 迭代训练过程（Iterative Training Procedure）

:::bi
In our experiments, the tasks are relatively simple, so a reasonable world model can be trained using a dataset collected from a random policy. But what if our environments become more sophisticated? In any difficult environment, only parts of the world are made available to the agent only after it learns how to strategically navigate through its world.

:::zh
在我们的实验里，任务相对简单，所以用随机策略收集的数据集就能训练出一个像样的世界模型。可如果环境变得更复杂呢？在任何困难的环境里，世界只有一部分会向智能体敞开，而且是在它学会如何有策略地在世界里穿行之后才敞开。
:::

:::bi
For more complicated tasks, an iterative training procedure is required. We need our agent to be able to explore its world, and constantly collect new observations so that its world model can be improved and refined over time. An iterative training procedure (Schmidhuber, 2015a) is as follows:

:::zh
对于更复杂的任务，就需要一个迭代训练过程。我们需要智能体能够探索它所在的世界，不断收集新的观测，让世界模型随着时间的推移不断改进和完善。迭代训练过程（Schmidhuber, 2015a）如下：
:::

:::bi
1. Initialize M, C with random model parameters.
2. Rollout to actual environment N times. Save all actions $a_t$ and observations $x_t$ during rollouts to storage.
3. Train M to model $P(x_{t+1}, r_{t+1}, a_{t+1}, d_{t+1} \mid x_t, a_t, h_t)$ and train C to optimize expected rewards inside of M.
4. Go back to (2) if task has not been completed.

:::zh
1. 用随机参数初始化 M 和 C。
2. 在真实环境里 rollout N 次，把 rollout 过程中的所有动作 $a_t$ 和观测 $x_t$ 存进存储。
3. 训练 M 去建模 $P(x_{t+1}, r_{t+1}, a_{t+1}, d_{t+1} \mid x_t, a_t, h_t)$，同时在 M 内部训练 C 来优化期望奖励。
4. 如果任务还没完成，回到第 (2) 步。
:::

:::bi
We have shown that one iteration of this training loop was enough to solve simple tasks. For more difficult tasks, we need our controller in Step 2 to actively explore parts of the environment that is beneficial to improve its world model. An exciting research direction is to look at ways to incorporate artificial curiosity and intrinsic motivation (Schmidhuber, 2010; 2006; 1991b; Pathak et al., 2017; Oudeyer et al., 2007) and information seeking (Schmidhuber et al., 1994; Gottlieb et al., 2013) abilities in an agent to encourage novel exploration (Lehman & Stanley, 2011). In particular, we can augment the reward function based on improvement in compression quality (Schmidhuber, 2010; 2006; 1991b; 2015a).

:::zh
我们已经证明，这个训练循环只跑一轮就足以解决简单任务。对更困难的任务，我们需要第 2 步里的控制器主动去探索那些有助于改进世界模型的区域。一个令人兴奋的研究方向，是往智能体里加入人工好奇心与内在动机（Schmidhuber, 2010; 2006; 1991b; [Pathak et al., 2017](https://pathak22.github.io/noreward-rl/); Oudeyer et al., 2007）以及信息寻求能力（Schmidhuber et al., 1994; Gottlieb et al., 2013），鼓励它做新颖的探索（Lehman & Stanley, 2011）。具体来说，我们可以根据压缩质量的提升来放大奖励函数（Schmidhuber, 2010; 2006; 1991b; 2015a）。
:::

:::bi
In the present approach, since M is a MDN-RNN that models a probability distribution for the next frame, if it does a poor job, then it means the agent has encountered parts of the world that it is not familiar with. Therefore we can adapt and reuse M's training loss function to encourage curiosity. By flipping the sign of M's loss function in the actual environment, the agent will be encouraged to explore parts of the world that it is not familiar with. The new data it collects may improve the world model.

:::zh
在目前的方案里，M 是一个为下一帧建模概率分布的 MDN-RNN。如果它预测得不好，就说明智能体遇到了自己还不熟悉的世界角落。于是我们可以改造并复用 M 的训练损失函数来鼓励好奇心。在真实环境里把 M 损失函数的符号翻过来，智能体就会被鼓励去探索自己不熟悉的世界区域。它收集到的新数据又可能反过来改进世界模型。
:::

:::annotation
“把预测损失翻个符号当好奇心奖励”这个操作很优雅。预测得越差，说明越新鲜，奖励越高。它和后来很多内在奖励方法，比如基于预测误差的好奇心，基于集成模型分歧的探索，是同一族思路。区别在于这里直接复用世界模型自己的损失，不需要额外的判别器或集成。
:::

:::bi
The iterative training procedure requires the M model to not only predict the next observation $x$ and done, but also predict the action and reward for the next time step. This may be required for more difficult tasks. For instance, if our agent needs to learn complex motor skills to walk around its environment, the world model will learn to imitate its own C model that has already learned to walk. After difficult motor skills, such as walking, is absorbed into a large world model with lots of capacity, the smaller C model can rely on the motor skills already absorbed by the world model and focus on learning more higher level skills to navigate itself using the motor skills it had already learned.

:::zh
迭代训练过程要求 M 模型不只预测下一个观测 $x$ 和结束标记，还要预测下一时间步的动作和奖励。对更困难的任务来说，这也许是必需的。举例来说，如果智能体需要学会复杂的运动技能才能在环境里走动，世界模型会学着去模仿它自己那个已经学会走路的 C 模型。等走路这样的困难运动技能被吸收进一个容量很大的世界模型之后，较小的 C 模型就可以依赖世界模型已经吸收的运动技能，把精力放在更高层的技能上，用已经学会的运动能力来导航。
:::

:::bi
An interesting connection to the neuroscience literature is the work on hippocampal replay that examines how the brain replays recent experiences when an animal rests or sleeps. Replaying recent experiences plays an important role in memory consolidation (Foster, 2017) - where hippocampus-dependent memories become independent of the hippocampus over a period of time. As (Foster, 2017) puts it, replay is less like dreaming and more like thought. We invite readers to read Replay Comes of Age (Foster, 2017) for a detailed overview of replay from a neuroscience perspective with connections to theoretical reinforcement learning.

:::zh
和神经科学文献之间有一个很有意思的联系：海马体回放（hippocampal replay）研究考察的是动物休息或睡眠时，大脑如何回放最近的经历。回放最近的经历在记忆巩固中起着重要作用（[Foster, 2017](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-neuro-072116-031538)），依赖海马体的记忆会在一段时间之后变得不再依赖海马体。正如 Foster（2017）所说，回放与其说像做梦，不如说更像思考。我们邀请读者去读一读《Replay Comes of Age》（Foster, 2017），从神经科学视角看回放的详细综述，以及它和理论强化学习之间的联系。
:::

:::annotation
把迭代训练那一节和在梦里训练那一节放在一起看，会发现一个漂亮的呼应。在梦里训练可以避开现实开销，迭代训练那一节又说也许我们本来就该在睡眠里反复回放白天收集到的数据。论文没有声称自己的算法模拟了海马体，只是指出两条思路在结构上相似：先用经验学一个可回放的世界模型，再在这个模型里反复练习。
:::

:::bi
Iterative training could allow the C-M model to develop a natural hierarchical way to learn. Recent works about self-play in RL (Sukhbaatar et al., 2017; Bansal et al., 2017; Al-Shedivat et al., 2017) and PowerPlay (Schmidhuber, 2013; Srivastava et al., 2012) also explores methods that lead to a natural curriculum learning (Schmidhuber, 2002), and we feel this is one of the more exciting research areas of reinforcement learning.

:::zh
迭代训练还有可能让 C-M 模型发展出一种自然的分层学习方式。最近关于强化学习里自博弈的研究（Sukhbaatar et al., 2017; Bansal et al., 2017; Al-Shedivat et al., 2017）以及 PowerPlay（Schmidhuber, 2013; Srivastava et al., 2012），也在探索能自然形成课程学习的方法（Schmidhuber, 2002）。我们觉得这是强化学习里更令人兴奋的研究方向之一。
:::

## 相关工作（Related Work）

:::bi
There is extensive literature on learning a dynamics model, and using this model to train a policy. Many concepts first explored in the 1980s for feed-forward neural networks (FNNs) (Werbos, 1987; Munro, 1987; Robinson & Fallside, 1989; Werbos, 1989; Nguyen & Widrow, 1989) and in the 1990s for RNNs (Schmidhuber, 1990a;b; 1991a; 1990c) laid some of the groundwork for Learning to Think (Schmidhuber, 2015a). The more recent PILCO (Deisenroth & Rasmussen, 2011; Duvenaud, 2016; McAllister & Rasmussen, 2016) is a probabilistic model-based search policy method designed to solve difficult control problems. Using data collected from the environment, PILCO uses a Gaussian process (GP) model to learn the system dynamics, and then uses this model to sample many trajectories in order to train a controller to perform a desired task, such as swinging up a pendulum, or riding a unicycle.

:::zh
关于学习动力学模型，再用这个模型训练策略，文献已经很多了。许多概念最早在 20 世纪 80 年代的前馈神经网络（FNN）里被探索（Werbos, 1987; Munro, 1987; Robinson & Fallside, 1989; Werbos, 1989; Nguyen & Widrow, 1989），90 年代又在 RNN 里继续探索（Schmidhuber, 1990a;b; 1991a; 1990c），为《Learning to Think》打下了一些基础（Schmidhuber, 2015a）。更近一些的 PILCO（Deisenroth & Rasmussen, 2011; Duvenaud, 2016; McAllister & Rasmussen, 2016）是一种基于概率模型的策略搜索方法，专门用来解决困难的控制问题。PILCO 用从环境里收集的数据，让高斯过程（GP）模型学出系统动力学，再用这个模型采样出大量轨迹，从而训练一个控制器去完成期望任务，比如把单摆摆起来，或者骑独轮车。
:::

:::figure
![Figure 20：带内部 RNN 世界模型的控制器](./images/world-models/fig-20.png)
Figure 20. A controller with internal RNN model of the world (Schmidhuber, 1990a).

:::zh
图 20. 一个带有内部 RNN 世界模型的控制器（Schmidhuber, 1990a）。
:::
:::

:::bi
While Gaussian processes work well with a small set of low dimension data, their computational complexity makes them difficult to scale up to model a large history of high dimensional observations. Other recent works (Gal et al., 2016; Depeweg et al., 2016) use Bayesian neural networks instead of GPs to learn a dynamics model. These methods have demonstrated promising results on challenging control tasks (Hein et al., 2017), where the states are known and well defined, and the observation is relatively low dimensional. Here we are interested in modelling dynamics observed from high dimensional visual data where our input is a sequence of raw pixel frames.

:::zh
高斯过程在一小部分低维数据上表现很好，但它的计算复杂度让它很难扩展到对大量高维观测历史建模。近期的其他工作（Gal et al., 2016; Depeweg et al., 2016）改用贝叶斯神经网络代替高斯过程来学习动力学模型。这些方法在困难的控制任务上展现了有希望的结果（Hein et al., 2017），不过那些任务里状态是已知且定义良好的，观测的维度也相对较低。而我们关心的是从高维视觉数据里建模动力学，输入是一串原始像素帧。
:::

:::bi
In robotic control applications, the ability to learn the dynamics of a system from observing only camera-based video inputs is a challenging but important problem. Early work on RL for active vision trained an FNN to take the current image frame of a video sequence to predict the next frame (Schmidhuber & Huber, 1991), and use this predictive model to train a fovea-shifting control network trying to find targets in a visual scene. To get around the difficulty of training a dynamical model to learn directly from high-dimensional pixel images, researchers explored using neural networks to first learn a compressed representation of the video frames. Recent work along these lines (Wahlstrm et al., 2014; 2015) was able to train controllers using the bottleneck hidden layer of an autoencoder as low-dimensional feature vectors to control a pendulum from pixel inputs.

:::zh
在机器人控制应用里，只靠摄像头视频输入来学习系统动力学，是一个有挑战但重要的问题。主动视觉早期的工作训练 FNN 拿视频序列的当前帧去预测下一帧（Schmidhuber & Huber, 1991），再用这个预测模型训练一个移动中央凹的控制网络，在视觉场景里寻找目标。为了绕开“动力学模型直接学高维像素”的困难，研究者开始尝试先用神经网络学出视频帧的压缩表征。沿着这条线，近期的工作（Wahlstrm et al., 2014; 2015）用自编码器的瓶颈隐藏层作为低维特征向量，从像素输入训练控制器去控制单摆。
:::

:::bi
Learning a model of the dynamics from a compressed latent space enable RL algorithms to be much more data-efficient (Finn et al., 2015; Watter et al., 2015; Finn, 2017). We invite readers to watch Finn's lecture on Model-Based RL (Finn, 2017) to learn more.

:::zh
从压缩后的潜空间学习动力学模型，能让强化学习算法在数据效率上高得多（Finn et al., 2015; Watter et al., 2015; Finn, 2017）。想了解更多，我们推荐读者去看 Finn 关于基于模型的强化学习的讲座（[Finn, 2017](https://youtu.be/iC2a7M9voYU?t=44m35s)）。
:::

:::bi
Video game environments are also popular in model-based RL research as a testbed for new ideas. (Matthew Guzdial, 2017) used a feed-forward convolutional neural network (CNN) to learn a forward simulation model of a video game. Learning to predict how different actions affect future states in the environment is useful for game-play agents, since if our agent can predict what happens in the future given its current state and action, it can simply select the best action that suits its goal. This has been demonstrated not only in early work (Nguyen & Widrow, 1989; Schmidhuber & Huber, 1991) (when compute was a million times more expensive than today) but also in recent studies (Dosovitskiy & Koltun, 2016) on several competitive VizDoom environments.

:::zh
视频游戏环境也是基于模型的强化学习研究里很受欢迎的试验场。Matthew Guzdial（2017）用前馈卷积神经网络（CNN）学习了一个视频游戏的前向模拟模型。学会预测不同动作会怎样影响环境的未来状态，对游戏智能体很有用，因为只要智能体能根据当前状态和动作预测接下来发生什么，它就可以直接挑出最符合目标的动作。这一点不仅在早期工作里被展示过（Nguyen & Widrow, 1989; Schmidhuber & Huber, 1991），那时算力比现在贵一百万倍，也在近期的研究里被验证过（[Dosovitskiy & Koltun, 2016](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.01779)），对象是几个有竞争性的 VizDoom 环境。
:::

:::bi
The works mentioned above use FNNs to predict the next video frame. We may want to use models that can capture longer term time dependencies. RNNs are powerful models suitable for sequence modelling (Graves, 2013). In a lecture called Hallucination with RNNs (Graves, 2015), Graves demonstrated the ability of RNNs to learn a probabilistic model of Atari game environments. He trained RNNs to learn the structure of such a game and then showed that they can hallucinate similar game levels on its own.

:::zh
上面这些工作都是用 FNN 预测下一帧视频。我们可能还想要能捕捉更长时间依赖的模型。RNN 是适合序列建模的强模型（Graves, 2013）。在名为《Hallucination with RNNs》的讲座里（[Graves, 2015](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-yX1SYeDHbg&t=49m33s)），Graves 展示了 RNN 学习 Atari 游戏环境概率模型的能力。他训练 RNN 学会这类游戏的结构，然后证明网络能够自己“幻觉”出类似的游戏关卡。
:::

:::bi
Using RNNs to develop internal models to reason about the future has been explored as early as 1990 in a paper called Making the World Differentiable (Schmidhuber, 1990a), and then further explored in (Schmidhuber, 1990b; 1991a; 1990c). A more recent paper called Learning to Think (Schmidhuber, 2015a) presented a unifying framework for building a RNN-based general problem solver that can learn a world model of its environment and also learn to reason about the future using this model. Subsequent works have used RNN-based models to generate many frames into the future (Chiappa et al., 2017; Oh et al., 2015; Denton & Birodkar, 2017), and also as an internal model to reason about the future (Silver et al., 2016; Weber et al., 2017; Watters et al., 2017).

:::zh
用 RNN 建立内部模型来推理未来，早在 1990 年一篇题为《Making the World Differentiable》的论文里就被探索过（Schmidhuber, 1990a），之后又在（Schmidhuber, 1990b; 1991a; 1990c）里继续深入。更近的一篇《Learning to Think》（Schmidhuber, 2015a）提出了一个统一框架，用来构建基于 RNN 的通用问题求解器，它既能学会环境的世界模型，也能用这个模型推理未来。后续工作用基于 RNN 的模型向未来生成很多帧（Chiappa et al., 2017; Oh et al., 2015; Denton & Birodkar, 2017），也把它们当作推理未来的内部模型（Silver et al., 2016; Weber et al., 2017; Watters et al., 2017）。
:::

:::bi
In this work, we used evolution strategies to train our controller, as it offers many benefits. For instance, we only need to provide the optimizer with the final cumulative reward, rather than the entire history. ES is also easy to parallelize - we can launch many instances of rollout with different solutions to many workers and quickly compute a set of cumulative rewards in parallel. Recent works (Fernando et al., 2017; Salimans et al., 2017; Ha, 2017b; Stanley & Clune, 2017) have confirmed that ES is a viable alternative to traditional Deep RL methods on many strong baselines. Before the popularity of Deep RL methods (Mnih et al., 2013), evolution-based algorithms have been shown to be effective at solving RL tasks (Stanley & Miikkulainen, 2002; Gomez et al., 2008; Gomez & Schmidhuber, 2005; Gauci & Stanley, 2010; Sehnke et al., 2010; Miikkulainen, 2013). Evolution-based algorithms have even been able to solve difficult RL tasks from high dimensional pixel inputs (Koutnik et al., 2013; Hausknecht et al., 2013; Parker & Bryant, 2012). More recent works (Alvernaz & Togelius, 2017) combine VAE and ES, which is similar to our approach.

:::zh
在这项工作里，我们用进化策略训练控制器，因为它带来很多好处。比如，我们只需要把最终的累计奖励交给优化器，而不需要提供完整的历史。进化策略也很容易并行，可以把不同解的多个 rollout 实例分发给很多工作进程，并行地快速算出一组累计奖励。近期的工作（Fernando et al., 2017; [Salimans et al., 2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.03864); [Ha, 2017b](http://blog.otoro.net/2017/11/12/evolving-stable-strategies/); [Stanley & Clune, 2017](https://eng.uber.com/deep-neuroevolution/)）已经确认，在许多强基线上，进化策略是传统深度强化学习方法的可行替代方案。在深度强化学习方法流行之前（Mnih et al., 2013），基于进化的算法就已经被证明能有效解决强化学习任务（Stanley & Miikkulainen, 2002; Gomez et al., 2008; Gomez & Schmidhuber, 2005; Gauci & Stanley, 2010; Sehnke et al., 2010; Miikkulainen, 2013）。基于进化的算法甚至能从高维像素输入解决困难的强化学习任务（Koutnik et al., 2013; Hausknecht et al., 2013; Parker & Bryant, 2012）。更近的工作（Alvernaz & Togelius, 2017）把 VAE 和进化策略结合起来，和我们的做法很相似。
:::

## 讨论（Discussion）

:::figure
![Figure 21：1990 年那张“RNN 控制器与环境交互”的古早手绘图](./images/world-models/fig-21.png)
Figure 21. Ancient drawing (1990) of a RNN-based controller interacting with an environment (Schmidhuber, 1990a).

:::zh
图 21. 一张 1990 年的古早手绘图，画的是基于 RNN 的控制器与环境交互（Schmidhuber, 1990a）。
:::
:::

:::bi
We have demonstrated the possibility of training an agent to perform tasks entirely inside of its simulated latent space dream world. This approach offers many practical benefits. For instance, running computationally intensive game engines require using heavy compute resources for rendering the game states into image frames, or calculating physics not immediately relevant to the game. We may not want to waste cycles training an agent in the actual environment, but instead train the agent as many times as we want inside its simulated environment. Training agents in the real world is even more expensive, so world models that are trained incrementally to simulate reality may prove to be useful for transferring policies back to the real world. Our approach may complement sim2real approaches outlined in (Bousmalis et al., 2017; Higgins et al., 2017).

:::zh
我们已经证明，完全可以只在模拟的潜空间梦境世界里训练智能体来完成任务。这种做法有很多实际好处。比如，运行计算密集的游戏引擎要消耗大量算力去把游戏状态渲染成图像帧，还要计算与游戏当前目标并不直接相关的物理。我们可能不想把算力浪费在真实环境里训练智能体，而是想在这个模拟环境里想训练多少次就训练多少次。在真实世界里训练智能体甚至更贵，所以那种逐步训练起来模拟现实的世界模型，也许对把策略迁移回真实世界很有用。我们的方法可以补充（Bousmalis et al., 2017; Higgins et al., 2017）里概述的 sim2real 方法。
:::

:::bi
Furthermore, we can take advantage of deep learning frameworks to accelerate our world model simulations using GPUs in a distributed environment. The benefit of implementing the world model as a fully differentiable recurrent computation graph also means that we may be able to train our agents in the dream directly using the backpropagation algorithm to fine-tune its policy to maximize an objective function (Schmidhuber, 1990a;b; 1991a).

:::zh
此外，我们还可以利用深度学习框架，在分布式环境里用 GPU 加速世界模型模拟。把世界模型实现成一个完全可微的循环计算图还有一个好处：我们也许可以直接在梦里用反向传播算法训练智能体，微调它的策略来最大化某个目标函数（Schmidhuber, 1990a;b; 1991a）。
:::

:::bi
The choice of using a VAE for the V model and training it as a standalone model also has its limitations, since it may encode parts of the observations that are not relevant to a task. After all, unsupervised learning cannot, by definition, know what will be useful for the task at hand. For instance, it reproduced unimportant detailed brick tile patterns on the side walls in the Doom environment, but failed to reproduce task-relevant tiles on the road in the Car Racing environment. By training together with a M model that predicts rewards, the VAE may learn to focus on task-relevant areas of the image, but the tradeoff here is that we may not be able to reuse the VAE effectively for new tasks without retraining.

:::zh
用 VAE 做 V 模型，并且把它当作独立模型训练的选择也有局限，因为它可能编码了观测里与任务无关的部分。毕竟无监督学习按定义并不知道什么对当前任务有用。比如，它把 Doom 环境侧墙上无关紧要的砖块纹理细节重建得很好，却没能重建 CarRacing 环境里赛道上与任务相关的格子。如果和一个会预测奖励的 M 模型一起训练，VAE 也许能学会关注图像里与任务相关的区域，但代价是我们可能无法在不重新训练的情况下，把 VAE 有效地复用到新任务上。
:::

:::bi
Learning task-relevant features has connections to neuroscience as well. Primary sensory neurons are released from inhibition when rewards are received, which suggests that they generally learn task-relevant features, rather than just any features, at least in adulthood (Pi et al., 2013).

:::zh
学习与任务相关的特征，也和神经科学有联系。收到奖励时，初级感觉神经元会从抑制状态中释放出来，这提示它们通常学的是与任务相关的特征，而不只是随便什么特征，至少成年后是这样（Pi et al., 2013）。
:::

:::bi
Another concern is the limited capacity of our world model. While modern storage devices can store large amounts of historical data generated using the iterative training procedure, our LSTM (Hochreiter & Schmidhuber, 1997; Gers et al., 2000)-based world model may not be able to store all of the recorded information inside its weight connections. While the human brain can hold decades and even centuries of memories to some resolution (Bartol et al., 2015), our neural networks trained with backpropagation have more limited capacity and suffer from issues such as catastrophic forgetting (Ratcliff, 1990; French, 1994; Kirkpatrick et al., 2016). Future work may explore replacing the small MDN-RNN network with higher capacity models (Shazeer et al., 2017; Ha et al., 2016; Suarez, 2017; van den Oord et al., 2016; Vaswani et al., 2017), or incorporating an external memory module (Gemici et al., 2017), if we want our agent to learn to explore more complicated worlds.

:::zh
另一个担忧是我们世界模型的容量有限。现代存储设备可以存下迭代训练过程产生的大量历史数据，但我们基于 LSTM（Hochreiter & Schmidhuber, 1997; Gers et al., 2000）的世界模型也许无法把所有记录下来的信息都存进权重连接里。人脑能以某种分辨率保存几十年甚至上百年的记忆（Bartol et al., 2015），而我们用反向传播训练的神经网络容量更有限，还会遭遇灾难性遗忘之类的问题（Ratcliff, 1990; French, 1994; Kirkpatrick et al., 2016）。如果想让智能体学会探索更复杂的世界，未来的工作可以尝试用容量更大的模型替换小小的 MDN-RNN 网络（Shazeer et al., 2017; Ha et al., 2016; Suarez, 2017; van den Oord et al., 2016; Vaswani et al., 2017），或者引入外部记忆模块（Gemici et al., 2017）。
:::

:::bi
Like early RNN-based C-M systems (Schmidhuber, 1990a;b; 1991a; 1990c), ours simulates possible futures time step by time step, without profiting from human-like hierarchical planning or abstract reasoning, which often ignores irrelevant spatial-temporal details. However, the more general Learning To Think (Schmidhuber, 2015a) approach is not limited to this rather naive approach. Instead it allows a recurrent C to learn to address subroutines of the recurrent M, and reuse them for problem solving in arbitrary computable ways, e.g., through hierarchical planning or other kinds of exploiting parts of M's program-like weight matrix.

:::zh
和早期基于 RNN 的 C-M 系统一样（Schmidhuber, 1990a;b; 1991a; 1990c），我们逐时间步地模拟可能的未来，没有借用人类式的分层规划或抽象推理，而后者常常会忽略无关的时空细节。不过，更通用的《Learning to Think》思路（Schmidhuber, 2015a）并不局限在这种相当朴素的做法上。它允许循环的 C 学会调用循环的 M 的子程序，并以任意可计算的方式复用这些子程序来解决问题，比如通过分层规划，或者以别的方式利用 M 那套类似程序的权重矩阵的某些部分。
:::

:::bi
A recent One Big Net (Schmidhuber, 2018) extension of the C-M approach collapses C and M into a single network, and uses PowerPlay-like (Schmidhuber, 2013; Srivastava et al., 2012) behavioural replay (where the behaviour of a teacher net is compressed into a student net (Schmidhuber, 1992)) to avoid forgetting old prediction and control skills when learning new ones. Experiments with those more general approaches are left for future work.

:::zh
C-M 方法最近有一个叫《One Big Net》的扩展（Schmidhuber, 2018），它把 C 和 M 合并成一个网络，并用类似 PowerPlay 的行为回放（Schmidhuber, 2013; Srivastava et al., 2012），也就是把教师网络的行为压缩进学生网络（Schmidhuber, 1992），来避免在学习新技能时忘掉旧的预测和控制技能。对这些更通用方法的实验，留待未来工作。
:::

:::annotation
讨论那一节的自我批评比很多论文都实在。作者主动点出了三个局限：VAE 不知道什么对任务有用，世界模型的容量有限，以及这个方法还只停留在逐步模拟，没有分层规划。读到这里再看今天的世界模型研究，会发现这三条其实正好对应后来三条主线：按任务对齐的表征，更强的生成模型和记忆，以及在潜空间里做规划和搜索。这篇 2018 年的论文把地图画得相当准。
:::

### 变分自编码器（Variational Autoencoder）

:::figure
![Figure 22：ConvVAE 各层的张量形状](./images/world-models/fig-22.png)
Figure 22. Description of tensor shapes at each layer of ConvVAE.

:::zh
图 22. ConvVAE 每一层的张量形状说明。
:::
:::

:::bi
We trained a Convolutional Variational Autoencoder (ConvVAE) model as the V Model of our agent. Unlike vanilla autoencoders, enforcing a Gaussian prior over the latent vector $z$ also limits the amount of information capacity for compressing each frame, but this Gaussian prior also makes the world model more robust to unrealistic $z$ vectors generated by the M Model.

:::zh
我们训练了一个卷积变分自编码器（ConvVAE）模型，作为智能体的 V 模型。和普通自编码器不同，在潜向量 $z$ 上强制一个高斯先验，同时也限制了压缩每一帧时可用的信息容量大小。但这个高斯先验还让世界模型对 M 模型生成的不切实际的 $z$ 向量更稳健。
:::

:::bi
As the environment may give us observations as high dimensional pixel images, we first resize each image to 64x64 pixels before and use this resized image as the V Model's observation. Each pixel is stored as three floating point values between 0 and 1 to represent each of the RGB channels. The ConvVAE takes in this 64x64x3 input tensor and passes this data through 4 convolutional layers to encode it into low dimension vectors $\mu$ and $\sigma$, each of size $N_z$. The latent vector $z$ is sampled from the Gaussian prior $\mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma I)$. In the Car Racing task, $N_z$ is 32 while for the Doom task $N_z$ is 64. The latent vector $z$ is passed through 4 of deconvolution layers used to decode and reconstruct the image.

:::zh
由于环境给我们的观测可能是高维像素图像，我们先把每张图缩放到 64x64 像素，再把缩放后的图作为 V 模型的观测。每个像素用 0 到 1 之间的三个浮点数表示 RGB 三个通道。ConvVAE 接收这个 64x64x3 的输入张量，把它送过 4 层卷积，编码成两个低维向量 $\mu$ 和 $\sigma$，大小都是 $N_z$。潜向量 $z$ 从高斯先验 $\mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma I)$ 里采样得到。在赛车任务里 $N_z$ 是 32，在 Doom 任务里 $N_z$ 是 64。潜向量 $z$ 再送过 4 层反卷积，用来解码，重建图像。
:::

:::bi
Each convolution and deconvolution layer uses a stride of 2. The layers are indicated in the diagram in Italics as Activation-type Output Channels x Filter Size. All convolutional and deconvolutional layers use relu activations except for the output layer as we need the output to be between 0 and 1. We trained the model for 1 epoch over the data collected from a random policy, using L2 distance between the input image and the reconstruction to quantify the reconstruction loss we optimize for, in addition to KL loss.

:::zh
每一层卷积和反卷积的步长都是 2。图里用斜体标出了各层的配置，格式是“激活类型 输出通道数 x 滤波器尺寸”。除了输出层，所有卷积和反卷积层都用 ReLU 激活，因为输出层需要把结果落在 0 和 1 之间。我们用随机策略收集的数据训练了 1 个 epoch，用输入图像和重建图像之间的 L2 距离来衡量重建损失，再加上 KL 损失一起优化。
:::

### 循环神经网络（Recurrent Neural Network）

:::bi
For the M Model, we use an LSTM (Hochreiter & Schmidhuber, 1997) recurrent neural network combined with a Mixture Density Network (Bishop, 1994) as the output layer. We use this network to model the probability distribution of the next $z$ in the next time step as a Mixture of Gaussian distribution. This approach is very similar to (Graves, 2013) in the Unconditional Handwriting Generation section and also the decoder-only section of SketchRNN (Ha & Eck, 2017). The only difference in the approach used is that we did not model the correlation parameter between each element of $z$, and instead had the MDN-RNN output a diagonal covariance matrix of a factored Gaussian distribution.

:::zh
M 模型使用 LSTM（Hochreiter & Schmidhuber, 1997）循环神经网络，输出层是一个混合密度网络（Bishop, 1994）。我们用这个网络把下一个时间步的 $z$ 的概率分布建模成高斯混合分布。这个做法和（Graves, 2013）里无条件手写生成那一节非常相似，也和 SketchRNN 的纯解码器部分相似（Ha & Eck, 2017）。唯一的区别在于，我们没有对 $z$ 各元素之间的相关性参数建模，而是让 MDN-RNN 输出一个因子化高斯分布的对角协方差矩阵。
:::

:::figure
![Figure 23：与 Graves 和 SketchRNN 类似的 MDN-RNN 解码器](./images/world-models/fig-23.png)
Figure 23. MDN-RNN decoder similar to (Graves, 2013; Ha & Eck, 2017)

:::zh
图 23. 与（Graves, 2013; Ha & Eck, 2017）类似的 MDN-RNN 解码器。
:::
:::

:::bi
Unlike the handwriting and sketch generation works, rather than using the MDN-RNN to model the pdf of the next pen stroke, we model instead the pdf of the next latent vector $z$. We would sample from this pdf at each time step to generate the hallucinated environments. In the Doom task, we also also use the MDN-RNN to predict the probability of whether the agent has died in this frame. If that probability is above 50%, then we set done to be true in the virtual environment. Given that death is a low probability event at each time step, we find the cutoff approach to more stable compared to sampling from the Bernoulli distribution.

:::zh
和手写，简笔画生成的工作不同，我们不是用 MDN-RNN 建模下一笔的概率密度，而是建模下一个潜向量 $z$ 的概率密度。在每个时间步，我们从这团概率密度里采样，用来生成幻觉环境。在 Doom 任务里，我们还用 MDN-RNN 预测智能体在这一帧死亡的概率。如果这个概率超过 50%，我们就在虚拟环境里把 done 置为真。考虑到每个时间步死亡都是小概率事件，我们发现用阈值截断比从伯努利分布采样更稳定。
:::

:::bi
The MDN-RNNs were trained for 20 epochs on the data collected from a random policy agent. In the Car Racing task, the LSTM used 256 hidden units, while the Doom task used 512 hidden units. In both tasks, we used 5 Gaussian mixtures and did not model the correlation $\rho$ parameter, hence $z$ is sampled from a factored mixture of Gaussian distribution.

:::zh
MDN-RNN 用随机策略智能体收集的数据训练了 20 个 epoch。在赛车任务里，LSTM 用了 256 个隐藏单元，Doom 任务用了 512 个。两个任务都用了 5 个高斯分量，并且没有对相关系数 $\rho$ 建模，因此 $z$ 是从因子化的高斯混合分布里采样出来的。
:::

:::bi
When training the MDN-RNN using teacher forcing from the recorded data, we store a pre-computed set of $\mu$ and $\sigma$ for each of the frames, and sample an input $z \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma)$ each time we construct a training batch, to prevent overfitting our MDN-RNN to a specific sampled $z$.

:::zh
用记录数据以 teacher forcing 方式训练 MDN-RNN 时，我们为每一帧预先存好一组 $\mu$ 和 $\sigma$，每次构造训练批次时都重新采样一个输入 $z \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma)$，防止 MDN-RNN 过拟合到某一个特定的采样 $z$ 上。
:::

:::annotation
这里有个小细节值得注意：训练 M 的时候每一批都重新采 $z$，相当于在潜空间里做了数据增强，避免模型把某一次采样的具体噪声也背下来。这和 VAE 的重参数化技巧是同一个思路，只不过这里用在时序模型的输入上。
:::

### 控制器（Controller）

:::bi
For both environments, we applied tanh nonlinearities to clip and bound the action space to the appropriate ranges. For instance, in the Car Racing task, the steering wheel has a range from -1 to 1, the acceleration pedal from 0 to 1, and the brakes from 0 to 1. In the Doom environment, we converted the discrete actions into a continuous action space between -1 to 1, and divided this range into thirds to indicate whether the agent is moving left, staying where it is, or moving to the right. We would give the C Model a feature vector as its input, consisting of $z$ and the hidden state of the MDN-RNN. In the Car Racing task, this hidden state is the output vector $h$ of the LSTM, while for the Doom task it is both the cell vector $c$ and the output vector $h$ of the LSTM.

:::zh
在两个环境里，我们都用 tanh 非线性把动作空间裁剪，限制在合适的范围内。比如在赛车任务里，方向盘的取值范围是 -1 到 1，油门是 0 到 1，刹车也是 0 到 1。在 Doom 环境里，我们把离散动作转换成 -1 到 1 之间的连续动作空间，再把这段区间三等分，分别表示智能体向左移动，原地不动，向右移动。我们给 C 模型一个特征向量作为输入，由 $z$ 和 MDN-RNN 的隐状态组成。在赛车任务里，这个隐状态是 LSTM 的输出向量 $h$，而在 Doom 任务里，它是 LSTM 的细胞向量 $c$ 和输出向量 $h$ 两者。
:::

### 进化策略（Evolution Strategies）

Following the approach described in Evolving Stable Strategies (Ha, 2017b), we used a population size of 64, and had each agent perform the task 16 times with different initial random seeds. The fitness value for the agent is the average cumulative reward of the 16 random rollouts. :::zh
我们用协方差矩阵自适应进化策略（CMA-ES）（Hansen, 2016）来进化 C 模型的权重。按照《Evolving Stable Strategies》里描述的做法（Ha, 2017b），我们用 64 的种群规模，让每个智能体用不同的随机种子把任务跑 16 次。智能体的适应度值，就是这 16 次随机 rollout 的平均累计奖励。下图画出了每一代里 64 个智能体中表现最好，表现最差和平均适应度的情况：

## 读后记

:::annotation
读完整篇，我印象最深的是它的取舍。世界模型对一个任务的奖励一无所知，却只凭预测下一帧，就把一个本来很难的连续控制问题变成了 867 个参数的线性回归加进化搜索。这套“大世界模型加小控制器”的分工，后来在基于模型的强化学习里被反复验证，Dreamer 系列用可微的想象轨迹训练策略，Genie 系列则把世界模型当作可交互环境的生成器，思路都能从这里找到影子。

这篇论文也有明显的时代印记。V 用的是 2013 年的 VAE，M 是 LSTM 加高斯混合，实验还停留在两个 Gym 环境上，作者在讨论里自己也承认没有分层规划，容量有限。可它把问题定义得非常干净：智能体该不该拥有一个可以做梦的模型，这个模型该用什么方式被利用，又被什么方式约束。放在今天看，这几个问题依然没有过时。

如果只带走一句话，我会带这一句：世界模型的价值不只是预测得更准，还在于它能不能提供一个足够可靠，又足够有挑战的环境，让智能体在里面练习。温度参数 $\tau$ 那一小节，把这句话变成了实验证据。
:::
